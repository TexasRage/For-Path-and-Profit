Construction allows the [[Expedition]] to establish a [[Basic Homestead]], construct new [[Facilities]], and expand the settlement.

Homestead construction projects have a listed cost that may be paid using Credits, Raw Materials, or a combination of both. Credits and Raw Materials represent equivalent means of acquiring the resources required for construction.

## Construction Projects

A construction project may include a new facility, an expansion of an existing facility, or another improvement to the Homestead.

The requirements for each project are listed in its individual rules.

## Performing Constructions

Characters may contribute to a construction project during the required construction period. Multiple characters may work on the same project simultaneously, allowing the Expedition to complete large projects more quickly.

The specific amount of time characters may contribute and any requirements for contributing are determined by the construction rules for the project.

[[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]] may also contribute to construction when permitted by the project. Their labor represents the routine workforce available at the settlement.

## Completing Constructions

Once the required construction time and resources have been provided, the project is completed and the new facility or improvement becomes operational.

Some facilities may have additional requirements before they can function, such as [[Basic Power Generator]] capacity, [[Sustenance]], [[Shelter]], or other Homestead infrastructure.
