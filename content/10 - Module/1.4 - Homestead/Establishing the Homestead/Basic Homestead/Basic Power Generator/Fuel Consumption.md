The Homestead's [[Basic Power Generator]] consumes [[Fuel Cell|Fuel Cells]] to provide power and life support to the settlement.

Under normal operating conditions, the Generator consumes 1 _[[Fuel Cell]]_ per week.

During [[The Anchor]], the Homestead consumes 1 additional _[[Fuel Cell]]_ per week due to the increased demands of the season.

The [[Basic Power Generator|Generator's]] Fuel consumption does not increase when additional facilities are constructed or activated. Additional Fuel consumption caused by operating the [[Basic Power Generator]] beyond its [[Population Rating]] is handled separately by the [[Population Rating]] rules.

If the [[Basic Power Generator]] does not have sufficient [[Fuel Cell|Fuel Cells]] to meet its required consumption, it cannot maintain normal operation. See [[Basic Power Generator]] for the consequences.
