The [[Basic Power Generator|Generator's]] Population Rating represents the number of [[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]] its life-support systems can comfortably sustain.

If the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead's]] population exceeds the [[Basic Power Generator|Generator's]] Population Rating, the [[Basic Power Generator]] must operate beyond its intended capacity to maintain life support.

For each [[Homestead Colonist|Colonist]] above the [[Basic Power Generator|Generator's]] Population Rating, the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] gains 1 [[Wear]] per day.

The [[Basic Power Generator|Generator's]] Population Rating can be increased through [[Homestead Upgrades]].
