The Water Well provides the [[Basic Homestead]] with a reliable source of fresh water for its [[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]].

The Well supplies the water required for the Homestead's [[Sustenance]] needs and supports the settlement's day-to-day operations.

If the Water Well is out of commission or its water becomes contaminated, the Homestead cannot fulfill its [[Sustenance]] needs until a suitable source of water is restored.
