A character may perform maintenance on the Homestead to reduce accumulated [[Wear]]. Maintenance represents repairing damaged infrastructure, servicing systems, and addressing the effects of prolonged use and environmental exposure.

When a character performs maintenance, they spend **1 [[Quarter-Day]]** and make a **Mechanics check** with a difficulty equal to the Homestead's current [[Wear]]. The character must also expend [[Materials]] as required by the maintenance.

The results are resolved as follows:

- \~~**Success:** Reduce Wear by 1 per net \[success].~~
- \~~**Advantage:** Reduce the Materials cost of the maintenance.~~
- \~~**Triumph:** Perform Preventative Maintenance. Ignore the next Wear the Homestead would gain.~~
- \~~**Failure:** No Wear is removed.~~
- \~~**Threat:** Increase the Materials cost of the maintenance.~~
- \~~**Despair:** Roll on the [[Homestead Failure]] table.~~
