The [[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]] living at a [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] require adequate supplies and infrastructure to remain healthy and productive. Personnel Needs are divided into three basic requirements: [[Consumable|Consumables]], [[Life Support]], and [[Shelter]].

## Personnel Requirements

[[Sustenance]] — Each Colonist requires Consumables.

[[Life Support]] — The [[Basic Power Generator]] provides the life-support systems required to sustain the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead's]] population.

[[Shelter]] — The [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] must provide suitable shelter to protect its [[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]] from the environment.

Each requirement has its own rules for determining whether the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] can adequately support its population.
