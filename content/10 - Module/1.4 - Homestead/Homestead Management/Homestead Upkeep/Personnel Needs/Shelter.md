The [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] must provide suitable shelter for its [[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]], protecting them from [[Mycorris|Mycorris's]] environment and allowing them to safely inhabit the settlement.

Each [[Homestead Colonist|Colonist]] requires 1 Shelter. A facility's Shelter rating determines how many [[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]] it can house.

The Living Quarters included in a [[Basic Homestead]] provides 5 Shelter. Additional Living Quarters may be constructed through [[Construction]] to increase the Homestead's available Shelter.

If the Homestead does not have sufficient Shelter for its population, the affected Colonists are considered unsheltered. For each day the Homestead has insufficient Shelter, it gains 1 [[Wear]].

Shelter provided by the Homestead is permanent and does not require individual Colonists to be assigned to specific living spaces unless a facility's rules state otherwise.
