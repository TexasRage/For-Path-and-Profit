Characters can store equipment and supplies in containers to organize cargo and make more efficient use of available space. Common storage options include _Crates_ and _Containers_.

The storage method determines how much cargo can be accommodated and how easily its contents can be accessed.

## Crates

Crates are smaller, flexible storage options designed to be handled by individuals or small crews. They are useful for field operations, short-distance hauling, and supplies that need to remain readily accessible.

Crates are commonly used to store mission-critical supplies such as _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_, _[[Protein|Proteins]]_, medical equipment, tools, and smaller equipment.

## Containers

Containers are large-scale storage units intended for bulk transport and long-term supply management. Due to their size and weight, containers generally require [[Vehicles]], droids, or other heavy equipment to move.

Containers are useful for transporting large quantities of _[[Material|Materials]]_, _[[Fuel Cell|Fuel Cells]]_, _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_, and other supplies between locations such as [[Basic Homestead|Homesteads]], Camps, and starships.

## Storage Capacity

Each crate and container has an [[Vehicle Encumbrance|Encumbrance Capacity]] that determines how much cargo it can hold.

Cargo stored inside a container uses its normal [[Encumbrance]] unless the container's capacity is modified by [[Packing]].

The GM may determine that unusually large, awkward, or irregularly shaped objects cannot be stored in a particular container regardless of its available capacity.

See [[Packing]] for rules governing Loose and Effective Packing.
