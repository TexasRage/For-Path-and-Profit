A vehicle may carry cargo beyond its listed **Encumbrance Capacity** by tightly packing, stacking, and securing cargo into every available space.

A vehicle may carry up to twice its normal Encumbrance Capacity while _Overloaded_.

While carrying more than its normal Encumbrance Capacity, the vehicle suffers the following effects:

- **Handling** is reduced by 1.
- If operating in a gravity environment, **Speed** is reduced by 1.
- The vehicle gains the [[Cramped]] condition.

If a vehicle's Speed is reduced to 0 as a result of Overloading, it cannot move until enough cargo is removed to bring it within its normal Encumbrance Capacity.

Overloading represents pushing a vehicle beyond its intended cargo capacity. The GM may impose additional complications when an overloaded vehicle travels through hazardous terrain, performs difficult maneuvers, or operates for extended periods.
