Characters may conceal equipment on their person or within suitable environmental cover. Whether an item can be concealed depends on its size, the character's clothing or equipment, and the circumstances.

## Concealing Equipment

Items with an [[Encumbrance]] value of 1 or less can normally be concealed on a character without requiring a check. A casual observer will not notice such items unless specifically searching for them.

If a character is physically searched, the searcher makes an **opposed Perception vs. Stealth check** to detect concealed equipment.

The searcher suffers \[boost] for each point of [[Encumbrance]] above 1 for the item being concealed. If multiple items are concealed, use the highest-[[Encumbrance]] item when determining this penalty.

The GM may modify the difficulty or apply additional \[boost] based on the character's species, clothing, body size, or circumstances of the search.

## Environmental Concealment

The same principles apply when concealing larger objects within environmental cover, cargo, structures, or other suitable surroundings.

The GM determines what can reasonably be concealed in the circumstances and applies \[boost] for each point of [[Encumbrance]] above what the available cover can naturally conceal.
