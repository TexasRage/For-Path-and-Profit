Characters are not normally expected to exceed their [[Encumbrance Threshold]]. When circumstances require a character to lift or move an object that exceeds their normal carrying capacity, they may attempt to do so with an **Athletics check**.

## Lifting Objects

When a character attempts to lift or move an object with an [[Encumbrance]] value greater than their [[Encumbrance Threshold]], make an **Athletics check**.

The difficulty is determined by how far the object's [[Encumbrance]] exceeds the character's threshold:

- Equal to or less than the threshold: **Simple (-)**
- 1 point above the threshold: **Easy (\[difficulty])**
- 2 points above the threshold: **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty])**
- 3 points above the threshold: **Hard (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty])**
- 4 points above the threshold: **Daunting (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty])**
- More than 4 points above the threshold: The object cannot normally be moved by that character alone.

## Assisting

Additional characters may assist with lifting or moving an object.

Each assisting character adds their Brawn to the effective [[Encumbrance Threshold]] for that specific task, representing their combined strength and coordination.

This increased threshold applies only to the lifting or carrying attempt and does not change either character's normal [[Encumbrance Threshold]].
