The Expedition relies on equipment to survive Mycorris, explore its wilderness, and overcome the challenges encountered during its operations. Equipment ranges from weapons and armor to specialized gear, vehicles, mounts, and attachments.

Equipment is acquired using [[Credits]], with availability determined by [[Rarity]]. Most equipment also has an [[Encumbrance]] value, representing the space and weight it occupies when carried.

## Equipment

[[Weapons]] — Weapons used for combat and defense.

[[Armor]] — Protective equipment that reduces the dangers of combat and hazardous environments.

[[Gear]] — Tools, supplies, and other equipment used during exploration and operations.

Equipment has a [[Durability]] that represents its physical condition and determines how it is affected by damage and wear.

## Vehicles and Mounts

[[Vehicles]] — Ground, atmospheric, and space vehicles used to transport characters and cargo.

[[Mounts]] — Creatures trained or equipped to carry characters and supplies.

Vehicles and Mounts have their own Durability rules appropriate to their size and function.

## Attachments

[[Weapon Attachments]] — Modifications that enhance or alter weapons.

[[Armor Attachments]] — Modifications that enhance or alter armor.

[[Vehicle Attachments]] — Modifications that enhance or alter vehicles.

Attachments require available [[Hard Points]] and modify the equipment to which they are installed.

## Equipment Rules

[[Credits]] — The primary currency used to purchase equipment and services.

[[Rarity]] — Determines how difficult equipment is to acquire and affects the difficulty of acquisition checks.

[[Encumbrance]] — Represents the amount of space and carrying capacity required to transport equipment.

[[Durability]] — Represents the condition of equipment and determines when it requires repair or replacement.
