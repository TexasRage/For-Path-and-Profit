Vibrospears are long melee weapons designed for hunting and combat against large or heavily armored targets. LaserHone’s Huntsman variant is the most well-known, helping define the standard design with its silent operation and high precision.

Over two meters in length, a Vibrospear uses ultrasonic vibration technology tuned beyond most hearing ranges, combined with dampening materials to suppress sound. Its monomolecular, leaf-shaped blade can cut through armor, steel, and stone, while its extended reach allows it to parry heavy melee strikes effectively.

Favored by scouts and hunters, Vibrospears are valued for their simplicity and effectiveness in close-range engagements.

A Vibrospear may be thrown as a ranged weapon. When thrown, use Ranged (Light) with Short Range. It gains Limited Ammo 1, but may be recovered and reused.
