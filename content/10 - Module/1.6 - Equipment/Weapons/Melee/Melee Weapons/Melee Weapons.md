Despite the prevalence of blasters and explosives, melee weapons remain common throughout the galaxy. Some warriors favor blades for their reliability and silence, while others rely on axes, polearms, or vibroweapons for the intimidation and stopping power they provide in close combat.

From ceremonial dueling blades to industrial tools repurposed for combat, melee weapons remain effective wherever ammunition, power supplies, or long-range engagements are impractical.
