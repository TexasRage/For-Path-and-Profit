Force Pikes are long polearms used primarily for crowd control and security enforcement. Roughly two meters in length and constructed from durable metal alloys, they combine the lethality of a vibroweapon with adjustable stun technology. The weapon's pronged tip crackles with energy when activated.

Force Pikes feature multiple power settings. In vibration mode, the energized prongs generate a vibrofield capable of cutting through flesh and armor. In stun mode, the weapon delivers a variable-output electrical discharge designed to incapacitate targets or disperse crowds.

When set to stun mode, a Force Pike loses the Pierce 2 quality. Force Pikes require two hands to wield.
