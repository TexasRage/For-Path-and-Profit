Electrostaffs are long polearms designed to keep opponents at a distance while delivering powerful electrical shocks. Constructed from durable, heat-resistant alloys, they are capable of parrying even the most dangerous melee weapons. Each end houses an electromagnetic pulse generator that crackles with energy when activated.

Most Electrostaffs feature adjustable power settings, ranging from low-output shocks intended for crowd control to powerful discharges capable of incapacitating or killing a target. Their exceptional reach and defensive capabilities make them favored by elite guards, temple sentinels, and other highly trained warriors.

Electrostaffs require two hands to wield.

**Models Include**: Baktoid Armor Workshop vz. 890 Thunderer, Arakyd Industries Stryker.
