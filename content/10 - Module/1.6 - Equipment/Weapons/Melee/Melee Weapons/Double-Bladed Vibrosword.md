The Double-Bladed Vibrosword is a rare and highly demanding melee weapon, dangerous to both wielder and opponent. An uncommon variant of the Vibrosword, it features an extended hilt with full-length vibroblades mounted at each end, creating a weapon nearly three meters in total length.

Designed for highly skilled combatants, it favors sweeping strikes, rapid spins, and intimidating flourishes. It excels at parrying melee attacks and counterattacking, and in expert hands can be devastating against multiple opponents in close-quarters combat. The Double-Bladed Vibrosword requires two hands to wield.
