Vibroknives are compact combat blades equipped with miniature vibration generators in the hilt, causing the blade to oscillate at high frequency. This greatly increases their cutting power, allowing them to slice through armor and dense materials with ease. They are commonly used for stealth, close-quarters combat, and covert operations.

**Models Include**: Merr-Sonn Treppus-2 Vibroblade.
