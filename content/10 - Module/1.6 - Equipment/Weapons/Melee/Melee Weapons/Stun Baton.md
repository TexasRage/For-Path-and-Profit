Stun Batons resemble standard police truncheons but incorporate internal power cells and conductive alloy striking surfaces that deliver a concentrated electrical discharge on impact. Commonly used by riot police, security forces, and criminal enforcers, they are designed to subdue rather than kill while remaining highly effective in close-quarters combat.

When used against organic targets, a Stun Baton delivers a powerful electrical discharge capable of overwhelming the nervous system and incapacitating an opponent with a well-placed strike.
