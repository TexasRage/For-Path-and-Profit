Brawl Weapons are designed to fit over or around the hands, enhancing punches, grapples, and other close-quarters strikes. Common examples include brass knuckles, shock gloves, and reinforced gauntlets, making them popular among enforcers, criminals, and anyone expecting a fight at arm's reach.

Unlike most melee weapons, Brawl Weapons are worn rather than carried, allowing the wielder to keep their hands free for climbing, manipulating objects, or grappling while remaining armed.
