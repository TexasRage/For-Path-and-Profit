Garrotes are simple weapons made from cord, wire, cable, or chain, often fitted with handles to provide leverage. Designed to silently restrain or eliminate a target, they are easily concealed and can be improvised from readily available materials. In addition to strangulation, a Garrote can also be used to parry unarmed attacks or assist in grappling.

A character who successfully hits with a Garrote may choose to make the target [[Suffocation|Suffocating]] instead of resolving the attack normally. A target held in this manner may attempt to break free with an **opposed Brawl check**, with the attacker adding one \[boost] for each consecutive round they maintain the hold.
