Brass Knuckles are simple metal striking weapons designed to reinforce the user's fists and deliver more punishing blows. Favored by enforcers, street gangs, and other close-quarters fighters, they also protect the wielder's hands when striking hard surfaces or heavily built opponents.

Compact enough to fit in a pocket or sleeve, Brass Knuckles are easy to conceal. Add \[setback] to any **Perception check** made to locate Brass Knuckles hidden on a character's person.
