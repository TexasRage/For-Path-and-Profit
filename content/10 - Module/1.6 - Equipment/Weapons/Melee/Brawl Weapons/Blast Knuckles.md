Blast Knuckles are armored gauntlets fitted with miniature blaster emitters built directly into the striking surface. When the wearer lands a punch, impact-sensitive sensors trigger the emitter, delivering a powerful point-blank energy blast alongside the strike. Their compact design limits the emitter's range to only a few centimeters, making them effective only in hand-to-hand combat.

The intense heat and recoil generated by each discharge place considerable strain on the wearer. Each successful hit with Blast Knuckles inflicts 1 Strain on the wielder, in addition to any Strain generated by the check. Blast Knuckles cannot inflict Strain damage to a target.
