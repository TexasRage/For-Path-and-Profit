Incendiary micro-rockets are loaded with volatile chemical compounds that ignite on impact, producing intense, sustained flames in a concentrated area. They effectively replicate the output of a flame projector in a compact, single-use warhead.

Often used to flush targets from cover or sealed positions, incendiary rockets are feared for their ability to rapidly escalate combat environments. However, their use carries significant risk, as fires can spread uncontrollably and may destroy the intended bounty or surrounding environment.
