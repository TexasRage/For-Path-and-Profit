While proton missiles are the most common ordnance used in shoulder-launched systems, a wide variety of specialized warheads have been developed to fulfill different tactical roles. Missile Tubes may be loaded with any compatible Missile type.

When a Missile is loaded, it replaces the launcher's Damage, Critical rating, and item qualities with those listed in the Missile's profile. This includes qualities such as Blast, Guided, Pierce, Breach, Concussive, Disorient, Ensnare, and Burn.
