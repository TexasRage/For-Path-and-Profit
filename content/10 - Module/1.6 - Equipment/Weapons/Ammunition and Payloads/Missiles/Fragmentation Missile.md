Fragmentation missiles are designed for anti-personnel use rather than vehicle destruction. Upon detonation, they disperse high-velocity shrapnel over a wide area, inflicting severe injuries through laceration and blunt trauma.

They are highly effective against unarmored and lightly armored targets, and remain dangerous even against opponents in heavier protection.

**Models Include**: Merr-Sonn C-88, SoroSuub Mk XVIII Anti-Personnel Missile.
