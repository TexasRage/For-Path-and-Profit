Concussion missiles produce a powerful shockwave on impact, designed to incapacitate or injure targets through force rather than fragmentation. They are used in a variety of roles, from non-lethal suppression to anti-armor engagements, depending on the payload configuration.

Concussion missiles possess the Disorient quality, affecting all characters within Short range of the target even if they are not damaged. They also possess the Concussive quality, affecting all characters engaged with the target regardless of whether they suffer damage.

**Models Include**: Golan Arms Mk.10.
