Plasma missiles convert a high-energy gas charge into a volatile plasma state upon activation. Functionally similar to a blaster, the resulting detonation produces an intense energy burst comparable to a concentrated blaster impact, significantly magnified in destructive force.

Due to the instability of the plasma warhead, the missile detonates after a short flight time regardless of impact, limiting its effective range compared to other missile types.
