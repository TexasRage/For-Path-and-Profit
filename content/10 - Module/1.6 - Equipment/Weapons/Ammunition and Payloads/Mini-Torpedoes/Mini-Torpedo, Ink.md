Ink mini-torpedoes release a reservoir of dense black fluid that rapidly disperses into a wide obscuring cloud upon detonation. The cloud blocks visibility and line of sight, making it ideal for covering retreats or disrupting enemy formations.

Any character caught in the cloud may become Disoriented. The cloud persists for up to four rounds, though it may linger longer in still or confined water.
