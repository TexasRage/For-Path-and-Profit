Concussion grenades rely on powerful shock waves rather than fragmentation to incapacitate or injure their targets. Effective against enemies behind cover or wearing heavy armor, they are commonly used to weaken defenders before an assault. While their lethal radius is relatively small, the concussive blast can leave nearby targets disoriented, nauseated, and struggling to recover.

If a concussion grenade's Disorient quality is activated, it affects all characters within Short range of the target.

**Models Include**: SoroSuub LXR-6 Concussion Grenade, Mier-Lang V-59 Concussion Grenade.
