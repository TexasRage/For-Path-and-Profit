Thermal detonators are among the most powerful handheld explosives ever developed. Using a volatile baradium core, they produce a devastating explosion capable of destroying structures and annihilating anything caught within the blast. Their destructive power makes them highly restricted or outright illegal in most regions.

On a \[Despair], the detonator explodes prematurely. The wielder suffers the weapon's full damage unless they perform an additional maneuver to move clear, in which case they suffer only the Blast damage.

Unlike most explosives, a thermal detonator's Blast affects all targets within Short range of the original target.

**Models Include**: Merr-Sonn Class-A Thermal Detonator.
