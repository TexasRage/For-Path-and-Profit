Incendiary grenades are highly restricted anti-personnel weapons that rely on intense heat and burning chemicals rather than fragmentation. Most are filled with a volatile, adhesive gel that ignites on contact with air or when triggered by a small internal charge.

Upon detonation, the burning gel splashes across targets and surfaces, clinging to armor, equipment, and structures while continuing to burn. They are effective at flushing enemies from cover and igniting confined spaces, but are widely banned due to their indiscriminate and persistent nature.

**Models Include**: Wynz-Tek Mk IV Firestorm, Merr-Sonn D-24 Inferno Grenade.
