Reusable, non-lethal containment device used by law enforcement and security forces to restrain fleeing suspects and control riots. When triggered, it releases a rapidly expanding semiliquid foam that hardens on contact with air into a ferrocrete-like mass before gradually becoming brittle over time.

The grenade’s Ensnare quality triggers automatically on any targets hit by the attack, without requiring \[Advantage].

Targets affected by a glop grenade must make an **Easy (\[difficulty]) Coordination check** to prevent foam from obstructing their airways. On failure, the target suffers the effects of suffocation.

Recovered grenades can be refilled using pressurized hypoinjectors, which transfer foam from sealed canisters into the device without atmospheric exposure. This requires an **Easy (\[difficulty]) Ranged (Light) or Mechanics check**. On failure, the foam is ruined; on any \[Threat], the user risks being caught in the expanding material and must make an Easy Coordination check to avoid being restrained as normal.

A standard purchase includes a hypoinjector and five refill canisters. Additional canisters may be purchased separately.

**Models Include**: Merr-Sonn’s G-20 Glop Grenade
