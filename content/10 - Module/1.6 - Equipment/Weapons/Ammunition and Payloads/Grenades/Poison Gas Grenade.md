Upon impact, the grenade activates internal emitters that release an aerosolized cloud of neurotoxin, anesthetic, neuroparalytic agent, or other poison at the GM’s discretion.

A poison gas grenade can be loaded with a single dose of poison (such as a neurotoxin, anesthetic, or neuroparalytic agent). When the grenade successfully hits a target—whether or not it inflicts damage—the target is also exposed to the loaded poison and suffers its effects as normal.

Poison gas lingers in the affected area for two rounds in open or windy conditions. In enclosed or poorly ventilated spaces such as rooms or ship corridors, it lingers for five rounds instead. Poison gas has no effect on droids or other non-breathing entities, and characters equipped with sealed environmental protection (such as breath masks, respirators, or rebreathers) are typically unaffected at the GM’s discretion.
