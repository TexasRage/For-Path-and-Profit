Sonic grenades use high-intensity acoustic pulses rather than explosives or chemical agents to deliver their effects. Upon detonation, they emit a focused shockwave of sound capable of shattering glass, cracking masonry, and damaging light structures and equipment.

Against living targets, the concussive sound causes severe pain, disorientation, and potential long-term hearing damage, with effects worsening at closer ranges. Sonic grenades are especially effective for clearing enclosed spaces and disrupting fortified positions without relying on fragmentation or fire.

**Models Include**: Krupx Munitions Groundshaker 7, Imperial Munitions M880 Sonic Grenade
