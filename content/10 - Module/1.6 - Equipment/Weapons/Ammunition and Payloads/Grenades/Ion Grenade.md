Ion grenades release a burst of ionized energy designed to overload and disable electronic systems within their blast radius. Commonly used against droids, vehicles, and heavily augmented targets, they are favored by bounty hunters, saboteurs, and security specialists.

The ion discharge can temporarily disable starship systems such as weapons, hyperdrives, and life support, making these grenades particularly effective during boarding actions or sabotage operations. As a result, most civilian vessels restrict or prohibit their carriage, and security scanners are often calibrated to detect ion-based devices.
