Stun grenades incapacitate targets using a combination of intense concussive force, sonic waves, and dazzling sensory effects rather than lethal fragmentation. They are commonly employed when taking targets alive or clearing confined spaces with minimal lasting injury.

Like frag grenades, stun grenades may be configured to detonate on impact or with a timed fuse of up to three rounds after activation. Most models do not feature pressure-release ("dead man's") fuses.

**Models Include**: Merr-Sonn C-10 Stun Grenade.
