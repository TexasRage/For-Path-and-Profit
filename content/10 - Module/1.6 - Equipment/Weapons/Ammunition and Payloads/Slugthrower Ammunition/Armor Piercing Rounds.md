Armor Piercing Rounds use hardened projectiles designed to defeat protective plating and other forms of personal armor. The projectile's core is typically constructed from a dense material capable of penetrating armor without deforming on impact, allowing the round to reach targets protected by conventional armor.

When loaded, Armor Piercing Rounds add the [[Pierce]] quality to the weapon's profile.
