Breach Shells are specialized scattergun ammunition designed to deliver a concentrated explosive charge against barriers and obstacles. The payload can shatter doors, thin walls, and other light structures, allowing the wielder to create a small opening where one did not previously exist.

When loaded, Breach Shells replace the weapon's [[Sweep]] quality with [[Blast]] and remove the weapon's [[Vicious]] quality. A Breach Shell may be used to blow a small opening through a thin wall or similar obstacle, at the GM's discretion.
