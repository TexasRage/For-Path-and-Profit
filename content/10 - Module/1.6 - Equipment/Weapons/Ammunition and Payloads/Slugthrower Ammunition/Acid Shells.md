Acid Shells contain a highly corrosive chemical payload designed to rupture on impact and spread across the target. The acid rapidly degrades exposed materials, making these shells effective against armor, equipment, and other durable obstacles.

When loaded, Acid Shells add the [[Corrosive]] quality to the weapon's profile and remove the weapon's [[Knockdown]] quality.
