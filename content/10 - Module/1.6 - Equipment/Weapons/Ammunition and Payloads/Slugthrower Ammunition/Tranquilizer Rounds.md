Tranquilizer Rounds deliver a powerful sedative through a specialized projectile designed to incapacitate living targets without causing significant physical trauma. The sedative acts rapidly, making these rounds useful for hunters, wildlife specialists, law enforcement, and others who need to capture targets alive.

When a target is hit by Tranquilizer Rounds, it must immediately make an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Resilience check**. On a failure, the target suffers 5 Strain, plus 2 additional Strain per \[threat] generated on the check.

The GM may spend a \[despair] generated on the check to cause the target to repeat the check at the beginning of the following round, with the difficulty upgraded once.
