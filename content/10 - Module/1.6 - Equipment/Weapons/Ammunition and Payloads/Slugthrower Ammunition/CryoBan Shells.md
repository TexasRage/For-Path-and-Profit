Cryoban Shells contain a specialized cryogenic compound that rapidly absorbs heat when the shell strikes its target. The sudden thermal shock can impair movement and disrupt sensitive equipment, making cryoban ammunition useful for controlling targets and operating in extreme environments.

When loaded, Cryoban Shells add the [[Brittle]] quality to the weapon's profile and remove the weapon's [[Knockdown]] quality.
