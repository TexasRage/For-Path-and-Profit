Flechette Rounds contain small, fin-stabilized projectiles equipped with guidance systems that allow them to adjust their trajectory after being fired. While individually less powerful than conventional rounds, their ability to correct their flight makes them particularly effective against fast-moving or evasive targets.

When loaded, Flechette Rounds add the [[Guided]] quality to the weapon's profile.
