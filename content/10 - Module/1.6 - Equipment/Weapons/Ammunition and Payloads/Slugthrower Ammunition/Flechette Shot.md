Flechette Shot replaces a conventional scattergun load with a dense payload of small, fin-stabilized projectiles. The projectiles are designed to maintain a stable trajectory and adjust their flight toward the target, extending the scattergun's effective range while sacrificing some of the raw impact of a conventional shell.

When loaded, Flechette Shot increases the weapon's Range by 1, adds the [[Guided]] quality, and removes the [[Knockdown]] quality.
