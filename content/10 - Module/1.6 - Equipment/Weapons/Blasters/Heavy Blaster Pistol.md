Heavy Blaster Pistols are favored by spacers who want their firepower to be immediately obvious. Built for intimidation as much as practicality, they sacrifice concealability and range in exchange for significant stopping power, capable of punching through most personal armor and inflicting severe damage at close range.

They bridge the gap between Blaster Pistols and Blaster Rifles, sacrificing portability for increased stopping power. Heavier and bulkier than standard sidearms, they remain more portable than long arms while delivering a more devastating shot than lighter pistols.

**Models Include**: BlasTech DT-57 "Annihilator", BlasTech DL-44, SoroSuub SSK-7, Merr-Sonn Model 434.
