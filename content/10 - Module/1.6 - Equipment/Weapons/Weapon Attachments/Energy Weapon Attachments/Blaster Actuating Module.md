Actuating Modules replace or enhance a blaster's internal firing mechanisms, reducing the delay between trigger pull and bolt discharge. Popular among mercenaries, bounty hunters, gunslingers, and others who rely on split-second reactions, these modifications improve a weapon's responsiveness and help ensure the first shot is the fastest.

This attachment can only be applied to Ranged (Light) blaster pistols.

**Base Modifiers**: Increases weapon damage by 1. Adds \[setback] to all checks made when using this weapon.

**Modification Options**: 2x Damage +1 Mods, 2x Item Quality ([[Pierce]] +1) Mods.

**Models Include**: Dolmax Heavy Blaster Actuator, Arakyd 97R Enhanced Blaster Actuating Module, SoroSuub X440 "Hammer" Blaster Actuating Module.
