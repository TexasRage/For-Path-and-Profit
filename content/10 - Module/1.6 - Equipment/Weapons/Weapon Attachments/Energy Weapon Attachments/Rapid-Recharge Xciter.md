Specially modified gas conversion enablers (XCiters) can accelerate a blaster's firing cycle beyond standard factory specifications. By improving the speed at which energy is converted and discharged, these upgraded XCiters allow blaster pistols and rifles to achieve significantly higher rates of fire, approaching the output of dedicated repeating blasters.

This attachment can be applied to any blaster.

**Base Modifiers**: The weapon gains the [[Auto-Fire]] quality and the [[Inaccurate]] 3 quality.

**Modification Options**: 2x Decrease [[Inaccurate]] quality by 1 Mods.

**Models Include**: BlasTech XCelerator, SoroSuub M-3 Cycling Module, various custom models.
