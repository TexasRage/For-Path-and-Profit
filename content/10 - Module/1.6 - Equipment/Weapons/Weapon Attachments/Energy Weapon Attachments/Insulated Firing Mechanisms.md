Extreme heat, freezing temperatures, moisture, and electrical interference can all compromise a blaster's delicate firing systems. Insulated firing mechanisms reinforce and shield these critical components, protecting the weapon from environmental hazards and ensuring reliable operation even under the harshest conditions.

This attachment can be applied to any personal-scale blaster weapon.

**Base Modifiers**: Increase the \[advantage] or \[threat] result cost of any effect that damages the weapon (such as the [[Sunder]] quality) by one \[advantage] or \[threat] result (as applicable). The weapon gains the [[Prepare]] 1 quality, or increases its existing [[Prepare]] quality by 1. Repairs to the weapon when damaged cost half as much.

**Modification Options**: 1x Reduce [[Prepare]] quality by 1 Mod.

**Models Include**: Czerka Parts Replacement Kit NX3-4r, BlasTech "My First Blaster" Safety Kit.
