There are many ways to increase the energy output of blaster weaponry, but few are as simple or affordable as a Tibanna-jack. By modifying the weapon's gas conversion system to accept a higher-energy charge from Tibanna gas, this attachment increases the power delivered by each blaster bolt. While effective, the increased output places additional stress on the weapon's components and may reduce overall efficiency.

This attachment can be applied to any blaster.

**Base Modifiers**: May upgrade the difficulty of an attack with this weapon once to reduce the weapon's critical rating by 1, to a minimum of 1. The GM may spend a \[despair] result to damage the weapon one step.

**Modification Options**: 1x Upgrade the difficulty of an attack one additional time to reduce the critical rating by 1 (to a minimum of 1) Mod, 2x Damage +1 Mods.
