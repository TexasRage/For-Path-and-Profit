Advances in ion technology have led to the development of specialized focusing coils capable of partially ionizing a standard blaster's plasma discharge. While less effective than dedicated ion weapons, these modified coils allow conventional blasters to disrupt droids, vehicles, and electronic systems without sacrificing their primary combat role.

This attachment can be applied to any personal-scale blaster weapon.

**Base Modifiers**: Increase weapon damage by 2 versus droids. Opponents may spend \[threat]\[threat] or \[despair] on combat checks using the weapon to damage the weapon one step.

**Modification Options**: 2x Damage +1 versus droids Mods, 1x Decrease weapon's critical rating versus droids by 1 to a minimum of 1 Mod, 1x Increase \[threat] result required to backfire by 1 Mod.

**Models Include**: BlasTech "Sparker" Ionization Coils.
