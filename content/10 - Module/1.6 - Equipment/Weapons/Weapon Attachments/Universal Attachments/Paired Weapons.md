Paired Weapon modifications adjust a weapon's balance, grip, and handling characteristics to improve its performance when wielded alongside another weapon. Favored by duelists, mercenaries, and warriors across the galaxy, these modifications allow skilled combatants to maintain control while fighting with two weapons at once.

This attachment can only be applied to a one-handed weapon.

**Base Modifiers**: Must be applied to two weapons at the same time. Reduces required to hit with the secondary weapon when two-weapon fighting with this pair of weapons by 1.

**Modification Options:** None.
