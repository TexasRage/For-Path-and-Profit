Some upgrades are not tied to a specific weapon type, but instead improve the connection between the wielder and the weapon itself. These modifications focus on handling, control, concealment, stabilization, or user integration rather than the weapon's firing or striking mechanism.

Universal Attachments may be installed on either ranged or melee weapons, provided the weapon meets any additional restrictions listed in the individual attachment entry.
