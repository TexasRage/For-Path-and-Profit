A Magnetic Weapon Tether is a simple modification consisting of a compact retention device integrated into a weapon. The tether allows a wielder to secure their weapon to their person, preventing accidental loss while climbing, operating in hazardous environments, or fighting in zero gravity.

This attachment can be applied to any one-handed weapon that could logically benefit from a weapon tether.

**Base Modifiers**: During their turn, the wielder may recover the weapon as an Incidental if they are Engaged with it.

**Modification Options**: 1x Innate Talent ([[Quick Draw]]) Mod.

**Models Include:** Czerka "At Hand" Mag-Tether.
