Componentization allows a weapon to be separated into multiple inconspicuous components for easier transport and concealment. Designed for hunters, scouts, and operators working in restricted environments, these modifications allow weapons to be carried discreetly while maintaining their normal functionality once reassembled. Assembling or disassembling a weapon with this modification requires 2 Maneuvers. While disassembled, the weapon cannot be used.

This attachment can be applied to any weapon with an Encumbrance of 2 or less.

**Base Modifiers**: Allows a weapon to be broken into pieces and disguised as other items. A character must make a **Formidable (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Mechanics or Skulduggery check** to deduce the items are weapon parts.

**Modification Options**: None.
