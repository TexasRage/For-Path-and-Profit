Gyrostabilizers, sometimes referred to as repulsorlift harnesses, use compact repulsorlift generators and stabilization systems to offset a weapon's weight. By reducing the strain of supporting and aiming heavy weapons, these systems allow operators to wield otherwise cumbersome armaments with greater control and endurance.

Gyrostabilizers can be applied to any Ranged (Heavy) or Gunnery weapon.

**Base Modifiers**: Decreases [[Cumbersome]] quality by 1.

**Modification Options**: 3x Decrease [[Cumbersome]] quality by 1 Mods, 1x Innate Talent ([[Barrage]]) Mod.

**Models Include**: TaggeCo. Type 2 Repulsorlift Harness, BlasTech Mk. IV Gyrostabilization Unit.
