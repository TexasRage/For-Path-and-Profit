Operating in darkness is rarely ideal, but an Integrated Illuminator can provide a significant advantage when visibility is limited. Mounted directly beneath or alongside a weapon's barrel, these compact spotlights illuminate targets and surroundings, allowing operators to engage threats more effectively in low-light environments.

This attachment can be applied to any ranged weapon that could logically benefit from the addition of a spotlight.

**Base Modifiers**: Removes up to \[setback]\[setback] due to [[Brightness|Darkness]] on any checks to use this weapon against targets within short range.

**Modification Options**: 1x Increase spotlight range to medium Mod, 2x Item Quality ([[Disorient]] +1) Mods.

**Models Include**: CSA "Nightfighter" Weapon Lamp, Loronar L09 Lamp.
