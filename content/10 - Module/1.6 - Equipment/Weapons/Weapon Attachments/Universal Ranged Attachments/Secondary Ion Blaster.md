Among the many weapons designed to be mounted beneath rifles and other long arms, Secondary Ion Blasters are a popular choice for operators who need additional firepower against droids, vehicles, and electronic systems. These compact ion weapons provide a specialized option without requiring the wielder to carry a separate sidearm.

Secondary Ion Blasters can be applied to any Ranged (Heavy) weapon.

**Base Modifiers**: Enables the weapon to fire using the following profile at the user's choice: (Ranged \[Heavy]; Damage 10; Critical 5; Range \[Short]; [[Disorient]] 5, [[Ion]]). Adds [[Cumbersome]] (+1) to weapon.

**Modification Options**: 2x Increase ion blaster's damage by +1 Mods.

**Models Include**: SoroSuub Droid Defender, BlasTech Model 9 Under-Barrel Ion Blaster.
