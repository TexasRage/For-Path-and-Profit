Often used by mercenaries, scouts, and special operations forces, Under-Barrel Grapnel Launchers provide a rapid means of scaling sheer surfaces during combat operations or covert infiltrations. These compact systems fire a secured grapnel line that allows operators to overcome vertical obstacles without carrying separate climbing equipment.

Under-Barrel Grapnel Launchers can be applied to any Ranged (Light) or Ranged (Heavy) weapon that could logically support a Grapnel Launcher.

**Base Modifiers**: As an action, a character may make an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Ranged (Light) check** to secure the grappling hook to an object within medium range. On success, as an action, they may reel in the cord, pulling themselves to the object (or, if the object is unsecured and lighter than they are, pulling it to them). A character may use the grappling hook to pull another character aloft with them; if they do, they must make an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Athletics check** to avoid losing their grip on their partner or the gun. Increases weapon's encumbrance by 2.

**Modification Options**: None.

**Models Include**: Merr-Sonn KR-2 Auxiliary Grapnel, TaggeCo "Skysailer" Grapnel Launcher.
