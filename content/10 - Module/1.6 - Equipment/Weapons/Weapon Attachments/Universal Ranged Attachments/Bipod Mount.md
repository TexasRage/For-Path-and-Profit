Bipods are simple stabilizing devices consisting of folding or collapsible legs mounted beneath the barrel of a long arm. Used to support heavy or unwieldy weapons, bipods improve stability and accuracy when firing from a stationary position.

This attachment can only be applied to rifles, carbines, and Light Repeating Blasters.

**Base Modifiers**: Decrease the weapon's [[Cumbersome]] rating by 2 when firing from a prone or crouched position (or can otherwise brace the bipod on something). Takes one preparation Maneuver to set up.

**Modification Options:** None.

**Models Include:** Galactic Arms XA Classic Bipod, Czerka AA20 Marksman Bipod.
