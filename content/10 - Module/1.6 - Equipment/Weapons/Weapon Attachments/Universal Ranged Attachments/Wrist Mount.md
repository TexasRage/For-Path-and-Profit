Originally developed to allow species without opposable thumbs or conventional hand structures to operate pistols, Wrist Mounts have become popular among bounty hunters, scouts, and specialists who need immediate access to a weapon while keeping their hands free. These compact systems secure a pistol-sized weapon to the forearm or wrist and allow it to be deployed quickly when needed.

Wrist Mounts can be applied to any Ranged (Light) weapon.

**Base Modifiers**: The weapon can be worn on the user's wrist, allowing them to use their hands without interference.

**Modification Options**: None.

**Models Include**: Corellian Arms Model 2 Wrist Mount, BlasTech WM-Alpha Pistol Brace.
