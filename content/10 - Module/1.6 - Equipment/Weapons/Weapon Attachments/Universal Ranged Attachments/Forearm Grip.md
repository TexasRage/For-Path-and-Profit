A Forearm Grip is a vertical handgrip mounted beneath the barrel of a rifle-sized weapon. Popular among mercenaries, soldiers, and security forces across the galaxy, Forearm Grips provide additional control and stability when maneuvering with a weapon, especially during rapid movement or close-quarters engagements.

This attachment can only be applied to rifle- and carbine-sized weapons (but not heavy rifles).

**Base Modifiers**: Decreases the additional difficulty of making **Ranged (Heavy) checks** with this weapon while engaged to an additional difficulty die (rather than two difficulty dice).

**Modification Options**: 1x Innate Talent ([[Point Blank]] rank +1) Mod, 1x Item Quality ([[Accurate]] +1) Mod.

**Models Include**: Dolmax 14A Detachable Pistol Grip, Galactic Arms Model 9 Forearm Grip, Merr-Sonn AG44S Ergonomic Tactical Foregrip.
