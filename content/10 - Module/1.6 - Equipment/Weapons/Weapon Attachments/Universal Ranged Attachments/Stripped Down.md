By removing redundant components, excess materials, and unnecessary features, a weapon can be significantly reduced in weight and profile. While stripped-down weapons are easier to carry and conceal, the removal of protective components and refinements can make them less durable and less comfortable to operate.

This attachment can be applied to any Ranged (Light) or Ranged (Heavy) weapon.

**Base Modifiers**: Decrease the weapon's encumbrance by 1 (to a minimum of 1). Add \[setback] to a character's **Perception checks** to find the weapon when it is hidden.

**Modification Options**: 2 Decrease encumbrance by 1 (to a minimum of 1) Mods.
