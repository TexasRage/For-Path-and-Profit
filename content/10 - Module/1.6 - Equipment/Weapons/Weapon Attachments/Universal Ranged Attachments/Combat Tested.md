Over the course of numerous battles, combatants often develop a personal connection with their weapons. These customizations can range from mission markings and victory tallies carved into the weapon's frame to trophies, decorations, or battlefield mementos attached to the weapon. Such modifications reflect the history and identity of both the wielder and the weapon.

This attachment can be applied to any weapon that the owner has used extensively in combat, as determined by the GM.

**Base Modifiers**: Owner adds \[boost] to **Discipline checks**.
