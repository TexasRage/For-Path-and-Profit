A common modification to holdout blasters and other compact pistols is the removal or alteration of the weapon's front sight. By reducing external features and eliminating identifying marks, Filed Sights make a weapon easier to conceal and less recognizable during casual inspection. While this modification can make precise aiming more difficult, it is favored by those who prioritize discretion over accuracy.

This attachment can only be applied to pistol-sized weapons. Performing this modification personally requires an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Mechanics check**. The listed price is used when paying someone else to perform the work.

Base Modifiers: Innate Talent ([[Quick Draw]]). Increases difficulty of combat checks to hit targets at ranges beyond short range by 1.
Modification Options: 1x Decreases the difficulty of checks to conceal the weapon by 1 Mod.
