Damaged weapons can be restored through maintenance and repair. Repairing a weapon requires the appropriate skill, a [[Tool Kit]], and time.

## Repairing Weapons

A character may repair a weapon as part of the [[Modify Equipment]] action.

Weapons may be repaired using the Mechanics skill or the skill normally used to wield the weapon. Weapons that rely on programming, circuitry, or electronics, such as laser turrets or missile launchers, may also be repaired using Computers.

The character makes a repair check with a difficulty determined by the weapon's current [[Weapon Damage|Damage]] level.

Each difficulty die of the repair check represents approximately 2 hours of work. Attempting repairs without the appropriate tools is not possible; the character must have a [[Tool Kit]].

A character may attempt to complete the repair in less time by upgrading the difficulty once.

## Repair Results

On a successful check, the weapon's Damage is reduced by one step.

If \[despair] is generated on the repair check, the weapon instead suffers one additional step of Damage.

## Repair Cost

Repair costs are based on the severity of the weapon's Damage.

Characters repairing their own equipment reduce the repair cost by 10% per \[advantage] generated on the repair check, while professional repair services generally charge the full repair cost.
