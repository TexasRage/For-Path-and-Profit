An ideal weapon for subduing most sentient beings, the electronet launcher uses the same principles as a conventional net launcher while eliminating many of the problems associated with tangled physical restraints. Instead of a traditional mesh, the launcher fires a web of energized filaments that entangle a target and deliver a disabling electrical charge. This makes the electronet launcher a popular tool for security forces, bounty hunters, and other capture specialists.
