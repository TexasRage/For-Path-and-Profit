When a target must be captured alive, bolas provide a simple but effective solution. Consisting of weighted cords designed to wrap around a target's limbs, bolas allow hunters, law enforcement personnel, and other professionals to restrain targets without causing serious injury.

Although stun weapons are generally easier to use, bolas offer several advantages. Their low-tech construction makes them difficult to detect with most weapons scanners, and they are less likely to cause lasting harm to a captive. They are particularly useful when energy weapons are impractical, restricted, or undesirable.

Bolas rely on the Ensnare quality to incapacitate a target, requiring skill and precision to use effectively.

A user may spend \[Triumph] to thoroughly entangle a target. While restrained in this manner, the target cannot perform Maneuvers, and their only action each turn is a **Formidable (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Athletics check** to free themselves.
