The preferred weapon of the Nightsisters of Dathomir, the energy bow is a carbon-alloy bow fitted with a plasma coupler string, a technology similar to the energy binders used to link podracing engines. The energized string allows the bow to propel specialized energy bolts rather than traditional arrows, combining the familiar form and handling of a primitive bow with more advanced technology.
