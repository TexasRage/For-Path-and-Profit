When a target must be captured alive, nets provide a simple and effective means of restraint. Designed to entangle opponents at short range, nets are commonly used by hunters, law enforcement personnel, bounty hunters, and other capture specialists.

Although stun weapons are generally easier to use, nets offer several advantages. Their low-tech construction makes them difficult to detect with most weapons scanners, and they are unlikely to cause lasting harm to a captive. Their simplicity also makes them useful in environments where energy weapons are impractical or restricted.

Nets rely on the Ensnare quality to restrain a target, requiring skill and precision to deploy effectively. Unlike more sophisticated restraint systems, a net can be recovered and reused after being removed from a target.

A user may spend \[Triumph] to thoroughly entangle a target. While restrained in this manner, the target cannot perform Maneuvers, and their only action each turn is a **Formidable (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Athletics check** to free themselves.
