Disruptor Pistols are illegal throughout most civilized systems due to their extreme lethality. They fire exceptionally high-energy particle beams capable of disintegrating limbs or large sections of a target's body on impact.

Based on conventional blaster technology, Disruptor Pistols incorporate oversized XCiters and specialized actuating modules capable of processing greatly increased volumes of blaster gas. The resulting beam passes through a reinforced galven focusing system that super-compresses and destabilizes the discharge into a highly destructive particle stream.

Their unstable output gives Disruptor Pistols relatively poor range, but their compact size makes them particularly dangerous in close-quarters engagements.

Whenever a target suffers a Critical Injury from a Disruptor Pistol, the result is treated as Crippled unless the rolled result would inflict a more severe injury.

Disruptor Pistols cannot benefit from attachments that increase Range or grant additional firing modes.

**Models Include**: Tenloss DX-2 Disruptor Pistol.
