Blaster Pistols are the most common sidearms carried throughout the galaxy. They offer a reliable balance of range, stopping power, and portability, making them well-suited for everyday carry in dangerous environments. Capable of penetrating plastoid armor at close range, they remain light enough to be worn comfortably for extended periods.

Scoundrels and gunslingers alike often favor the familiar silhouette of a well-worn blaster on the hip, both for practicality and appearance. These weapons are also common among shipboard crews, where their firepower is sufficient for close-quarters defense without posing an excessive risk of breaching a vessel’s hull.

**Models Include**: BlasTech DH-17, BlasTech SE-14c, Merr-Sonn Model 434, BlasTech SE-14.
