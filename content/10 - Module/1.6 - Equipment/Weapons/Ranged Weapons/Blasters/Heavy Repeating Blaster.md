Heavy Repeating Blasters are devastating support weapons designed to destroy light machinery, penetrate armored targets, and suppress enemies in cover. They are capable of sustained, high-volume fire that can overwhelm fortified positions and deny movement across open ground.

Due to their size and power requirements, Heavy Repeating Blasters are typically operated as crew-served weapons. The listed price includes both a **power pack** and a **tripod**, allowing the weapon to be deployed as a stable firing platform without requiring additional equipment. An auxiliary operator may still be useful for managing power supply systems, cooling, or targeting support equipment.

When deployed in the field, these weapons are intended for static or semi-static engagements rather than mobile combat. Attempting to fire without proper stabilization is extremely difficult and generally reserved for only the strongest or most specialized operators.

Setting up a Heavy Repeating Blaster requires a gun crew and **3 Actions** to fully deploy the tripod, connect the power pack, and prepare the weapon for firing. The weapon cannot be fired effectively until all 3 Actions have been completed.

**Models Include**: BlasTech EWHB-12, BlasTech E-Web, Merr-Sonn Mk. II.
