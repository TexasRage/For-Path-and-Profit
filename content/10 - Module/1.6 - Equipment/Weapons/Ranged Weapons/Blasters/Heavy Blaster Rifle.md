Hunters, marksmen, and specialists often favor Heavy Blaster Rifles equipped with enhanced accelerant gas systems, advanced XCiters, enlarged focusing crystals, or a combination of these modifications. Designed for maximum impact, these weapons deliver exceptionally powerful bolts capable of penetrating heavy armor and engaging large or hardened targets at long range while still allowing controlled bursts of fire when needed.

Though unwieldy compared to standard rifles, Heavy Blaster Rifles excel in long-range engagements against both armored and unarmored targets. Their overwhelming stopping power often serves as a deterrent in its own right, sending a clear message to any who witness their use.

**Models Include**: BlasTech DLT-19, BlasTech RT-97C Heavy Blaster Rifle.
