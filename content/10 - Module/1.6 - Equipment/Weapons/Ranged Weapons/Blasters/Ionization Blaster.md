Ionization Blasters are specialized weapons designed to disable droids, vehicles, and electronic systems rather than inflict conventional physical damage. By projecting a concentrated ionized discharge, they overload circuitry, disrupt power distribution, and temporarily shut down sensitive components.

While largely ineffective against living creatures, Ionization Blasters are highly valued by bounty hunters, law enforcement agencies, security forces, and mechanics who need to capture droids or disable machinery without causing permanent damage. Their disruptive energy can render even heavily armored droids temporarily inert, making them an indispensable tool when electronic targets are expected.

Against organic targets, Ionization Blasters inflict only Strain damage, representing the painful electrical shock produced by the ionized discharge.

**Models Include**: Merr-Sonn DEMP 2 Ion Carbine, scavenged Jawa scrap ion blaster.
