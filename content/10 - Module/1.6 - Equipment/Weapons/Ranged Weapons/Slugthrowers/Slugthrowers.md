Considered the weapons of "backwater" worlds, _Slugthrowers_ are nonetheless simple, reliable, and easily manufactured or repaired in regions with limited infrastructure or technology.

Their basic principle is straightforward: a propellant charge is sealed within a cartridge and, when ignited, propels a solid projectile—typically metal—through the weapon's barrel. The size of the projectile and the force of the propellant determine its stopping power, while barrel length and rifling, which imparts spin to the projectile, directly affect range and accuracy.

Despite widespread bias against these "antiquated" weapons, _Slugthrowers_ remain favored by snipers, assassins, and hunters. While they are louder than blaster weapons and often produce visible muzzle discharge, they lack the bright signature of energy fire, making them harder to trace in darkness or across long distances.
