Not all weapons are intended for direct engagements. _Launchers_ and _Projectors_ deliver explosives, chemicals, incendiaries, and other specialized payloads capable of affecting large areas, breaching defenses, or controlling the battlefield.

While often cumbersome and costly to operate, these weapons provide capabilities that conventional firearms cannot, making them valuable tools for military units, security forces, hunters, and explorers facing dangerous environments. Their effectiveness often depends as much on the payload employed as the weapon itself.
