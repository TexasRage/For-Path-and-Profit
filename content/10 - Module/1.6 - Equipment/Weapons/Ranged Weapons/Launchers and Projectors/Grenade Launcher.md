Grenade Launchers are designed to deliver explosive munitions far beyond the range at which they could be thrown by hand. Commonly used by military units, mercenaries, and security forces, these weapons provide valuable anti-personnel and light anti-vehicle firepower in a portable package.

The listed price does not include ammunition. [[Grenades]] for a launcher cost the same as the equivalent hand Grenades, though many merchants offer a 50% discount when purchasing a case of six.

Launcher Grenades are specially designed munitions that must be fired from a Grenade Launcher and cannot be thrown by hand. The listed profile assumes the weapon is loaded with standard Fragmentation Grenades, though other grenade types may be used, altering the weapon's Damage, Critical rating, and qualities.

**Models Include:** Z50 Grenade Launcher and RGL-318 Rotary Grenade Launcher.
