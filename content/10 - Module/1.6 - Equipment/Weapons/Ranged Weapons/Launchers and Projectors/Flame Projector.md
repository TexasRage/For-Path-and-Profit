Flame Projectors are incendiary weapons designed primarily for area denial, demolition, and the destruction of organic or flammable targets such as crops, structures, and fortifications. Though often issued for military or industrial use, their devastating effectiveness against personnel makes them equally feared as anti-personnel weapons.

These weapons project a wide cone of ignited fuel, allowing the user to engulf multiple targets at once. Their indiscriminate nature makes them especially dangerous in close-quarters engagements, where friend and foe alike may be caught in the flames.

Flame Projectors rely on the Burn quality to inflict ongoing Damage. Burning fuel clings to whatever it strikes, continuing to burn until extinguished or the flames consume the remaining fuel.

**Models Include**: Merr-Sonn C-22 Flame Carbine, Merr-Sonn CR-24 Flame Rifle.
