Acid Projectors are highly restricted weapons that discharge streams of corrosive chemicals capable of rapidly eating through armor, equipment, and organic matter. To improve safety during handling, the reactive compounds are typically stored in separate reservoirs and only combine as they leave the weapon, reducing the risk of accidental exposure.

Like Flame Projectors, Acid Projectors project their payload in a narrow cone, making them effective against multiple closely grouped targets. The corrosive compounds cling to anything they strike, continuing to erode armor and exposed surfaces long after the initial attack before eventually becoming inert..
