# MICRO-ROCKET PISTOLS

Micro-Rocket Pistols are compact launchers designed to fire miniature [[Micro-Rocket|Micro-Rockets]]. Typically holding up to three Micro-Rockets, they provide a versatile alternative to larger launch systems while remaining small enough to be carried as a sidearm. An internal launch mechanism propels each Micro-Rocket at high speed, extending its effective range beyond that of most handheld launchers.

Micro-Rockets are loaded individually and may vary in payload and effect. When a Micro-Rocket is loaded, its **Damage**, **Critical rating**, and **qualities** replace those of the launcher.

See [[Micro-Rocket]] for available Micro-Rocket types.
