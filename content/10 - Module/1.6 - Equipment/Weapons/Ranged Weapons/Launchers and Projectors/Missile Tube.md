Missile Tubes are heavy, portable launchers designed to fire rocket-propelled warheads against vehicles, fortifications, and other hardened targets. While most launchers fire high-explosive or proton-charged [[Missiles]], a variety of specialized warheads are also available.

To improve portability and accuracy, Missile Tubes are often equipped with advanced targeting systems capable of tracking a target's speed, range, and signature. Many support guided firing modes that allow Missiles to home in on designated targets, while others can be fired directly without guidance.

Guided Missiles may be vulnerable to countermeasures such as decoys, chaff, or other defensive systems. Alternatively, the wielder may choose to fire a Missile directly, sacrificing guidance in exchange for a projectile that cannot be diverted by such countermeasures.

See [[Missiles]] for available ammunition types.

**Models Include:** Golan Arms HH-15 and Merr-Sonn PLX-2M.
