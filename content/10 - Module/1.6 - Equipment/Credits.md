Credits are the standard currency used throughout the galaxy. Goods and services are typically exchanged using Credits, which are accepted by most governments, corporations, and merchants.

Credits may exist as physical currency or be stored electronically on secure _[[Credsticks]]_, allowing wealth to be carried and transferred across vast distances.

Most travelers rely on _[[Credsticks]]_ backed by major financial institutions, making them widely accepted and difficult to counterfeit. Physical currency remains common in remote regions, frontier settlements, and criminal circles where anonymity or limited infrastructure makes electronic transactions more practical.

## Physical Credits

Physical Credits can be carried in bulk using the following [[Encumbrance]] guidelines:

- **100 Credits:** 1 Encumbrance when carried in a small pouch of coins or bills.
- **1,000 Credits:** 2 Encumbrance when carried in a briefcase.
- **1,000 Credits:** 1 Encumbrance when the briefcase is carried in one hand. That hand cannot be used for other tasks while carrying it.

Electronic Credits stored on _[[Credsticks]]_ do not have an Encumbrance value unless otherwise specified.
