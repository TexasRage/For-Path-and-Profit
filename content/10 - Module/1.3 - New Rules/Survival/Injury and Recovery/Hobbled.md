A Hobbled character has suffered an injury that impairs their movement and slows the [[Expedition]].

During an [[Explore|Explore Action]], the [[Expedition|Expedition's]] movement is reduced by 1 hex while the character is traveling with the group.

During structured gameplay, the character loses their free [[Maneuver]] each turn.

### Recovery

A character recovers from Hobbled using the normal [[Injury and Recovery|Injury recovery]] rules.
