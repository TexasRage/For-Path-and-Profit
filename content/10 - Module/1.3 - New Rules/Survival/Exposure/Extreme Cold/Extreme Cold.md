While exposed to Extreme Cold, a character suffers \[setback] on all checks. Characters wearing Light Armor suffer an additional \[setback].

At the end of the first [[Quarter-Day]] spent exposed to Extreme Cold, a character must succeed on an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Resilience check** or suffer 1 rank of _[[Exhaustion]]_. The difficulty increases by 1 for each consecutive [[Quarter-Day]] the character remains exposed to Extreme Cold.

A \[Despair] result on the **Resilience check** causes the character to suffer _[[Frostbite]]_.
