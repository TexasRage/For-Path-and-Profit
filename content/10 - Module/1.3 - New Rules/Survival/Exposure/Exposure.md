Extreme temperatures can threaten a character's health when they lack adequate protection. Exposure occurs when a character is subjected to [[Extreme Heat]] or [[Extreme Cold]] without sufficient protection from clothing, equipment, shelter, or other environmental measures.

## Extreme Heat

Characters exposed to extreme heat must make an appropriate check to resist its effects. Failure can result in [[Heatstroke]].

Characters may avoid or reduce the effects of extreme heat through appropriate protective equipment, shelter, or other preparations.

## Extreme Cold

Characters exposed to extreme cold must make an appropriate check to resist its effects. Failure can result in [[Frostbite]].

Characters may avoid or reduce the effects of extreme cold through appropriate protective equipment, shelter, or other preparations.
