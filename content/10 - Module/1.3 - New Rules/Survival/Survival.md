The frontier is not a place that supports life easily. Resources are scarce, travel is slow and dangerous, and even short journeys can become life-threatening without preparation. Every action away from safety carries risk, and survival is measured less by strength in combat and more by how well a group manages exhaustion, weight, exposure, and access to basic needs over time.

Survival rules represent the ongoing challenges of keeping a character alive and capable while operating away from safety. They cover the resources required to sustain the expedition, the dangers of the environment, the consequences of injury, and the requirements for recovering from the rigors of travel and exploration.

## Food and Water

Characters must maintain adequate supplies of food and water while traveling or exploring.

These necessities are abstracted into _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_, which characters use to satisfy their [[Daily Needs]]. Characters who go without Consumables suffer [[Deprivation]].

See [[Food and Water]] for the rules governing these supplies.

## Exposure

The frontier exposes characters to environmental conditions that can threaten their health and ability to continue traveling.

Weather, temperature, terrain, and other environmental hazards can cause [[Exposure]]. Appropriate equipment and preparation can protect characters from these dangers.

See [[Exposure]] for the rules governing environmental exposure and its effects.

## Injury and Recovery

Injuries and other physical hardships can impair a character and limit their ability to recover.

Characters can treat injuries and recover from their effects through appropriate care and [[Rest]].

See [[Injury and Recovery]] for the rules governing injury, treatment, and recovery.

## Resting Requirements

Rest is necessary to recover from the physical and mental demands of exploration.

Characters must meet certain requirements before they can fully benefit from [[Rest]]. Conditions such as [[Deprivation]], [[Exhaustion]], and untreated injuries can interfere with recovery.

See [[Resting Requirements]] for the rules governing rest and recovery.
