Once per day, during a [[Long Rest]], each character may make a **[[Daily Needs|Daily Needs check]]** if they have at least 1 _[[Consumable|Consumable]]_. The character rolls the \[force] to determine how effectively they ration their supplies.

The result is resolved as follows:

- \[dark]\[dark]: The character's ration is exhausted. Remove 1 _[[Consumable]]_ from their inventory.
- \[dark]: The character conserves their supplies, but does so inefficiently. They do not remove a _[[Consumable]]_, but recover only half their [[Strain]] from their [[Long Rest]], rounded down.
- \[light]: The character rations their supplies normally. Nothing happens.
- \[light]\[light]: The character rations their supplies exceptionally well. They do not remove a _[[Consumable]]_ and recover twice as much [[Strain]] from their [[Long Rest]].

If a character has no _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_, they do not make a **[[Daily Needs|Daily Needs check]]**. Instead, if they reach the end of the day without having made a **[[Daily Needs|Daily Needs check]]**, they immediately suffer the effects of [[Deprivation]].
