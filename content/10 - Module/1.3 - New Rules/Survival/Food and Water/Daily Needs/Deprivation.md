If a character goes one [[Planetary Day]] without satisfying their [[Daily Needs]], they become _Deprived_. While _Deprived_, the character can recover only half the Strain normally recovered from a [[Long Rest]] (rounded down).

A character can remain _Deprived_ for a number of consecutive days equal to their Brawn plus their ranks in Resilience. At the end of each subsequent day they remain _Deprived_, they gain 1 rank of _Exhaustion_.

These effects persist for as long as the character remains _Deprived_. Once a character satisfies their [[Daily Needs]], all effects of _Deprivation_ are removed, although any _Exhaustion_ gained remains.
