[[Mycorris]] contains ancient research ruins, forgotten facilities, and unique natural formations. The **Adventure Action** allows the [[Expedition]] to investigate these locations and uncover their secrets.

Adventuring may take anywhere from a few hours to several days, depending on the size and complexity of the site. While adventuring, the [[Expedition]] may still only benefit from one [[Short Rest]] per [[Quarter-Day]].

The [[Expedition]] may also perform [[Make Camp]], [[Cook]], [[Scan]], [[Modify Equipment]], and [[Rest]] while inside an adventure site. The GM determines the difficulty and availability of these actions based on the location and surrounding conditions.

The [[Expedition]] cannot perform [[Explore]], [[Forage]], [[Hunt]], or [[Fish]] while inside an adventure site. To perform these actions, the [[Expedition]] must leave the site and may return later.
