The [[Expedition]] may **Cook** raw _[[Protein|Proteins]]_ gathered through [[Hunt]] or [[Fish]] into _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_, preparing them for long-term use as rations.

When a character performs the **Cook Action**, they convert any number of acquired _[[Protein|Proteins]]_ into _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_. The character must have _[[Cooking Utensils]]_ and access to a fire or portable stove.

If the character has _[[Flame Paste]]_ or another means of creating a contained flame, they may create a campfire as part of this action. If another party member is performing [[Make Camp]] during the same [[Quarter-Day]], they may contribute equipment or assistance to establish a shared fire.

The character makes an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Survival or Knowledge (Education) check**.

On a success, the character converts the raw _[[Protein|Proteins]]_ into an equal number of _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_.

On a failure, the character partially spoils or burns the _[[Protein|Proteins]]_, converting only half of them into _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_, rounded down.

If the check fails with \[failure]\[failure]\[failure] or generates \[despair], the entire batch is spoiled or rendered inedible, and no _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_ are produced.
