While exploring [[Mycorris]], equipment and weapons may become damaged. A character may perform the **Modify Equipment Action** to repair damaged items, install or remove attachments, or otherwise modify their equipment.

When a character performs the **Modify Equipment Action**, they spend **1 [[Quarter-Day]]** inspecting, repairing, or modifying equipment. The specific requirements, checks, and effects depend on the type of equipment being modified.

## Repairing Equipment

Repairing equipment requires a [[Tool Kit]], along with any associated costs in **Credits** or [[Materials]].

Each type of equipment has its own repair procedure:

[[Weapon Repair]] — Rules for repairing damaged weapons.

[[Armor Repair]] — Rules for repairing damaged armor.

[[Gear Repair]] — Rules for repairing damaged gear.

## Attachments

[[Attachments]] may be installed or removed as part of this action without additional time. A character may install or remove any number of attachments, provided the equipment has sufficient **Hard Points**.

## Field Repairs

Repairing equipment in the field is inherently difficult. Repair checks made as part of this action are upgraded once due to the environmental conditions.
