At the beginning of each day, the GM rolls a d6 and applies the result using the **Weather Shift** table. The result adjusts the current [[Weather|Weather Counter]] up or down from its previous value.

When adjusting the [[Weather|Weather Counter]], the GM must follow the seasonal restrictions shown in the **[[Weather|Weather Counter]]** table. If the counter would land on a [[Weather Condition]] unavailable during the current [[Seasons|Season]], that condition is skipped and the counter continues in the same direction until a valid condition is reached. If no valid condition exists in that direction, the [[Weather|Weather Counter]] does not change.

**Example:** The current season is [[The Anchor]], and the [[Weather|Weather Counter]] is 5. The GM rolls a 6, increasing the counter by 2. This would normally result in 7, but values 6–9 are not available during [[The Anchor]]. The counter continues upward until it reaches the next available condition at 11, resulting in [[Weather Condition|Medium Snowfall]].

If instead the GM rolls a 2, the counter decreases by 1, which would normally result in 4. However, values 0–4 are not available during [[The Anchor]], and there are no valid conditions in that direction. The [[Weather|Weather Counter]] remains at 5, and the weather does not change.

| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 6   | The [[Weather\|Weather Counter]] goes up by 2                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 5   | The [[Weather\|Weather Counter]] goes up by 1                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 4-3 | If the [[Weather\|Weather Counter]] is higher than the season’s Perfect Day then it goes down by 1.<br><br>If the [[Weather\|Weather Counter]] is lower than the season’s Perfect Day then it goes up by 1.<br><br>If the [[Weather\|Weather Counter]] is equal to the season’s Perfect Day then nothing happens. |
| 2   | The [[Weather\|Weather Counter]] goes down by 1                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 1   | The [[Weather\|Weather Counter]] goes down by 2                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
