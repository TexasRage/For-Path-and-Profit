Weather on [[Mycorris]] changes gradually throughout each season. Rather than determining weather independently each day, current conditions are tracked using the Weather Counter, which represents the planet's atmospheric state.

At the beginning of the campaign, the GM determines the current [[Seasons|Season]] and sets the Weather Counter to that season's Ideal Day value. The resulting [[Weather Condition|Weather Condition]] applies for the remainder of the day.

At the start of each subsequent [[Planetary Day]], the GM rolls for a [[Shifts|Weather Shift]], adjusting the Weather Counter up or down from its current value. The new value determines the day's weather. The Weather Counter persists between days, allowing periods of stable weather as well as gradual transitions into more severe conditions.

Each season has its own range of available weather conditions. If a Weather Counter shift would produce a condition unavailable during the current [[Seasons|Season]], the counter skips that condition and continues in the same direction until it reaches a valid condition.

**Example:** The expedition begins during [[The Anchor]]. The Anchor's Ideal Day is 5, so the Weather Counter begins at 5, resulting in a [[Weather Condition|Cool Day]]. On the following day, the GM rolls for a [[Shifts|Weather Shift]] to determine how the weather changes.
