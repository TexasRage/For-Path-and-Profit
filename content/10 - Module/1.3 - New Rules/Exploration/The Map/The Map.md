A full map of [[Mycorris]] is provided within this book for the Game Master. The player-facing map is intentionally incomplete, with many locations and Points of Interest left undiscovered. These areas are revealed gradually through exploration, allowing players to chart the world and uncover its secrets over the course of the campaign.

[[Mycorris]] is a continuous world without hard borders. If the players reach the edge of the map, they emerge on the opposite side of the world rather than stopping or turning back. The planet is treated as a globe, allowing travel to wrap naturally across its surface.

### Hexes

The world map is divided into 40 km superhexes, each further subdivided into smaller 5 km hexes for detailed exploration.

Superhexes provide the broad scale used to represent the planet and long-distance travel. The smaller hexes are used when the expedition is exploring a specific region in greater detail.

Subtle alphanumeric markers are included on the map to assist with tracking and referencing specific regions during play without disrupting the map's presentation.

### Terrain

Each hex has a [[Terrain]] type representing its predominant environment. Terrain influences the difficulty of exploration and determines the kinds of challenges, resources, and discoveries characters may encounter.

See [[Terrain]] for the effects of individual terrain types.
