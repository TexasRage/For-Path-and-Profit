Brightness describes the amount of available light in an area. The current [[Quarter-Day]] and [[Seasons|Season]] determine the prevailing level of natural light.

- **Bright Light:** No penalties.
- **Dim Light:** Characters suffer \[setback]\[setback] on sight-based checks. Sight range is limited to Long range. Characters gain \[boost]\[boost] on **Stealth checks**.
- **Darkness:** Characters suffer \[setback]\[setback]\[setback] on sight-based checks. Sight range is limited to Medium range. Characters gain \[boost]\[boost]\[boost] on **Stealth checks**.

| Quarter | Bloom | Siphon | Anchor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Morning | Bright Light | Bright Light | Dim Light |
| Afternoon | Bright Light | Bright Light | Bright Light |
| Evening | Dim Light | Bright Light | Dim Light |
| Night | Darkness | Darkness | Darkness |
