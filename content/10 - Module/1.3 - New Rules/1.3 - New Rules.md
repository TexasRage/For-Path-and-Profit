This chapter introduces the rules and systems added or modified for this adventure. These rules expand the standard Star Wars RPG rules to support the challenges of exploration, survival, and extended operations on [[Mycorris]].

Unless otherwise stated, the standard Star Wars RPG rules remain in effect. Where a rule in this chapter conflicts with a standard rule, the rule presented here takes precedence.

### Survival and Exploration

[[Daily Needs]] — Tracks the basic requirements characters must meet to remain healthy during extended operations.

[[Consumable|Consumables]] — Represents the food, water, and other basic necessities required to sustain a character.

[[Deprivation]] — Describes the consequences of failing to meet a character's [[Daily Needs]].

[[Exhaustion]] — Represents the cumulative effects of prolonged physical strain, deprivation, and environmental hardship.

[[Injury and Recovery]] — Introduces persistent injuries that require rest or medical attention to recover from.

[[Climate|Environmental Hazards]] — Covers conditions caused by exposure to dangerous environments, including [[Extreme Heat]], [[Extreme Cold]], and [[Suffocation]].

[[Rest]] — Replaces the standard rest and recovery procedures with the [[Short Rest]] and [[Long Rest]] systems used during the expedition.

[[Logistics]] — Covers the storage, packing, transportation, and management of supplies and equipment during extended operations.
