Enforcers understand that violence is often most effective when it is threatened rather than used. They combine combat ability with intimidation, blackmail, negotiation, and knowledge of the criminal underworld. An Enforcer may serve as hired muscle, a debt collector, or the representative of a criminal organization, delivering its demands and ensuring they are obeyed. Although capable of handling themselves in a fight, Enforcers prefer to use violence as a tool rather than an end in itself.

An Enforcer's additional Career skills are **Brawl, Coercion, Knowledge (Underworld), and Streetwise**. If Enforcer is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills allow an Enforcer to navigate criminal circles, recognize local customs and threats, find work, and handle tense social situations while still serving as a capable combatant.
