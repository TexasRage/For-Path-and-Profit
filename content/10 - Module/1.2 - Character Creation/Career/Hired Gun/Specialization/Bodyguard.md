Bodyguards specialize in protecting people and places, using their combat skills, toughness, and awareness to keep others safe when violence breaks out. They are equally suited to serving as armed escorts, defensive specialists, or heavy combatants, and their skills make them valuable to employers ranging from legitimate organizations to criminal enterprises throughout the Outer Rim.

A Bodyguard's additional Career skills are **Gunnery, Perception, Piloting (Planetary), and Ranged (Heavy)**. If Bodyguard is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. Their combination of weapon, vehicle, and perception skills allows them to operate heavy weapons, protect their surroundings, and serve as capable pilots while keeping others out of harm's way.
