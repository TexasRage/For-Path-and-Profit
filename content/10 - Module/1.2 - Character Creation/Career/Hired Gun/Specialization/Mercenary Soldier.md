Mercenary Soldiers are warfare specialists, combining ranged combat with military discipline and battlefield leadership. They are experienced combatants who understand tactics, organization, and the practical realities of fighting as part of a unit. Most have previous experience with a military or organized fighting force and bring that training into their work as soldiers for hire. In a group, a Mercenary Soldier can take command during combat, coordinating allies while remaining a capable combatant themselves.

A Mercenary Soldier's additional Career skills are **Discipline, Gunnery, Leadership, and Ranged (Heavy)**. If Mercenary Soldier is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills allow the Mercenary Soldier to remain composed under pressure, operate heavy weapons, direct allies, and fight effectively from range.
