The Hired Gun’s eight career skills are **Athletics, Brawl, Discipline, Melee, Piloting (Planetary), Ranged (Light), Resilience, and Vigilance**. He automatically gains one rank in four of these skills (of his choosing) without spending experience, and he receives a discount when he spends experience to purchase ranks in any of these skills. A Hired Gun is a professional fighter employed to achieve direct, often violent objectives—protecting clients, securing locations, supporting larger forces, or providing the muscle needed to complete dangerous work. Unlike a Bounty Hunter, who focuses on tracking and capturing specific targets, the Hired Gun is valued for straightforward combat effectiveness.

Hired Guns are common throughout the Outer Rim, where governments, corporations, settlements, criminal organizations, and private individuals frequently need people capable of handling violence. Some are former soldiers, police officers, security personnel, or militia members, while others learned to fight through necessity in harsh environments. Many work as independent mercenaries, while others belong to mercenary companies, security firms, military units, or criminal groups—trading freedom for steady employment, equipment, and contacts. Independent Hired Guns enjoy greater autonomy but must maintain a strong reputation, as a single failed contract or dispute can make future work difficult to find.

## HIRED GUN BACKGROUND

Hired Guns come from all walks of life. Some are trained soldiers, mercenaries, bodyguards, or security personnel, while others learned to fight because violence was simply part of the world they grew up in. Some chose the profession willingly, while others discovered that their combat skills were the only reliable way to make a living. They may have left military service, lost a position of authority, or turned to mercenary work after legitimate opportunities disappeared.

The frontier offers Hired Guns plenty of work. Settlements need guards, expeditions need protection, and travelers need someone willing to face whatever waits beyond the next horizon. An expedition may provide steady employment, new opportunities, or a chance to leave an old life behind. A Hired Gun's background should explain how they learned to fight, why they chose this profession, and what brought them to the expedition.

### YESTERDAY'S HERO

Once, the character was celebrated for their accomplishments, respected by their peers, and perhaps even famous throughout their homeworld. Whether through a political enemy, a public scandal, a failed mission, or simply the passage of time, that success eventually came to an end. Now the character finds themselves far removed from their former status, with only their skills and experience remaining. They may have turned to work as a Hired Gun to rebuild their reputation and reclaim what they lost, or they may simply be trying to escape a past that refuses to stay buried.

### EX-MILITARY

The character spent part of their life serving in a military, planetary defense force, or other organized fighting force. When their service ended, they found that their most valuable and marketable skills were still those they had learned as a soldier. Rather than return to civilian life, they turned to mercenary work, security, or other dangerous jobs where their combat experience could earn them a living. Their military service may have ended honorably, been cut short by disagreement or circumstance, or left them with reasons to avoid returning home.

### THE AVENGER

The character's life was changed by someone who wronged them, leaving them consumed by the need for revenge. They abandoned their former life to pursue their target, taking whatever work is necessary to survive while gathering the skills, resources, and information needed to eventually deliver retribution. Every job brings another opportunity to get closer to their goal, and the character is willing to endure almost anything until their vengeance is complete.

### SCION OF A LEGACY

The character was raised under the shadow of a parent, mentor, or other respected figure whose reputation shaped their life. Whether they admired that legacy or resented the expectations placed upon them, they eventually chose a path of their own. Becoming a Hired Gun may allow them to make use of skills passed down to them while forging their own identity, or it may be an attempt to prove themselves worthy of the name they carry. They may strive to surpass their predecessor, restore a tarnished family reputation, or deliberately build a legacy unlike the one that came before.

### PEACE TURNED TO WAR

The character once lived a peaceful life, working an ordinary profession far removed from violence. Then something happened that forced them to abandon that life and learn how to fight. A war, tragedy, personal loss, or other crisis changed their path and pushed them toward becoming a Hired Gun. Although they have developed the skills necessary to survive their new life, their former profession and the values they learned there still influence who they are.

## HIRED GUN OBLIGATIONS

Hired Guns often carry obligations from the work they have done and the people they have worked for. They may owe money to former employers, favors to old associates, or compensation to those affected by their actions. Contracts can also create lasting relationships with employers, clients, comrades, or rivals, and leaving a job does not always mean leaving its consequences behind. For some Hired Guns, the expedition may be a chance to escape old debts or find steadier work. Others may have joined because they are still bound by a contract to protect the expedition or its members.

As the expedition continues, new employers, enemies, and responsibilities may create additional obligations of their own. Obligation helps define what a Hired Gun has left behind and who may still have a claim on them. Players should consider how their Obligation shaped their career, why they joined the expedition, and what consequences might follow them to Mycorris. Players may use [[Hired Gun Obligations]] in place of [[Core Obligations]]. They may roll randomly or choose an Obligation that fits their character’s history. Characters begin with the normal amount of Obligation based on the size of the group and may take on additional Obligation to gain extra starting experience or credits.

## HIRED GUN SPECIALIZATIONS

The Hired Gun career offers a wide range of Specializations for characters who make their living through combat, protection, and the use of force. Some are disciplined professionals trained for military service or bodyguard work, while others have built their reputations as enforcers, demolitions experts, or heavy weapons specialists. Each Specialization represents a different way of surviving—and making a living—in a galaxy where there is always someone willing to pay for a capable fighter.

## HIRED GUN MOTIVATIONS

Hired Guns are often driven by the circumstances that led them to make a living through violence. Their Motivation may come from ambition, personal loss, a desire for security, loyalty to a cause, or simply the need to find a place in a dangerous galaxy. A Hired Gun's Motivation helps explain why they continue taking dangerous work and what they ultimately hope to gain from the life.

Hired Guns may roll on the [[Types of Motivation]] table or choose a Motivation that fits their character. The career-specific result is Conflict. If a character has Conflict as their Motivation, they may then choose or roll for a specific Conflict on the [[Specific Conflicts]] table. These options represent the different struggles that can drive a Hired Gun, from seeking revenge or proving themselves to overcoming a troubled past or finding purpose in a life built around violence.
