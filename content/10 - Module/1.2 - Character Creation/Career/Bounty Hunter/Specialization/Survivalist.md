Survivalists specialize in tracking and hunting targets beyond the reach of civilization. They are experts at surviving hostile environments, finding what others cannot, and keeping themselves and their companions alive in the wilderness. A Survivalist adds **Knowledge (Xenology), Perception, Resilience, and Survival** to their Career Skills. If Survivalist is their starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain 1 free rank in each. Their ability to navigate, hunt, identify threats, and live off the land makes them particularly valuable on expeditions into unexplored territory.
