Fringers are seasoned travelers of the galaxy’s remote regions. They know how to find work, avoid trouble, locate useful contacts, and survive where civilized infrastructure is scarce. Fringers add **Deception, Knowledge (Underworld), Streetwise, and Vigilance** to their Career Skills. If this is the character’s starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain one free rank in each without spending starting experience.

A Fringer may join the expedition because they are already familiar with life beyond established worlds, or because they see an opportunity to put their frontier experience to greater use. Their knowledge of remote communities, criminal networks, and the less reputable corners of the galaxy makes them invaluable when an expedition ventures beyond the reach of civilization.
