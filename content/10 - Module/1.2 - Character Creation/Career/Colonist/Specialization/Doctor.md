Doctors bring medical knowledge to places where professional care is often scarce. Whether trained on a Core World or taught through years of frontier experience, they are skilled at treating injuries, illnesses, and the unexpected problems that arise far from modern medical facilities.

A Doctor adds **Cool, Knowledge (Education), Medicine, and Resilience** to their Career Skills. If Doctor is their starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain one free rank in each, without spending starting experience. A Doctor may join the expedition to provide desperately needed medical care, pursue medical research, escape the limitations of established practice, or simply seek a place where their skills can make a greater difference. On a frontier world, a Doctor can quickly become one of the settlement's most valuable members.
