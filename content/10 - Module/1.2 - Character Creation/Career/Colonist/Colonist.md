The Colonist is a professional, leader, or specialist who relies on knowledge, social connections, and practical expertise rather than combat. Colonists may serve as doctors, politicians, scholars, merchants, administrators, or other professionals whose skills are valuable throughout the galaxy. Their eight Career Skills are **Charm, Deception, Knowledge (Core Worlds), Knowledge (Education), Knowledge (Lore), Leadership, Negotiation, and Streetwise**. They gain 1 free rank in four of these skills of their choice and receive a discount when purchasing ranks in any of them.

Colonists excel at solving problems through knowledge, negotiation, and leadership, whether building communities, managing organizations, treating the wounded, or navigating competing interests. For this adventure, a Colonist might join the expedition seeking opportunity, pursuing a professional or personal goal, representing an organization, or helping establish a new settlement. Their Motivation and Obligation should help explain why they are willing to leave their former life behind and commit to the expedition.

## COLONIST BACKGROUNDS

Colonists can come from almost any walk of life. Some leave established worlds seeking open spaces, new opportunities, or a chance to escape their past, while others are born and raised on remote settlements where generations of families have worked to make difficult worlds more livable. A Colonist might be a skilled professional, a settler seeking a fresh start, a frontier native, or someone with nowhere else to go. What they share is a willingness to leave the familiar behind and build something new.

For this adventure, players should consider why their character joined the expedition and what they hope to accomplish on the new world. They might be seeking a new home, escaping their past, pursuing an opportunity, or bringing skills the expedition needs. Their Motivation and Obligation can provide additional reasons for joining the expedition and goals they hope to pursue once they arrive.

### THE OPPORTUNIST

The Opportunist sees the expedition as a chance to build a better future. They may seek wealth, status, experience, influence, or simply a fresh start. Some bring valuable skills the settlement needs, while others see untapped resources, businesses, or positions of authority waiting to be claimed. Willing to take risks and embrace uncertainty, Opportunists may underestimate the challenges of frontier life or clash with those who see them as outsiders seeking to profit from the colony.

Whatever their profession, the Opportunist believes the expedition offers something worth pursuing. They may be seeking experience, establishing a business or practice, representing an organization, studying an unexplored world, or simply looking for a place to build a new life.

### THE GRIZZLED SETTLER

The Grizzled Settler has spent years enduring the hardships of frontier life. They may have arrived with dreams of building a better future, only to see those dreams eroded by disease, disaster, violence, economic hardship, or the simple difficulty of surviving on a remote world. Years of hardship have left them practical, cynical, and slow to trust. They have learned that good intentions do not make a settlement succeed and that even the best plans can be undone by circumstances beyond anyone's control.

Despite their pessimism, their experience can make them exceptionally valuable to an expedition. They may have spent years treating illness with inadequate supplies, fighting lawlessness, losing everything to a failed business, or abandoning a formal career to survive on their own. Whatever their profession, the Grizzled Settler has learned the hard way what life on the frontier demands.

### THE FUGITIVE

The galaxy is full of those running from someone or something. Some flee bounty hunters, governments, corporations, or the law; others are victims of false accusations, political persecution, or enemies they cannot face. For them, the expedition offers a chance to disappear into a new frontier and start over.

A Fugitive may join because Mycorris offers distance, anonymity, and the possibility of a new identity. They might be fleeing financial crimes, political enemies, corporate secrets, unethical actions, or the consequences of abusing their authority. Whatever their reason, the character must decide whether Mycorris is truly a fresh start—or simply another place to run.

### THE LOCAL LEADER

The character grew up or established a life in a small frontier community, where a handful of trusted individuals were responsible for keeping the settlement running. Without the institutions and resources of more developed worlds, local leaders often serve as teachers, healers, business owners, forepersons, advocates, or lawkeepers. The character may have filled one of these roles, becoming an important part of the community simply because someone had to.

The expedition offers a chance to put those skills toward something larger. They may have been recruited for their expertise, volunteered to give their community a better future, or simply recognized an opportunity to build something new. Leaving their old community behind means accepting responsibility for the people and obligations they leave behind—and deciding what they hope to build on Mycorris.

### THE IDEALIST

The character could have remained somewhere safer and more comfortable, but instead chose to venture into the unknown because they believe their skills can make a difference. The galaxy is full of communities struggling with poverty, disease, exploitation, and isolation, and the character sees the expedition as an opportunity to help people who might otherwise be forgotten.

The character joins because they believe its success can create something better than what came before. Whether driven by compassion, justice, discovery, or a desire to build a better future, they are willing to accept the dangers of the unknown to pursue that ideal.

## COLONIST OBLIGATION

Colonists can have a particularly complicated relationship with Obligation. Many have left behind an established life, along with the debts, relationships, responsibilities, and problems that came with it. Others remain tied to the people or communities they left behind, creating obligations that can follow them across the galaxy. For some, the expedition is an attempt to escape an old Obligation; for others, it is an opportunity to fulfill a responsibility by securing the resources or opportunities they need.

Colonists may also develop new obligations through the expedition. Establishing a settlement on an unfamiliar world creates strong ties between its members, and everyone depends on one another for survival. A failed harvest, damaged equipment, or dangerous discovery can affect the entire community. As the settlement grows, characters may find themselves responsible for people who depend upon them. Obligation helps define where a Colonist came from, what they left behind, and what continues to demand their attention. Players may use [[Colonist Obligations]] in place of [[Core Obligations]], rolling randomly or selecting an Obligation that fits their character. Characters begin with the normal amount of Obligation based on group size and may take on additional Obligation for additional starting experience or credits.

The following specializations represent some of the most common paths taken by Colonists. They reflect the diverse roles needed to build, govern, and sustain communities, from professionals and leaders to skilled workers and frontier specialists. A Colonist’s chosen specialization can help define both the life they led before the expedition and the role they hope to fill on Mycorris.

These specializations are not limited to life on established colonies. Each can provide a reason for joining the expedition and a valuable role in establishing a new settlement, whether that means treating its people, organizing its resources, managing its affairs, protecting its community, or helping it grow. ## COLONIST MOTIVATION

Colonists are driven by the desire to build something where nothing existed before. Their Motivation may come from the pursuit of a better life, the desire to establish a home, the need to provide for others, or the satisfaction of turning an undeveloped frontier into something lasting. A Colonist's Motivation helps explain what they hope to create and why they are willing to endure the hardships of settling a new world.

Colonists may roll on the [[Types of Motivation]] table or choose a Motivation that fits their character. The career-specific result is Creation. If a character has Creation as their Motivation, they may then choose or roll for a specific Creation on the table [[Specific Creations]]. These options represent the different things that can drive a Colonist, from building a home or community and establishing a successful business to creating a legacy, developing the frontier, or proving that they can build something that will endure.
