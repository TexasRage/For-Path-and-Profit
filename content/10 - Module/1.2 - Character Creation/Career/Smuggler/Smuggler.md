The Smuggler's eight Career skills are **Coordination, Deception, Knowledge (Underworld), Perception, Piloting (Space), Skulduggery, Streetwise, and Vigilance**. They automatically gain one rank in four of these skills of their choosing without spending experience, and receive a discount when spending experience to purchase ranks in any of these skills. Smugglers are crafty operators who make their living moving people, goods, and information through the galaxy, often avoiding Imperial authorities and other dangers along the way.

Though Smugglers value their freedom, that freedom often comes with debts, dangerous employers, and the constant need to keep their ships operational. Many become indebted to powerful individuals or organizations, taking increasingly risky jobs to stay in business or earn enough to regain their independence. Despite these complications, Smugglers are invaluable throughout the Outer Rim, where they transport goods that legitimate trade cannot provide. Their knowledge of the underworld, fringe communities, and unconventional methods makes them useful to anyone willing to operate outside the law.

## SMUGGLER BACKGROUND

Smugglers come from a wide variety of backgrounds, and there are countless paths that can lead someone into a life of moving goods and people outside legitimate channels. Some learned the trade from family or a mentor, while others turned to smuggling after losing legitimate work or finding themselves on the wrong side of the law. Whatever their origin, their skills, connections, and experience have shaped how they survive on the fringes of the galaxy.

A Smuggler's background should explain how they entered the trade, what they did before becoming a Smuggler, and what brought them to the expedition. The following options provide starting points for developing a character's history and can be combined with their Motivation and Obligation to create a more complete character concept. Players are not limited to these options and may create a background of their own.

### DISADVANTAGED

The character grew up in poverty, oppression, or another difficult circumstance where survival was never guaranteed, whether through being poor, enslaved, exploited, or simply lacking basic resources. This harsh upbringing forced them to rely on themselves, adapt quickly, and seize whatever opportunities they could find. Their difficult past shaped the instincts, habits, and skills that now allow them to survive on the fringes of the galaxy.

### PRIVILEGED

The character comes from a life of wealth, status, or rare opportunity, whether through birth, inheritance, or a fortunate upbringing. They may have since lost their fortune, abandoned their old life, or simply grown tired of the expectations that came with it. Even so, they often retain the confidence, habits, and worldview of someone accustomed to privilege and influence. Their former life continues to shape how they navigate the galaxy and interact with those around them.

### EX-MILITARY

The character previously served in the Republic military, a planetary defense force, a local security organization, or another organized fighting force before leaving or being discharged. Their service provided discipline, training, and valuable connections, but they may have left seeking greater freedom, after refusing orders they could not accept, or simply because they could no longer tolerate military life. They may have left honorably, been expelled for misconduct, or become disillusioned with the organization they served. Whatever the circumstances, their time in uniform shaped how they approach danger, authority, and life on the fringes of the galaxy.

### RESPECTABLE

The character once made an honest living and had little reason to break the law, until changing circumstances pushed them toward smuggling. They may have lost legitimate work, fallen victim to corrupt officials, been unfairly accused of a crime, or simply found that legal trade could no longer provide a living. Others may have always viewed laws as obstacles rather than moral boundaries, gradually expanding from legitimate business into increasingly questionable ventures. Whatever the cause, the character's transition into smuggling was not necessarily planned—they simply discovered that the opportunities they needed existed outside the law.

### CYNICAL

The character's view of the galaxy was changed by a traumatic event, betrayal, or devastating loss that left them with little faith in law, society, or other people. They may have lost their home, been betrayed by someone they trusted, or suffered an injustice that convinced them the galaxy rewards those who look out for themselves. As a result, they approach life with suspicion and rarely take anything at face value. They may be fiercely independent, reluctant to trust others, or determined to ensure they are never again caught unprepared or at someone else's mercy.

### BORN AND BRED

The character has been involved in smuggling for as long as they can remember. They may come from a family of smugglers, have been raised by criminals or outlaws, or simply grown up in a community where smuggling was a normal way of life. They learned early how to navigate the fringes of society, recognize danger, and make a living outside conventional laws and institutions. Smuggling is not merely a career for them—it is the only life they have ever known, and they are comfortable with the risks and freedoms that come with it.

## SMUGGLER OBLIGATIONS

Smugglers often carry obligations from the jobs they take and the people they depend on. They may owe credits to employers, favors to contacts, promises to associates, or compensation for cargo that never reached its destination. A Smuggler may pay off one debt only to take on another, making the ability to manage competing responsibilities an essential part of surviving the profession.

The expedition may give a Smuggler a chance to escape old debts, find new clients, or turn their skills toward something more profitable. Others may already be bound to the expedition through a contract, favor, or dangerous commitment. As the expedition continues, new contacts, enemies, debts, and opportunities can create obligations of their own. Players may use [[Smuggler Obligations]] in place of [[Core Obligations]], rolling randomly or choosing an Obligation that fits their character’s history. Characters may also take on additional Obligation to gain extra starting experience or credits.

## SMUGGLER SPECIALIZATIONS

The Smuggler is one of the galaxy's most versatile careers, with specializations covering pilots, scoundrels, thieves, and other specialists who survive through cunning, deception, and adaptability. The available specializations allow characters to approach smuggling in many different ways, from skilled pilots and criminals to charismatic negotiators, gamblers, and gunslingers.

## SMUGGLER MOTIVATIONS

Smugglers may come from very different backgrounds, but most are driven by the promise of a **Reward**. The job requires them to transport dangerous cargo, deal with questionable employers, and constantly risk capture or worse, so they need something worth taking those risks for. That reward is not always credits; a Smuggler might seek independence, respect, security for their family, excitement, or some other personal goal. The Reward that drives a character can reveal much about who they are and how they approach life on the fringe.

A Smuggler's Motivation can also provide insight into their Background and influence how they interact with the rest of the group. Career-specific Motivations are determined or chosen from the [[Types of Motivation]] table. The Smuggler’s type of Motivation is Reward; if this result is selected, the player may then roll or choose a specific Reward from the [[Specific Rewards]] table to determine what ultimately drives their Smuggler.
