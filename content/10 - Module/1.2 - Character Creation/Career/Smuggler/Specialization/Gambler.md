Gamblers thrive on risk, deception, and reading the people around them. They know that winning is not simply a matter of luck, but of recognizing opportunities, identifying tells, and knowing when to bluff or take a chance. Whether playing a game of chance, negotiating a deal, or talking an opponent out of violence, a Gambler keeps others guessing while maintaining an air of calm confidence. Their talents make them equally comfortable at the Sabacc table, in high-stakes negotiations, or running a con.

A Gambler's additional Career skills are **Computers, Cool, Deception, and Skulduggery**. If Gambler is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills allow the Gambler to deceive others, manipulate situations, and use sleight of hand or technology to keep the odds in their favor.
