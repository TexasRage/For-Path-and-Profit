Blockade Runners are expert starship pilots who treat their vessels as extensions of their own instincts and abilities. They can push aging or underpowered ships beyond their normal performance and excel at evading pursuit, navigating dangerous routes, and getting through situations where other pilots would turn back. Their skills make them valuable to smugglers, merchants, explorers, and anyone else who needs a pilot capable of getting a ship through hostile territory.

A Blockade Runner's additional Career skills are **Astrogation, Gunnery, Piloting (Planetary), and Piloting (Space)**. If Blockade Runner is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. Their talents further enhance their abilities in the cockpit, making them particularly effective during space travel, pursuit, and starship combat.
