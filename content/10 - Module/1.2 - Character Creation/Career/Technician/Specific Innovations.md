| d100   | Specific Innovations                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-10   | **Fabrication**: The character enjoys building useful things, refining existing designs rather than inventing entirely new concepts. They believe the best creations come from study, experimentation, and practical improvement.                                                         |
| 11-20  | **Invention**: The character is driven to create something genuinely new. Solving problems is satisfying, but the act of inventing may be even more rewarding, even when a simpler solution would work.                                                                                   |
| 21-30  | **Modification**: The character loves improving existing technology. They constantly add parts, rewrite code, and find new uses for equipment, often continuing to tinker long after others consider the work finished.                                                                   |
| 31-40  | **Personal Improvement**: The character seeks to overcome the limitations of their own body and mind. Cybernetics, augmentation, and experimental technology offer a path toward becoming stronger, faster, smarter, or simply more capable.                                              |
| 41-50  | **Waste Not, Want Not**: The character refuses to let useful technology go to waste. Scrap, broken machinery, obsolete components, and discarded droids are all potential resources waiting to be repaired, rebuilt, or repurposed.                                                       |
| 51-60  | **Technological Fascination**: The character is obsessed with a particular field of technology, whether starships, weapons, droids, cybernetics, or something more obscure. They know its history, models, variations, and every obscure detail they can discover.                        |
| 61-70  | **Technical Business**: The character dreams of building a successful business around their technical expertise. Whether legitimate or illicit, their operation is always on their mind, and every new opportunity is a chance to expand or improve it.                                   |
| 71-80  | **Technological Historian**: The character seeks out ancient and obsolete technology, studying and preserving devices that others consider worthless. Their collection may contain priceless discoveries—or nothing but expensive junk.                                                   |
| 81-90  | **Technological Necessity**: The character is determined to create or acquire a specific device, system, or piece of technology. Perhaps it could save a life, protect a community, or solve an otherwise impossible problem. Completing it may become their life's greatest achievement. |
| 91-100 | **Technical Rival**: The character is driven to surpass another Technician. Whether the rivalry is mutual or entirely one-sided, every new invention, modification, or accomplishment becomes another opportunity to prove they are the better engineer.                                  |
