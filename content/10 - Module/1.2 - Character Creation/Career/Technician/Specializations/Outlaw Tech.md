Outlaw Techs operate outside conventional technical institutions, developing modifications and devices that legitimate engineers might refuse to build. They work with salvaged components, restricted technology, and experimental designs, often finding solutions by combining whatever materials are available. Their creations may be crude, ingenious, dangerous, or all three.

An Outlaw Tech's additional Career skills are **Coordination, Knowledge (Underworld), Mechanics, and Skulduggery**. If Outlaw Tech is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills allow Outlaw Techs to work with unconventional technology, acquire restricted components, and modify equipment without relying on legitimate suppliers or facilities.
