Mechanics are practical engineers who specialize in keeping vehicles, machinery, and other complex equipment operational. They can diagnose problems, perform repairs, and keep equipment functioning under conditions where replacement parts and proper facilities may be difficult to find. Mechanics are especially valuable on the frontier, where a broken machine may mean the difference between completing a job and being stranded.

A Mechanic's additional Career skills are **Astrogation, Coordination, Mechanics, and Piloting (Planetary)**. If Mechanic is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills allow Mechanics to understand complex machinery, operate vehicles, and keep equipment functioning even when working far from proper repair facilities.
