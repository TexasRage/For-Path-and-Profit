Cyber Techs specialize in the intersection of biology and machinery. They install, maintain, and modify cybernetic implants, helping individuals replace damaged body parts or enhance their natural capabilities. Their work requires both technical precision and an understanding of the living beings who depend on their creations.

A Cyber Tech's additional Career skills are **Cool, Medicine, Mechanics, and Resilience**. If Cyber Tech is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills allow Cyber Techs to work safely with cybernetic systems, treat the biological complications of augmentation, and endure the demanding conditions of their profession.
