| d100   | Specific Obligation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01-08  | **Crew**: The Technician feels responsible for the safety and well-being of a crew or team. Whether maintaining equipment or looking after its members, they place the crew's needs above their own.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 09-16  | **Contract**: The Technician is bound by an important contract to provide repairs, installations, or technical services. Delays or failure to fulfill the agreement can have serious consequences.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 17-24  | **Blackmail**: Someone possesses damaging technical evidence against the Technician, such as stolen records, sliced data, or maintenance logs. To keep the secret buried, the Technician must comply with their demands.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 25-32  | **Bounty**: A bounty has been placed on the Technician after exposing, or attempting to expose, the crimes of a powerful individual or organization. Their knowledge makes them a valuable target.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 33-40  | **Criminal**: The character has a criminal record or is accused of a crime (rightly or wrongly), most likely for using technology illegally or providing the means for another to do so. Though the PC is not currently in custody, there are outstanding warrants for the PC's arrest. The PC might seek to settle this Obligation by paying ongoing legal costs or fines, burying evidence, or convincingly demonstrating innocence in the matter. |
| 41-48  | **Debt**: The Technician owes a substantial debt, often from training, specialized equipment, or starting a business. Repayment may require credits, services, or both.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 49-56  | **Failed Installation/Repair**: A disastrous repair or modification ruined both the Technician's work and reputation. Restoring the damaged item—and their good name—has become a personal priority.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 57-64  | **Family**: The Technician's family depends on their specialized skills to maintain essential equipment or infrastructure. Finding a permanent solution is the only way to escape these ongoing responsibilities.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 65-72  | **Favor**: The Technician owes a significant favor to someone who once protected or assisted them. That debt may be repaid through many small acts or one difficult request.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 73-80  | **Unfinished Business**: The Technician is consumed by an important long-term project, whether a prototype, starship refit, or complex program. Until it is completed, it remains a constant burden on their mind.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 81-88  | **Obsession**: The Technician has an unhealthy fascination with technology, droids, or computers. They are driven to improve, repair, or invent, often at the expense of other responsibilities.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 89-96  | **Responsibility**: The Technician feels a strong sense of responsibility toward a person, organization, community, or important project. This may include a mentor, apprentice, business, or group that depends on their expertise. Fulfilling significant duties or helping those who rely on them can reduce this Obligation.                                                                                                                     |
| 97-100 | Roll twice on this chart. Starting Obligation is split into two different origins. (This does not increase the Obligation's magnitude; divide the starting Obligation into two equal parts, each with a different type).                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
