A character with a Cause Motivation is driven by an external group, belief, or ideal that they consider important. They are willing to take risks or step outside their comfort zone to support their cause, which may benefit others, serve their own interests, or even promote beliefs that others consider dangerous or harmful.

### Specific Causes

| d100   | Specific Cause                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-10   | **Spirituality**: The character actively supports a religious or spiritual belief or organization. Their beliefs may follow an established tradition, such as the Jedi or Sith, or another faith or philosophy.                             |
| 11-20  | **The Weak**: The character fights for the underdog and opposes bullies, oppression, and tyranny. They put the needs of those who cannot defend themselves before their own.                                                                |
| 21-30  | **Non-Human Rights**: The character fights for equal rights and treatment for non-human species. They oppose discrimination and work to challenge systems that treat certain species as inferior.                                           |
| 31-40  | **Local Politics**: The character supports a political cause within a particular planet, system, or community. They may campaign for candidates, support reforms, or actively work within a political organization.                         |
| 41-50  | **Freedom**: The character opposes tyranny and seeks to expand freedom and self-determination throughout the galaxy. They may actively resist oppressive governments or simply support those who fight against them.                        |
| 51-60  | **Outlaws**: The character sympathizes with those who live outside the law, including smugglers, mercenaries, and other outlaws. They may provide aid to such groups or oppose laws they consider unjust.                                   |
| 61-70  | **Emancipation**: The character considers slavery and indentured servitude an abomination. They actively work to free those held in bondage and oppose organizations that profit from slavery.                                              |
| 71-80  | **Droid Rights**: The character believes droids deserve recognition and fair treatment as members of galactic society rather than merely tools or servants.                                                                                 |
| 81-90  | **Commerce**: The character champions free trade and the interests of merchants, traders, and businesses. They may oppose restrictions that interfere with commerce or advocate for economic opportunity.                                   |
| 91-100 | **Order**: The character believes that stability and strong institutions are necessary for a functioning society. They may support the Republic, another government, or an established authority they believe can maintain peace and order. |
