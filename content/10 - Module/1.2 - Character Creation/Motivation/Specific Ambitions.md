| d100   | Specific Ambition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-10   | **Friendship**: The character seeks meaningful friendships and goes out of their way to make a good impression. They may be naturally social or rely on their actions and deeds to earn the trust of others.                                              |
| 11-20  | **Love**: The character is driven by love and intimacy. They may already have someone they love or hope to find a person with whom they can share their life.                                                                                             |
| 21-30  | **Freedom**: The character desires the freedom to live life on their own terms. They may seek to escape their own Obligations or work to free others from oppression and servitude.                                                                       |
| 31-40  | **Fame**: The character seeks recognition and wants their accomplishments known throughout the galaxy. They revel in attention and hope their deeds will earn them a reputation.                                                                          |
| 41-50  | **Wealth**: The character is motivated by money and material prosperity. They may pursue legitimate business, investments, valuable discoveries, or less respectable means of increasing their wealth.                                                    |
| 51-60  | **Status**: The character seeks to improve their social standing through titles, honors, achievements, or influence. They may come from humble beginnings or simply desire a higher position than the one they currently hold.                            |
| 61-70  | **Expertise**: The character strives to excel in their chosen profession or master a particular skill. They constantly seek opportunities to learn, practice, and improve.                                                                                |
| 71-80  | **Wanderlust**: The character is driven to explore the galaxy and experience places, cultures, and opportunities beyond the familiar. They may seek unexplored regions, new experiences, or simply the excitement of never staying in one place for long. |
| 81-90  | **Power**: The character seeks authority and control over their circumstances and, perhaps, others. They may desire political influence, leadership, wealth, or simply the ability to determine their own fate.                                           |
| 91-100 | **Spirituality**: The character is driven by a religious, philosophical, or spiritual calling. Their beliefs may be rooted in an established tradition, such as the Jedi or Sith, or in a personal philosophy of their own.                               |
