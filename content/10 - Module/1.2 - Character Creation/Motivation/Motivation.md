Every character has a reason for joining the Republic Expedition to Mycorris. Motivation helps define what drives a character forward, what they hope to accomplish, and what makes their journey meaningful.

While Obligation represents the debts, responsibilities, and complications a character must resolve, Motivation represents the goals, beliefs, and relationships that inspire them to take action. A character's Obligation may explain what they are running from, but their Motivation explains what they are searching for.

During character creation, each player chooses one or two Motivations. Players may select from the standard Motivation types, a Career-specific Motivation type, or create a custom Motivation with the GM's approval. Career-specific Motivation types are presented within the appropriate Career section.

A character's Motivation may be connected to their Obligation, but the two do not need to be related. A character may join the expedition to escape a debt, seek redemption for a past mistake, protect someone they care about, or pursue a discovery that could change their future.

### WHY JOIN THE EXPEDITION?

Before choosing a Motivation, consider why your character accepted a place on the expedition. Some common reasons include:

- Seeking knowledge, fame, or recognition through exploration.
- Searching for valuable resources or new opportunities.
- Building a new life away from old enemies or obligations.
- Protecting a person, group, or ideal.
- Fulfilling a promise or repaying a debt.
- Pursuing a personal mystery connected to Mycorris.
- Seeking answers about the planet's unusual properties.
- Representing an organization with interests in the expedition.

Your Motivation should provide the GM with opportunities to create personal stories and challenges throughout the campaign.

## TYPES OF MOTIVATION

Motivations are organized into four broad categories: Ambition, Cause, Relationship, and Career-specific. During character creation, players may choose a category and determine their specific Motivation from the corresponding table, or create a custom Motivation with the GM's approval. Players may also roll on the Types of Motivation table.

Some Careers also provide an additional Motivation category representing goals or drives commonly associated with that profession. Career-specific Motivation categories and their tables are presented within the appropriate Career section.

#### Types of Motivation

### AMBITION

A character with an Ambition Motivation is driven by a personal goal, often centered on self-improvement or achievement. They may seek love, wealth, status, power, knowledge, or another personal desire. For specific categories of ambition, see [[Specific Ambitions]], which details the types of ambitions players can choose from. They should consider what they ultimately hope to achieve and why it matters to them.

### CAUSE

A character with a Cause Motivation is driven by an external group, belief, or ideal that they consider important. They are willing to take risks or step outside their comfort zone to support their cause, which may benefit others, serve their own interests, or even promote beliefs that others consider dangerous or harmful. Players can roll on [[Specific Causes]], which lists the different types of causes.

### RELATIONSHIP

A character with a Relationship Motivation is driven by their connection to a specific person, creature, group, or place. The relationship may be loving, respectful, competitive, antagonistic, or one-sided, but the character is willing to set aside their own needs to protect or support its subject. Players can look at [[Specific Relations]] for the different types of relations.

## MOTIVATIONS IN PLAY

Unlike Obligation, Motivation does not have a direct mechanical effect. Instead, it serves as a guide for roleplaying and provides the GM with opportunities to challenge characters with choices related to their goals and beliefs.

The GM can use a character's Motivation to introduce opportunities, complications, and rewards. A character driven by discovery may uncover a lost secret on Mycorris, while a character motivated by protecting others may face difficult choices involving the safety of the expedition.

Over time, a character's experiences may strengthen, challenge, or change their Motivation. These changes should occur naturally through the story as the character's goals evolve.
