The Homestead gives the [[Expedition]] a permanent foothold on [[Mycorris]]. It provides a secure base from which the party can recover, store supplies, process resources, develop new capabilities, and support increasingly ambitious expeditions.

Establishing a Homestead represents a major milestone in the campaign. As the settlement develops, the party can construct new facilities, improve existing ones, and expand its ability to support the Expedition.
