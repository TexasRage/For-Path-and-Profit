Homestead Upgrades improve the capabilities of existing facilities or provide new benefits to the settlement. Upgrades represent investments in infrastructure, equipment, personnel, and specialized technology that allow the Homestead to support increasingly demanding operations.

Upgrades are purchased and constructed through the [[Construction]] system. Unless otherwise specified, an upgrade remains permanently installed once completed.

The addition of any facility upgrade requires the expenditure of either Credits or Obligation, as well as a defined construction time. These costs represent the resources, labor, and logistical effort required to integrate new systems into the Homestead.

## Facility Upgrades

Each facility has its own available upgrades. Upgrades may improve production, increase capacity, reduce upkeep, or provide entirely new capabilities.

[[Market Upgrades]] — Improve the Homestead's ability to trade and acquire goods.

[[Workshop Upgrades]] — Expand manufacturing, repair, and equipment modification capabilities.

[[Food Production Upgrades]] — Improve the production and storage of food and other supplies.

[[Infirmary Upgrades]] — Improve medical treatment and medical supply production.

[[Defense Upgrades]] — Improve the Homestead's protection against hostile threats.

[[Dock Upgrades]] — Improve vehicle storage, maintenance, and deployment capabilities.

[[Lab Upgrades]] — Improve research and specialized resource processing.
