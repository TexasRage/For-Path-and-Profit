The Kitchen serves as the Homestead's primary food preparation area and storage facility for _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_.

[[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]] draw their daily _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_ from the Kitchen's stock. The Kitchen must contain sufficient _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_ to meet the Homestead's daily [[Sustenance]] requirements.

_[[Consumable|Consumables]]_ produced by the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] or brought to the settlement by the [[Expedition]] may be stored in the Kitchen.
