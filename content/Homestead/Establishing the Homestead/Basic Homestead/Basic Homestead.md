The Basic Homestead is the initial settlement established by the [[Expedition]] on [[Mycorris]]. It provides the party with a secure base of operations and the basic infrastructure needed to support continued exploration.

The party must select a four-hex cluster, representing a 10-square-kilometer operational area, and secure it by exploring and clearing immediate threats before construction can begin.

## Facilities

The Basic Homestead includes the following facilities:

- [[Main House]]
- [[Kitchen]]
- [[Basic Power Generator]]
- [[Water Well]]
- [[Living Quarters]]

The Expedition begins with 1 Homestead Colonist per player character, representing settlers and support personnel assisting in establishing and operating the settlement.

## Construction

**Cost:** 50,000 Credits\
**Obligation:** +5\
**Construction Time:** 6 Planetary Days

See [[Construction]] for the rules governing Homestead construction, including available methods of payment and contributing labor.

The 5 Obligation gained from establishing the Basic Homestead is permanent and cannot be settled. See [[Homestead Obligation]].
