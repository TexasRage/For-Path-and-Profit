The Homestead must provide adequate food, water, and other basic necessities to sustain its [[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]].

Each [[Homestead Colonist|Colonist]] requires 1 _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_ per day from the Homestead's [[Kitchen]] stock.

During [[The Siphon]], each [[Homestead Colonist|Colonist]] requires 1 additional _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_ per day due to the increased demands of the season.

At the end of each [[Planetary Day]], the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] consumes the required _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_ for each [[Homestead Colonist|Colonist]]. If the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] cannot provide the required _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_, it gains 1 [[Wear]] for that day.

_[[Consumable|Consumables]]_ produced by the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] or supplied by the [[Expedition]] may be used to meet this requirement.
