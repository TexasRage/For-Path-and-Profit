Infrastructure represents the ongoing wear placed on a [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] by its environment, inhabitants, and daily operations. If left unattended, accumulated [[Wear]] can eventually cause [[Homestead Failure|Homestead Failures]].

## Wear

[[Wear]] accumulates from several sources, including severe weather, attacks, inadequate shelter, insufficient [[Consumable|Consumables]], overpopulation, and normal wear and tear.

## Maintenance

Accumulated [[Wear]] can be reduced through regular maintenance.

[[Performing Maintenance]] — Rules for reducing [[Wear]] and resolving the results of a maintenance check.

## Homestead Failure

As Wear accumulates, the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] becomes increasingly unstable.

[[Homestead Failure]] — Rules for determining when accumulated [[Wear]] causes infrastructure failures and resolving those failures.
