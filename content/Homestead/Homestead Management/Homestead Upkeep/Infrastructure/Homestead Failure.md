As [[Wear]] accumulates, the Homestead's infrastructure becomes increasingly unstable. Rather than immediately causing individual systems to fail, accumulated Wear increases the Homestead's [[Homestead Obligation]], representing the growing financial and logistical burden of keeping the settlement operational.

## Homestead Obligation

The Homestead gains Obligation as its Wear reaches increasingly severe levels.

- **5 Wear:** +1 Homestead Obligation
- **10 Wear:** +1 Homestead Obligation
- **15 Wear:** +1 Homestead Obligation
- **20 Wear:** +1 Homestead Obligation
- **21+ Wear:** +1 Homestead Obligation for each additional point of Wear

Each threshold represents an increasing level of infrastructure deterioration and the resulting burden placed on the settlement.

When [[Wear]] is reduced below a threshold, the corresponding Homestead Obligation is removed.

For example, a Homestead with 22 Wear has **7 Homestead Obligation** from Wear. If Wear is reduced to 20, the Obligation associated with the 21st and 22nd points of Wear is removed, leaving **5 Homestead Obligation**.

Homestead Obligation generated by Wear is **not permanent** and may be removed as the Homestead's condition improves.
