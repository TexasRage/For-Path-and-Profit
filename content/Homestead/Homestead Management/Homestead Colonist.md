Colonists are the settlers and support personnel who inhabit and operate the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]]. They provide the labor required to maintain the settlement and support its facilities.

The [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] begins with 1 Colonist per player character. Additional Colonists may be brought to [[Mycorris]] as the settlement develops.

Each Colonist requires adequate [[Sustenance]], [[Life Support]], and [[Shelter]].

## Population

The [[Basic Homestead|Homestead's]] total population determines its ongoing personnel requirements.

The [[Basic Homestead|Homestead's]] population must be supported by its [[Basic Power Generator|Population Rating]] and available [[Shelter]]. See [[Population Rating]] and [[Shelter]] for the consequences of exceeding either capacity.

## Labor

Colonists perform routine labor around the settlement, including maintaining facilities, gathering resources, processing supplies, and supporting the [[Expedition]].

Unless a facility specifically states otherwise, Colonists do not need to be individually assigned to facilities or tracked as separate workers. Their labor is abstracted into the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead's]] normal operations.
