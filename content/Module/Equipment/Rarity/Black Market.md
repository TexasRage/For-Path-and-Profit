Not all goods are legally available on every world. Governments, corporations, and local authorities may restrict or prohibit certain equipment, particularly weapons, explosives, and other dangerous or controlled goods.

Items marked **(R)** are Restricted and always count as Black Market goods, regardless of where they are being acquired.

## Acquiring Black Market Goods

Black Market goods require a **Streetwise check** instead of the normal **Negotiation check**.

The check uses the item's [[Rarity]] to determine its difficulty. Apply any appropriate Rarity modifiers based on the location, then apply additional modifiers based on local law enforcement, planetary regulations, and the availability of criminal contacts.

Purchasing Black Market goods requires a [[Quarter-Day]], representing the time spent locating a seller and arranging the transaction.

The results of the check are resolved as described in [[Acquiring Equipment]], with the following additional risks:

- \[threat]\[threat]\[threat]: The transaction goes badly. The [[Expedition]] is either discovered by law enforcement or double-crossed by the seller.
- \[despair]: The transaction goes badly. The [[Expedition]] is either discovered by law enforcement or double-crossed by the seller.

The GM determines the specific consequence based on the circumstances of the transaction.

## Restricted Goods

Restricted goods remain illegal or controlled regardless of how they are acquired. Possession, transport, or use of a Restricted item may attract attention from local authorities or other interested parties.

The GM determines the consequences based on the laws and circumstances of the current location.

## Black Market Contacts

Characters may establish relationships with Black Market sellers and criminal contacts.

A \[Triumph] when acquiring a Black Market item may establish a lasting contact, allowing the character to use that seller for future acquisitions. The GM may grant additional benefits or complications based on the nature of the relationship.
