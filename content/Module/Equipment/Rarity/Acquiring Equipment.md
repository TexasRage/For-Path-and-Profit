To acquire an item, a character must locate a seller and make an acquisition check. The check represents finding the item, negotiating with the seller, and completing the purchase.

Purchasing an item requires a [[Quarter-Day]]. This represents the time spent searching for the item or finding the best available deal.

The standard acquisition check is a **Negotiation check**. The GM may instead call for an appropriate **Knowledge check** when the character is attempting to locate a specialized or obscure item.

The difficulty of the check is determined by the item's [[Rarity]]. Every odd Rarity value also adds \[setback] to the check.

## Rarity

[[Rarity]] is a numerical value from 0–10 representing how difficult an item is to locate.

## Rarity Above 10

[[Rarity]] modifiers can increase an item's effective [[Rarity]] above 10. Once an item's effective [[Rarity]] exceeds 10, the acquisition check becomes Impossible.

Instead of increasing the difficulty further, each point of [[Rarity]] above 10 upgrades the difficulty once.

## Rarity Modifiers

The GM may modify an item's [[Rarity]] based on the location where it is being acquired.

###### TABLE 6-2: RARITY MODIFIERS

|**Rarity Modifier**|**Location**|
|---|---|
|-2|Primary Core World|
|-1|Other Core World|
|-1|World on primary trade lane|
|+0|Colony or Inner Rim world|
|+0|Civilized world|
|+1|Mid-Rim world|
|+1|Recently settled or out-of-the-way world|
|+2|Outer Rim world|
|+2|Frontier world|
|+3|Wild Space world|
|+4|Uncivilized world|

The GM may apply additional modifiers when local conditions, supply chains, scarcity, or other circumstances affect availability.

## Purchasing

If the acquisition check succeeds, resolve the results as follows:

- \[success]: Each net \[success] represents one copy of the item the character is able to locate and purchase.
- \[Advantage]: Reduce the final price by 5% per \[advantage], to a maximum reduction of 25%.
- \[Threat]: Increase the final price by 5% per \[threat], to a maximum increase of 25%.
- \[Triumph]: The character establishes a meaningful connection with the seller, granting **\[boost]** on a future acquisition check made with that same contact.
- \[Despair]: Introduce a narrative complication related to the acquisition, such as attracting unwanted attention, dealing with an unreliable seller, discovering a counterfeit item, or creating a new problem for the character.

A failed check means the character is unable to locate or acquire the item at that time. The GM may allow the character to try again after a reasonable change in circumstances, such as traveling to another settlement or finding a new seller.
