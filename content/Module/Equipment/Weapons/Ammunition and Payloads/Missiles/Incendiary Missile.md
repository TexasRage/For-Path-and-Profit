Incendiary missiles are devastating anti-personnel weapons filled with volatile chemical gel that ignites on exposure to air or upon activation of a small internal charge. The resulting fire spreads rapidly and clings to surfaces, burning at extreme temperatures capable of consuming infantry formations and igniting large areas of terrain.
