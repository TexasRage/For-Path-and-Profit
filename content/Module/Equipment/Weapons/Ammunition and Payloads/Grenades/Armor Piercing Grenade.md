Armor-piercing grenades are designed to defeat armored vehicles, fortified positions, and hardened targets. Their shaped charge focuses the explosive force into a narrow jet capable of penetrating thick armor, making them less effective against unarmored personnel. Some models incorporate magnetic or adhesive compounds, allowing them to attach securely to vehicles or structures for demolition and sabotage.
