Plasma grenades use modified blaster technology to generate a contained sphere of blaster energy. When activated, an internal XCiter energizes pressurized blaster gas before triggering a controlled discharge, producing a focused energy burst rather than a conventional explosive shockwave.

The resulting blast has an energy signature similar to a concentrated blaster bolt, capable of cutting through armored targets and tightly packed formations. Their lack of a traditional concussive shockwave makes them especially useful in confined or urban environments where structural damage must be minimized.

**Models Include**: SoroSuub FlashFire Mk IX, Kirgo Blastworks NOVA40 Plasma Grenade.
