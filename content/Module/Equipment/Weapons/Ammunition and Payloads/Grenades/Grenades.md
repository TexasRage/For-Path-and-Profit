Grenades are compact explosive or specialized munitions designed to affect targets within a confined area. They are commonly used for breaching, suppressing enemies, and delivering specialized effects where conventional weapons are less effective.

Grenades can be configured to detonate on impact or with a timed fuse of up to three rounds after activation. Some models also feature pressure-release ("dead man's") fuses that detonate immediately when released.
