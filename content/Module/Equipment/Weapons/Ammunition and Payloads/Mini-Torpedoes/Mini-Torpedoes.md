Mini-torpedoes are compact, self-propelled explosive munitions designed for use in portable launchers and specialized weapon systems. Though smaller than standard torpedoes, they deliver a wide range of effects depending on their payload, from fragmentation and ion discharge to entangling or non-lethal suppression.

Versatile and highly adaptable, mini-torpedoes are used in both infantry support and anti-vehicle roles, making them a favored option for units that require heavy firepower in a compact form.

When a Mini-Torpedo is loaded, it replaces the launcher's **Damage**, **Critical rating**, **Range**, and **item qualities** with those listed in the Mini-Torpedo's profile. Regardless of the ammunition used, the launcher retains any qualities specifically noted as inherent to the launcher.
