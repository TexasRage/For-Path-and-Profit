Ion Rounds are designed to disrupt electronic systems rather than inflict maximum physical damage. Their projectiles contain a compact ionizing charge that releases a burst of charged particles on impact, interfering with circuitry, droids, sensors, and other electronic systems.

When loaded, Ion Rounds add the [[Ion]] quality to the weapon's profile.
