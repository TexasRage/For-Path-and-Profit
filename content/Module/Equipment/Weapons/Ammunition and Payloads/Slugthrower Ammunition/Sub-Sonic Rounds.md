Subsonic Rounds use reduced propellant charges to keep the projectile below the speed of sound. This eliminates the sharp crack produced by a supersonic projectile, making the weapon significantly quieter when fired. The reduced velocity comes at the cost of stopping power.

When loaded, Subsonic Rounds add the [[Suppress]] quality to the weapon's profile.
