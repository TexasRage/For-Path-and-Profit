Incendiary Shells replace a conventional scattergun load with a payload of highly flammable compounds designed to ignite on impact. The burning material spreads across the target and surrounding area, making these shells particularly effective for setting vegetation, exposed equipment, and other flammable materials alight.

When loaded, Incendiary Shells add the [[Burn]] quality to the weapon's profile and remove the weapon's [[Knockdown]] quality.
