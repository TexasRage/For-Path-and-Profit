Stun Shells are specialized scattergun ammunition designed to incapacitate targets without causing significant physical trauma. The shells use a combination of conductive materials and dispersal compounds to deliver a powerful electrical shock across the target, making them useful for law enforcement, security forces, and hunters who need to take targets alive.

When loaded, Stun Shells replace the weapon's normal Damage with [[Stun Damage]], add the [[Disorient]] quality, and remove the weapon's [[Vicious]] and [[Knockdown]] qualities.
