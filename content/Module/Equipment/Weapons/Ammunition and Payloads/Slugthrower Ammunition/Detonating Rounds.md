Detonating Rounds contain a compact explosive charge that detonates upon impact, converting a conventional slugthrower into a powerful source of explosive firepower. The rounds are particularly effective against clustered targets, light cover, and targets that would normally require dedicated explosive weapons.

The unstable nature of the ammunition makes it heavily regulated and difficult to acquire outside military or specialized markets.

When loaded, Detonating Rounds add the [[Blast]] and [[Knockdown]] qualities to the weapon's profile.
