Tracer Rounds contain a small pyrotechnic compound that burns brightly as the projectile travels, producing a visible streak that allows the shooter to observe the projectile's trajectory and adjust their aim. This makes them particularly useful when engaging targets in darkness or other conditions where the shooter's ability to see the target is limited.

The bright tracer effect can also make the shooter's position easier to identify, making these rounds a useful tool for both precision fire and battlefield coordination.

When loaded, Tracer Rounds gain the [[Tracer]] quality.
