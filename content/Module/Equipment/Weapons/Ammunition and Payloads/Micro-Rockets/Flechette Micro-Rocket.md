Flechette micro-rockets detonate just before impact, dispersing a cloud of razor-edged metal shards over a wide area. While they lack the armor penetration of explosive or shaped-charge warheads, they are highly effective at inflicting severe injuries across multiple targets.

They are often favored for hunting large or heavily resilient creatures, where sustained volleys can wear down even the toughest prey.
