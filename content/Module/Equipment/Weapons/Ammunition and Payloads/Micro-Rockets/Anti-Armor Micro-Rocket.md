When faced with heavily protected targets or light vehicles, anti-armor micro-rocket warheads use shaped explosive charges to concentrate their force into a focused penetrative blast. This allows them to defeat defenses that would normally resist conventional explosives.

While not sufficient to threaten heavily armored war machines such as walkers or heavy armored transports, anti-armor micro-rockets are highly effective against war droids, speeders, combat swoops, and armored personnel or bodyguards.
