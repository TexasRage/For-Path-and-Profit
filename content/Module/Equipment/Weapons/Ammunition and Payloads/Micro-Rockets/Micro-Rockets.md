Micro-rockets are compact missile systems used by bounty hunters, mercenaries, and specialist infantry who need heavy firepower in a portable form. Often mounted on armor or integrated into wrist launchers, they deliver a wide range of specialized warheads tailored to different combat roles.

Micro-rockets are loaded individually and may vary in payload and effect. When a Micro-Rocket is loaded into a compatible launcher, it replaces the launcher’s Damage, Critical rating, and item qualities with those listed in the Micro-Rocket's profile.

Micro-rockets are typically fired using a dedicated launcher or mounting system. In emergencies, they may be fired without a launcher, though this is extremely dangerous. When doing so, the character makes the attack using Mechanics instead of Ranged (Heavy), and the difficulty of the check is upgraded once.
