Ion micro-rockets are specialized warheads designed to disable electronic systems rather than cause physical destruction. While bounties on droids are rare, they are often highly valuable, making ion warheads a worthwhile investment for hunters tasked with capturing or disabling such targets.

Upon detonation, the ion pulse can overload and temporarily disable droids and electronic systems. Against unshielded equipment, it may burn out capacitors, disrupt power cells, or damage sensitive circuitry.

The Sunder quality of an ion micro-rocket may only be applied to powered equipment such as sensors, blasters, or vibro-weapons. It cannot be used to damage non-powered items, as they lack circuitry to disrupt.
