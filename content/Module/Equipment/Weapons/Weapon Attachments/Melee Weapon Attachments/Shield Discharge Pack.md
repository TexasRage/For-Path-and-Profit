Despite being nearly as ancient as warfare itself, shields remain popular throughout the galaxy. They serve as trusted defenses for warriors on low-tech worlds and provide valuable protection for security forces operating in densely populated areas, where traditional armor may be impractical or insufficient.

This attachment can only be applied to [[Shield|Shields]].

**Base Modifiers**: When the wielder suffers a hit from a **Melee, Brawl, or Lightsaber combat check**, after the attack is resolved, the character may spend \[threat]\[threat] results or \[despair] result to cause the attacker to suffer 3 Strain (this ignores soak).

**Modification Options**: 1x Innate Talent ([[Defensive Stance]]) Mod, 2x Item Quality ([[Disorient]] +1) Mods.
