As deadly and dangerous as they are, vibro weapons can be made even deadlier with the inclusion of an overclock setting. By temporarily increasing the speed and power of the vibro-motor, the weapon delivers devastating strikes at the cost of additional wear on the weapon's components.

This attachment can be applied to any vibro weapon or any other powered Melee or Brawl weapon.

**Base Modifiers**: Once per turn as a maneuver, the wielder may activate the overclock setting to grant the weapon [[Vicious]] 3 (or increase the value of its [[Vicious]] quality by +3) until the end of the next round. If the wielder generates \[threat]\[threat]\[threat] or \[despair] on a combat check with the weapon before the end of the next round, the weapon overheats or shorts out and ceases to function entirely until the end of the encounter.

**Modification Options**: 1x Add an automatic \[advantage] to combat checks with this weapon Mod, 1x Activate attachment as an incidental Mod.

**Models Include**: SoroSuub Hyperblade.
