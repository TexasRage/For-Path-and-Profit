Shock pulse emitters operate on principles similar to a blaster's Stun setting, but at a much shorter effective range—so short that they are of little use as standalone weapons. Instead, they are typically integrated into armor, shields, or other close-quarters equipment, allowing the wearer to incapacitate opponents who venture too close.

This attachment to any bludgeoning Melee or Brawl weapon.

**Base Modifiers**: Grants the weapon the [[Stun]] (+2) quality.

**Modification Options**: 1x Item Quality ([[Disorient]] +1) Mod, 2x Item Quality ([[Stun]] +1) Mods.

**Models Include**: Corellian Personal Defense Stun Pad.
