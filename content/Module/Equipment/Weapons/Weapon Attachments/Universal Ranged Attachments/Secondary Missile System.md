Arms manufacturers are constantly developing new weapon systems to provide soldiers and operators with greater firepower without requiring additional equipment. Secondary Missile Systems mount compact launchers beneath or alongside rifles, allowing users to deploy specialized munitions while keeping their primary weapon ready.

Secondary Missile Systems can be applied to any rifle that uses the Ranged (Heavy) skill.

**Base Modifiers**: Enables the weapon to fire rockets or missiles. This uses the missile's profile (including Range) and requires the Gunnery skill to use. Adds [[Cumbersome]] (+2) quality to the weapon and increases encumbrance by +3. Missiles have [[Limited Ammo]] 1, so the launcher must be reloaded.

**Modification Options**: None.
