By removing or deactivating weapon safeties, firing interlocks, and other protective mechanisms, a weapon can be brought into action more quickly. While this modification is favored by some combatants who value speed above all else, the lack of safeguards increases the risk of accidental discharge or improper handling.

This attachment can be applied to any Ranged (Light) or Ranged (Heavy) weapon that could logically have such features removed.

**Base Modifiers**: Adds \[boost] to the first combat check made with the weapon during an encounter. Whenever the character suffers an [[Ammunition and Payloads|Out of Ammunition]] condition or the weapon suffers 1 step of [[Weapon Damage|Damage]], the wielder additionally suffers 4 [[Strain]].

**Modification Options**: None.
