Many pistols are designed to cycle and fire as quickly as the trigger can be depressed. Hair Trigger modifications reduce trigger pull and shorten the weapon's reset, allowing experienced shooters to fire rapid, accurate follow-up shots with minimal effort. Favored by duelists, gunslingers, and professional marksmen, these modifications improve a pistol's responsiveness without the complexity of a fully automatic firing mechanism.

This attachment can only be applied to Ranged (Light) pistols that do not have the [[Auto-Fire]] quality.

**Base Modifiers**: Grants the pistol the [[Linked]] 1 and [[Inaccurate]] 2 item qualities.

**Modification Options**: 1x Item Quality ([[Inaccurate]] –1) Mod.

**Models Include**: Morellian MWC-HS Hyper Trigger, BlasTech Industries 009 Fastshot.
