Motion Trackers integrate advanced sensors, predictive targeting software, and onboard processors to analyze and anticipate a target's movement. The system continuously feeds tracking data to the wielder through a helmet display, heads-up display, neural interface, or other compatible systems, allowing for more effective engagement of moving targets.

Motion Trackers can be applied to any Ranged (Heavy) weapon.

**Base Modifiers**: Remove \[setback] due to the target's movement or use of the [[Guarded Stance]] maneuver.

**Modification Options**: 1x Innate Talent ([[Precise Aim]]) Mod.

**Models Include**: CryonCorp Xv3 "Surefire" Motion Predictor, Industrial Automaton A-3 Tracking Computer.
