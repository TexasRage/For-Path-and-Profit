The varied and often hazardous environments found throughout the galaxy can hinder and damage even the most reliable equipment. Environmental Adaptation Kits consist of protective seals, reinforced components, filtration systems, and specialized coatings designed to help weapons operate in extreme conditions. These modifications are commonly used by explorers, scouts, soldiers, and frontier operators who must rely on their equipment in unpredictable environments.

This attachment can be applied to any ranged weapon.

**Base Modifiers:** Prevents a weapon from breaking down as a result of a specific type of environment.

**Modification Options:** None.
