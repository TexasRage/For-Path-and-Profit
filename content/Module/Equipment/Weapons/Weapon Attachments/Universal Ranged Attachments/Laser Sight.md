A Laser Sight projects a low-power beam that marks a weapon's point of impact with a visible dot. Favored by soldiers, security personnel, and law enforcement, Laser Sights improve target acquisition during fast-paced engagements and low-light conditions, allowing shooters to engage targets without relying solely on traditional sights.

Laser Sights can be applied to any Ranged (Light) or Ranged (Heavy) weapon that does not possess the [[Blast]] quality.

**Base Modifiers**: Gain an automatic \[advantage] on successful combat checks with this weapon.

**Modification Options**: None.
