Designed to allow a single operator to carry and deploy even the heaviest portable weapons, Weapon Harnesses distribute a weapon's weight across a reinforced support frame. Common among heavy weapons teams, these systems use mechanical supports, servos, and repulsorlift assistance to reduce fatigue and allow greater mobility while operating large weapons.

Weapon Harnesses can only be applied to Ranged (Heavy) and Gunnery weapons.

**Base Modifiers**: Decrease the weapon's [[Cumbersome]] rating by 2.

**Modification Options**: 1 Innate Talent ([[Brace]]) Mod.

**Models Include**: Arakyd WW480 "Goliath" Weapons Harness, Galactic Arms HR12 Power Harness, SoroSuub "Little Giant" Heavy Weapon Power Assist.
