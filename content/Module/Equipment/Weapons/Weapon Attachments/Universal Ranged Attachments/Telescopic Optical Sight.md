Popular among snipers, hunters, and marksmen, telescopic sights use magnified optics to provide a clearer view of distant targets. Consisting of a long optical tube with precision lenses mounted above the weapon, these sights allow users to engage targets at extended ranges with greater accuracy.

This attachment can be applied to any ranged weapon that could logically benefit from the addition of a sight.

**Base Modifiers**: Reduces the difficulty of ranged combat checks at long and extreme range by one.

**Modification Options**: None.

**Models Include**: Zikon Optics Model 40 Marksman Scope, Merr-Sonn 80Z Telescopic Optical Sight.
