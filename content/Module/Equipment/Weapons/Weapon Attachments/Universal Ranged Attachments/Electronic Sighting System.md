By adding a compact electronic projector to the top of a ranged weapon, a small holographic reticle is projected a short distance beyond the weapon's barrel. This provides the wielder with an intuitive aiming reference, improving target acquisition and shot placement without obstructing the user's view.

This attachment can be applied to any ranged weapon that could logically benefit from the addition of a sight.

A weapon can benefit from only one Sight attachment at a time. A Sight can be installed or removed from a weapon as a Maneuver

**Base Modifiers**: Allows the shooter to aim as an incidental once per round at short range. Decrease the difficulty of **Perception checks** to locate the shooter in low light conditions by 1.

**Modification Options**: 1x Item Quality ([[Accurate]] +1) Mod.

**Models Include**: SoroSuub True-Site System.
