Night Vision Scopes use advanced optical systems similar to electrobinoculars to enhance infrared wavelengths and reveal heat signatures invisible to the naked eye. These scopes allow users to identify targets in darkness, track warm bodies through concealment, and operate effectively in low-visibility environments.

Night Vision Scopes can be applied to any ranged weapon that could logically benefit from a scope, excluding single-use weapons.

**Base Modifiers**: Remove up to \[setback]\[setback] on any checks to use this weapon due to [[Brightness|Darkness]].

**Modification Options**: None.

**Models Include**: Fabritech LRS Low-Light Scope, Zikon Optics Model 45 Infrascope.
