Weapon Slings are lengths of reinforced hide, fabric, or synthetic material designed to improve the handling and transport of long arms such as blaster rifles. By allowing a weapon to be secured against the body while keeping the operator's hands free, slings are common among soldiers, hunters, scouts, and anyone who regularly carries a long weapon.

Weapon Slings can only be applied to Ranged (Heavy) weapons.

**Base Modifiers**: Decrease [[Cumbersome]] rating by 1.

**Modification Options**: 1 Innate Talent ([[Quick Draw]]) Mod.

**Models Include**: Custom-Tooled Bantha Hide Sling, Tactical Three-Point Sling, Quick-Release One-Point Drop Sling.
