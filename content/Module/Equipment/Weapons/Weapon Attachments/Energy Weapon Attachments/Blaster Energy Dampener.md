Blasters are among the most powerful personal ranged weapons available, but their brilliant muzzle flash and highly visible energy bolts make them difficult to use discreetly. Energy dampeners reduce the intensity of a blaster's discharge by dispersing excess energy and suppressing the weapon's visual signature, making shots significantly less conspicuous at the cost of some performance.

This attachment can only be applied to Ranged (Light) and Ranged (Heavy) blaster weapons.

**Base Modifiers**: Increase the difficulty of checks made to detect this weapon's fire by 1. Decrease weapon damage by 1.

**Modification Options**: 1x Add \[setback] to checks made to detect this weapon's fire Mod.

**Models Include**: Czerka B78 Energy Dampener.
