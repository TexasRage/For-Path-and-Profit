While some weaponsmiths focus on extracting more power from a blaster's energy cell, others seek to increase the energy delivered with each shot. Overcharged actuating modules enhance the weapon's firing sequence, allowing more energy to be transferred into each blaster bolt before discharge. The result is a more powerful shot, though the increased output places additional strain on the weapon's internal systems.

This attachment can be applied to any blaster pistol or blaster carbine.

**Base Modifiers**: Increases weapon damage by +1. The GM may spend \[threat]\[threat] results from any combat check made with this weapon to have it become damaged one step.

**Modification Options**: 1x Damage +1 Mod.

**Models Include**: BlasTech Mk. II Actuating Module, SoroSuub BlastPro.
