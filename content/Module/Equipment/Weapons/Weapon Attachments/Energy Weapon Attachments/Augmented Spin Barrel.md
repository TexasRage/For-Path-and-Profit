An Augmented Spin Barrel incorporates a series of magnetic induction coils and enhanced spin vanes that impart greater rotational stability to a blaster bolt as it travels through the barrel. Favored by marksmen and professional soldiers, this modification improves bolt cohesion and consistency, producing more accurate shots at the expense of increased power demands and mechanical complexity.

Augmented Spin Barrels can only be applied to Blaster Rifles and Heavy Blaster Rifles.

**Base Modifiers**: Increase weapon damage by 1. Adds \[setback] to all **Mechanics checks** when performing maintenance on this weapon.

**Modification Options**: 2x Damage +1 Mods, 1x Item Quality ([[Accurate]] +1) Mod, 1x Item Quality ([[Pierce]] +1) Mod.

**Models Include**: Dolmax 242X "Penetrator" Augmented Spin Barrel, Arakyd HH780 HotShot Barrel, BlasTech Model 213 Perforator Barrel, Dolmax 243X "Penetrator" Augmented Spin Barrel.
