By altering a blaster's Galven pattern, armorers can reshape and compress a plasma bolt to improve its penetration against hardened targets. While the modified energy profile sacrifices some stability and reliability, it delivers a more concentrated impact capable of punching through armor that would normally disperse a standard blaster bolt.

This attachment can only be applied to blaster pistols and heavy blaster pistols.

**Base Modifiers**: Increases weapon damage by 1. Adds automatic \[threat]\[threat] results to all combat checks made using this weapon.

**Modification Options**: 2x Damage +1 Mods, 2x Item Quality ([[Pierce]] +1) Mods.
