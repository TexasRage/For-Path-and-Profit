Truncheons are simple blunt weapons made from materials such as metal, wood, bone, or reinforced composites. Though basic in design, they are effective tools for controlling opponents through impact trauma, capable of bruising flesh, breaking bones, and inflicting concussive injuries.

Commonly used by law enforcement and security personnel, Truncheons are favored for riot control and the restraint of non-lethal targets. In many jurisdictions, they are preferred over more dangerous weapons as a means of maintaining order.
