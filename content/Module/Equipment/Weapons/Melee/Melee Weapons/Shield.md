Shields are handheld defensive weapons used to block attacks, control space, and protect their wielder in close-quarters combat. Worn on the forearm or gripped in one hand, they provide significant frontal protection while also serving as effective tools for shoving, striking, and creating openings against an opponent. They are commonly employed by law enforcement, military units, and security personnel during riot control, boarding actions, and other confined engagements.

Shields may only be used while wielding a one-handed weapon. Some models incorporate an integrated mag-lock or mounting system that allows a Ranged (Heavy) weapon to be secured to the shield, enabling it to be fired one-handed. Shields equipped with this feature cost an additional 100 credits, provide no Hard Points, and add \[setback] to combat checks made with both the shield and the mounted weapon.
