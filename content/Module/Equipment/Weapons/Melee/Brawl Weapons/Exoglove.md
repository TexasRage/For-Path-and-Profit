Exogloves are strength-enhancing wearables commonly found around spaceports on backwater worlds, produced by the Mephilis Corporation. Each glove contains concealed grappling hooks in the fingertips, allowing workers to move cargo, climb surfaces, and perform other tasks that require additional strength. In an emergency, they can also serve as effective weapons.

Wearing one or more Exogloves increases a character's Brawn by 1.

As an action, a character may make an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Ranged (Light) check** to secure the Exogloves’ grappling hook to an object within Medium Range. If successful, as a maneuver on their next turn, they may reel in the cord, pulling themselves to the object. Alternatively, if the object is unsecured and lighter than they are, they may pull it toward them.
