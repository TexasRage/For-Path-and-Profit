Blaster Rifles are among the standard firearms carried by soldiers and heavy security forces throughout the galaxy. Their longer barrels and robust power systems provide greater range, improved accuracy, and stronger, more consistent bolts than Blaster Pistols. Many models feature modular designs, including folding stocks, adjustable grips, and accessory rails, allowing them to be adapted for a variety of combat roles.

Blaster Rifles excel in sustained engagements at medium and long distances, making them well suited to open battlefields, military patrols, and defensive positions.
