Light Repeating Blasters serve as support weapons for military and mercenary units. Designed for sustained rapid fire, they excel at suppressing enemy positions and overwhelming unarmored or lightly armored targets through volume of fire rather than precision.

The constant stream of blaster bolts is often enough to force opponents into cover, disrupting movement and breaking coordinated advances before they can close distance.

**Models Include**: BlasTech T-21, Merr-Sonn M-40 "Thunderbolt".
