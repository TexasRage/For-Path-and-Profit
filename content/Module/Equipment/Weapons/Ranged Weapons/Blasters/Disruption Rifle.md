Disruptor Rifles are specialized marksman's weapons that use reinforced focusing assemblies to stabilize the destructive particle stream of conventional disruptor technology. Like all Disruptor Weapons, they are illegal throughout most civilized systems due to their extreme lethality.

Their reinforced focusing systems allow Disruptor Rifles to maintain the devastating power of a disruptor weapon while extending its effective range, making them particularly feared in the hands of trained marksmen and sharpshooters.

Whenever a target suffers a Critical Injury from a Disruptor Rifle, the result is treated as Crippled unless the rolled result would inflict a more severe injury.

Disruptor Rifles cannot benefit from attachments that increase Range or grant additional firing modes.

**Models Include**: Tenloss DXR-6 Disruptor Rifle.
