Projectile Weapons are among the oldest tools of war and hunting, relying on the user's strength and skill rather than complex technology.

While limited in range compared to modern firearms, they are inexpensive, reliable, and easy to maintain, making them popular among hunters, survivalists, and warriors across the galaxy.
