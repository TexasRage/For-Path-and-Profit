A larger and more powerful variant of the Slugthrower Pistol, Slugthrower Rifles use chemical propellant to launch solid slugs at high velocity toward distant targets. Their longer barrels and improved construction allow for greater range and impact force, though they generally lack the versatility and armor penetration of most energy-based weapons. Ammunition is often specialized, requiring either careful handcrafting or reliable supply chains, making sustained use a logistical concern in the field.

Despite these limitations, Slugthrower Rifles remain common among primitive, nomadic, and frontier cultures. In regions where blaster technology is rare or difficult to maintain, these weapons offer a dependable alternative for hunting and warfare. Some groups, such as the Tusken Raiders of Tatooine, are known to employ crude or improvised variants, occasionally supplementing their supply through scavenging or raiding.

**Models Include**: Dresselian Projectile Rifle, Czerka Arms 6-2Aug2 Hunting Rifle.
