Short, compact, and loud, Slugthrower Pistols are among the simplest and least common firearms in the galaxy. They propel physical slugs using chemical propellant rather than energy discharge, producing a sharp report and visible muzzle flash when fired. While generally outclassed by Blaster Pistols in most environments, they remain valued for their reliability, ease of maintenance, and independence from power cells or blaster gas.

Most users carry these weapons due to personal tradition, cultural preference, or necessity rather than widespread availability. They are often heirlooms, improvised sidearms, or locally manufactured weapons found in low-technology regions where energy weapons are scarce.
