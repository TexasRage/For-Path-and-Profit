Personal Deflector Shields are rare, expensive, and sophisticated defensive systems capable of absorbing sustained energy fire. Unlike most personal protective equipment, a deflector shield surrounds the wearer with an energy field that disperses incoming attacks before they can reach the armor beneath.

While a Personal Deflector Shield is active, the GM may spend any \[despair] generated by the wearer to cause the shield to run out of power. The shield provides no further benefits for the remainder of the encounter.
