Energy Shields are portable defensive systems that project an energy barrier around the wearer, protecting them from incoming attacks without relying on physical plating or reinforced armor construction.

Unlike standard armor, an Energy Shield may be equipped alongside **Light, Medium, or Heavy Armor**, functioning as an additional defensive layer rather than replacing worn armor.

When equipped without armor, an Energy Shield reduces its Encumbrance by **3**. When equipped alongside armor, it instead reduces its Encumbrance by **2**, reflecting the additional strain and bulk of operating both systems simultaneously.
