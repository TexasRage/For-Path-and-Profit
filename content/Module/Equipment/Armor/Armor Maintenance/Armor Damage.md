Armor is built to withstand the rigors of combat and hazardous environments, but repeated impacts, severe conditions, and other stresses can eventually compromise its protective systems.

Armor primarily suffers Damage from [[Critical Injuries]] and environmental effects. The GM may also cause armor to suffer Damage when circumstances would reasonably damage or degrade it.

Each time armor suffers Damage, it advances one step along the Damage track, applying the effects of its new Damage level. If armor would suffer Damage beyond Major, it is instead Destroyed and cannot provide protection until repaired.

#### Damage Level

| Damage    | Difficulty                                  | Penalty for Use                    |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Minor     | Easy (\[difficulty])                         | Soak reduced by 1                  |
| Moderate  | Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty])          | [[Critical Injuries]] +10 severity |
| Major     | Hard (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) | [[Critical Injuries]] +20 severity |
| Destroyed | Beyond Repair                               | Beyond Repair                      |

These penalties are cumulative. For example, armor suffering Major Damage has its Soak reduced by 1 and increases the severity of Critical Injuries by 20.

## CRITICAL INJURIES

Critical Injuries are a primary source of armor Damage. When a Critical Injury causes the character's armor to suffer Damage, the armor advances one step along its Damage track.

Damaged armor also makes subsequent Critical Injuries more dangerous. The increased severity applies to any Critical Injury suffered while the armor remains at the corresponding Damage level.

## ENVIRONMENTAL DAMAGE

Hazardous environments can damage armor when its protective materials or systems are subjected to conditions beyond their intended limits.

The GM may cause armor to suffer Damage from environmental hazards such as extreme heat, extreme cold, corrosive substances, radiation, electrical discharges, or other conditions capable of degrading protective equipment.

[[Armor Repair]] describes how damaged armor can be restored.
