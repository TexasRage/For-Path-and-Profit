Laminate Armor consists of form-fitted plastoid plates designed to protect the body's most important areas, worn over a snug, reinforced armorweave body glove. The layered construction provides reliable protection while allowing the wearer to retain practical mobility in combat.

Laminate Armor includes an **integrated helmet** as part of the armor profile. The helmet is considered part of the armor for the purposes of attachments and modifications, but provides no additional effects or equipment unless otherwise specified.

Laminate Armor may be **Sealed Armor**, allowing it to protect the wearer from hazardous environments when properly equipped.
