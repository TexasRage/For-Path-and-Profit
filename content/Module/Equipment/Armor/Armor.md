Armor protects its wearer from the dangers encountered throughout the galaxy. From lightweight protective clothing to military-grade combat armor, different armor systems balance protection, mobility, and environmental resistance in different ways.

The armor presented in this chapter represents equipment commonly encountered by explorers, settlers, mercenaries, soldiers, and other inhabitants of the galaxy. Armor statistics can be found in the appropriate armor tables.

## Armor Maintenance

Armor Maintenance is a new mechanic that represents the ongoing care and upkeep required to keep armor in working condition. The rules for armor maintenance explain how armor is affected by use, damage, and other circumstances, as well as how it can be maintained and restored.

[[Armor Damage]] — Rules for armor damage and degradation.

[[Armor Repair]] — Rules for repairing damaged armor.

## Light Armor

Light Armor provides protection while preserving mobility and flexibility. It is commonly worn by explorers, scouts, travelers, and others who need protection without the bulk of heavier armor.

[[Light Armor]] — Light protective equipment and clothing.

## Heavy Armor

Heavy Armor provides substantial protection at the cost of mobility and increased Encumbrance. It is typically used by heavily armed soldiers, specialists, and personnel expecting sustained exposure to hostile environments or combat.

[[Heavy Armor]] — Heavy protective equipment.

## Energy Shields

Energy Shields use directed energy fields or similar technology to protect their wearer from incoming attacks. Unlike conventional armor, shields provide protection through an active defensive system rather than physical armor plating.

[[Energy Shields]] — Personal energy shielding systems.

## Armor Attachments

Armor can be customized with specialized equipment and modifications that enhance its protective capabilities or provide additional functions.

[[Armor Attachments]] — Rules and options for modifying armor.
