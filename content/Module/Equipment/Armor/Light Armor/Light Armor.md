Light Armor represents the first true step into combat-grade protection, built for users who expect to face direct threats while maintaining mobility and flexibility. This category primarily includes reinforced clothing, concealed armor, and lightweight protective vests, ranging from discreet protective wear to more visibly armored configurations.

Light Armor is commonly used by professionals who require adaptable protection without the bulk of heavier armor systems. Its low-profile construction makes it particularly well suited to scouts, explorers, security personnel, and others who need protection without drawing attention to their equipment.
