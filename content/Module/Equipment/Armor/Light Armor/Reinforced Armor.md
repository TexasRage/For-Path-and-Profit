Reinforced Armor is designed for personnel who expect to face violent confrontations, riots, and other situations where conventional protective clothing is insufficient. Heavy protective plating and reinforced materials provide reliable coverage against impacts and physical attacks while maintaining enough mobility for prolonged use.

Reinforced Armor includes an integrated helmet as part of its armor profile. The helmet is considered part of the armor for the purposes of attachments and modifications but provides no additional effects or equipment unless otherwise specified.
