Heavy Clothing offers little protection against direct attacks, but provides basic coverage and protection from the elements. Leather jackets, technician's jumpsuits, and thick wool cloaks are common examples.

In frontier regions and unsettled worlds, Heavy Clothing is common everyday wear, valued for its durability, comfort, and ability to withstand harsh environmental conditions rather than its protective capabilities.

**Models Include:** Aye-lixe Fabrico "Hard Wear" work clothes, Kamperdine Clothing Specialists ToughSuit adventuring gear.
