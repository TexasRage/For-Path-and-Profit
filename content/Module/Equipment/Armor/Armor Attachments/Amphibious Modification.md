For sentients without the evolutionary adaptations necessary to survive underwater, specialized equipment provides the ability to operate in aquatic environments. Amphibious Modifications integrate breathing apparatuses, sealed filtration systems, and compact air supplies into armor, allowing the wearer to function beneath the surface for extended periods.

This attachment can be applied to any armor that could logically accommodate an air tank or breathing apparatus.

**Base Modifiers**: The armor provides enough oxygen (or applicable gas) for up to five hours of submersion or in any airless environment (although it does not protect against vacuum). In addition, the user removes a setback die from physical checks due to underwater conditions.

**Modification Options**: 2x Increase oxygen supply by two hours Mods.

**Models Include**: Gandorthral Atmospherics Submersible 9, BioTech Mk. II Air Supply.
