Few suits of armor remain factory-standard for long. Armor is routinely modified, reinforced, and personalized to suit the wearer's needs, combat role, and environment. The attachments presented in this chapter represent some of the most common modifications available, but they are far from an exhaustive list. Players and Game Masters are encouraged to treat them as examples of the many ways armor can be customized throughout the galaxy.

Although Hard Points represent an armor's capacity for customization, physical space and compatibility still matter. Certain attachments can only be installed on specific types of armor, while others may require a particular configuration, such as a helmet, sealed suit, or powered armor. Some attachments may also be incompatible with one another or require additional equipment to function. When selecting attachments, players and Game Masters should use common sense and refer to the restrictions listed in each attachment's description.
