The way cargo is arranged within a [[Storage|crate or container]] determines how efficiently its available space can be used.

Characters may store cargo using **Loose Packing** or **Effective Packing**.

## Loose Packing

Loose Packing represents the standard method of filling a container. Items are placed without careful arrangement, prioritizing speed and accessibility.

A container using Loose Packing can hold cargo whose combined Encumbrance does not exceed its listed Encumbrance Capacity.

## Effective Packing

Effective Packing represents the careful organization, compression, and optimization of a container's contents.

An Effectively Packed container has **twice its listed Encumbrance Capacity**.

A character may also disassemble large or unwieldy items as part of the packing process. An item with an Encumbrance value up to twice the container's listed capacity may be stored in this manner, provided it is disassembled during packing.

For example, a weapon with 10 Encumbrance can be disassembled and stored in a container with an Encumbrance Capacity of 5.

Items stored in this manner are considered **Disassembled** and must be reassembled before they can be used.

Effectively Packed containers are less accessible than loosely packed containers. The GM may require additional time or appropriate equipment to access or remove items from an Effectively Packed container.
