A vehicle may be transported as cargo aboard another vehicle, vessel, or other suitable means of transportation. The space required to carry a vehicle is determined by its Silhouette.

##### Vehicle Encumbrance

|**Silhouette**|**Encumbrance**|
|---|--:|
|1|20|
|2|50|
|3|200|
|4|1,000|
|5|10,000|
|6|100,000|

At its listed Encumbrance, the carried vehicle is considered stored under **Cramped** conditions.

While stored under Cramped conditions:

- Launch time is doubled.
- Maintenance and repair checks performed aboard the carried vehicle suffer \[setback].

If a vehicle is allocated space equal to **twice its listed Encumbrance**, it is considered properly stored with adequate access. The vehicle may be launched normally, and maintenance and repair checks performed aboard it do not suffer the penalties associated with Cramped storage.

Space alone does not guarantee that a vehicle can be transported. The vehicle must still physically fit through access points such as doors, corridors, loading bays, and other passages.

Larger vehicles may require modified infrastructure or specialized equipment to be loaded, transported, or deployed.
