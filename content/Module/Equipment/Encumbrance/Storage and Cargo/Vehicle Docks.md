Vehicles may be stored and deployed from dedicated docking facilities. The type of dock determines how easily a vehicle can be accessed, maintained, and launched.

## Cargo Bays

Cargo Bays are general-purpose storage areas that are not designed specifically for vehicle operations.

Vehicles stored in a Cargo Bay are typically treated as being under **Cramped** conditions unless additional space is allocated.

Launching a vehicle from a Cargo Bay may take longer, and access to the vehicle may be limited by surrounding cargo.

## Hanger Bays

Hangar Bays are specialized facilities designed to accommodate vehicle storage, maintenance, and deployment. They provide adequate clearance, reinforced support, and suitable access for vehicle operations.

A vessel equipped with a **Ship Complement** or **Retrofitted Hangar Bay** is considered to have a Hangar Bay for the purposes of vehicle storage and launch.

Vehicles deployed from a Hangar Bay reduce their launch time by **2 rounds**, to a minimum of 1 round.

See [[Vehicle Launch]] for the rules governing vehicle deployment.
