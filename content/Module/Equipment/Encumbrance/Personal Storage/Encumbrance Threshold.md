A character's Encumbrance Threshold represents the amount of equipment they can comfortably carry without suffering penalties.

A character's Encumbrance Threshold is equal to 5 + their Brawn.

## Encumbered

When a character's total [[Encumbrance]] exceeds their Encumbrance Threshold, they become Encumbered.

An Encumbered character suffers \[setback] on all Agility- and Brawn-based checks for each point of [[Encumbrance]] above their Encumbrance Threshold.

This penalty is cumulative with other sources of \[setback].

## Overencumbered

A character becomes Overencumbered when their [[Encumbrance]] exceeds their Brawn by an amount equal to or greater than their Brawn.

An Overencumbered character loses their Free Maneuver each turn.

They may still perform up to two Maneuvers during their turn, but each Maneuver costs 2 Strain.

While Overencumbered, a character also suffers the effects described in [[Encumbrance]], including gaining 1 _[[Exhaustion]]_ for each [[Quarter-Day]] spent in this state.
