Encumbrance represents the weight, bulk, and general difficulty of transporting equipment and supplies. It is intentionally abstract rather than a precise measure of mass, allowing the game to track carrying capacity without requiring every item to be individually weighed.

Managing Encumbrance is an important part of extended expeditions. Characters must balance the equipment they carry with the supplies and resources needed to survive and operate in the field.
