The Main House serves as the central administrative building of the [[Basic Homestead]] and the primary base of operations for the [[Expedition]].

The building contains offices, common areas, and basic accommodations for the Expedition, allowing the party to maintain a permanent presence at the Homestead without occupying the settlement's [[Shelter|Living Quarters]].

The Main House also serves as the administrative center for the Homestead, providing a location for coordinating the settlement's operations and managing its facilities and [[Homestead Colonists]].

## CONSTRUCTION

The Main House is included with the [[Basic Homestead]] and does not require separate construction.
