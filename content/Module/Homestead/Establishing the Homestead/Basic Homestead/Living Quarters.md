Living Quarters provide permanent shelter for [[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]], protecting them from the environment and providing suitable living space within the Homestead.

Each Living Quarters provides 5 [[Shelter]].

Additional Living Quarters may be constructed as the Homestead expands, allowing it to accommodate additional Homestead Colonists.
