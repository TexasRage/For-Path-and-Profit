The Basic Power Generator provides the power and life support required to operate the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] and sustain its [[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]].

The Generator has a [[Population Rating]] that determines how many Homestead Colonists its life-support systems can support. Its fuel requirements are determined by [[Fuel Consumption]].

The Generator may be improved through [[Homestead Upgrades]], increasing its Population Rating and providing additional capabilities.
