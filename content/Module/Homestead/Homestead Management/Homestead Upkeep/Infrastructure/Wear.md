A Homestead's buildings, utilities, and infrastructure are constantly exposed to environmental conditions, daily use, and other sources of stress. Over time, this causes **Wear** to accumulate across the Homestead.

Wear represents the overall condition of the Homestead's infrastructure. If allowed to accumulate, it can eventually result in system failures.

## Sources of Wear

The Homestead gains Wear from the following sources:

- **Severe Weather:** At the end of each day, the Homestead gains Wear based on the most severe weather condition experienced that day.
  - **Heavy Rain/Thunderstorm:** +1 Wear
  - **Ion Storm/Blizzard:** +2 Wear
- **Homestead Attack:** The Homestead gains Wear based on the source of the attack.
  - **Animal Attack:** +1 Wear
  - **Native Attack:** +2 Wear
  - **Other Attack:** +3 Wear
- **Insufficient Shelter:** Each day personnel lack adequate shelter, the Homestead gains +1 Wear.
- **Insufficient Consumables:** Each day the Homestead cannot provide the required Consumables, it gains +1 Wear.
- **Overpopulation:** Each day the Homestead has more Colonists than its Generator's Population Rating, it gains +1 Wear for each Colonist above the rating.
- **Wear and Tear:** The Homestead gains +1 Wear at the end of each week.

The GM may assign additional Wear when circumstances place unusual stress or damage on the Homestead.
