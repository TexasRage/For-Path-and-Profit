A [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]] requires ongoing resources and upkeep to remain operational. As the settlement grows, its demands increase, requiring the [[Expedition]] to manage its [[Personnel Needs]], [[Life Support]], power, and [[Infrastructure]].

Homestead upkeep is divided into three areas:

[[Personnel Needs]] — The [[Sustenance]], life support, and [[Shelter]] required to support the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead's]] [[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]].

[[Basic Power Generator]] — Provides the power and life support required by the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead]]. Its [[Population Rating]] and [[Fuel Consumption]] determine its operating requirements.

[[Infrastructure]] — Represents the ongoing [[Wear]] placed on the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead's]] buildings, utilities, and infrastructure, along with the maintenance required to keep them functioning.

Failure to properly manage any of these areas can reduce the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead's]] effectiveness and eventually lead to system failures.
