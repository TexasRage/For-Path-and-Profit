The Homestead's [[Basic Power Generator]] provides the environmental systems required to keep [[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]] alive and allow them to inhabit the settlement safely.

The [[Population Rating|Generator's Population Rating]] determines how many [[Homestead Colonist|Colonists]] its life-support systems can comfortably sustain.

If the [[Basic Homestead|Homestead's]] population exceeds the [[Population Rating|Generator's Population Rating]], the [[Basic Homestead| Homestead]] gains 1 [[Wear]] per day for each [[Homestead Colonist|Colonist]] above the rating.

See [[Population Rating]] for the [[Basic Power Generator|Generator's]] capacity and [[Fuel Consumption]] for its ongoing power requirements.
