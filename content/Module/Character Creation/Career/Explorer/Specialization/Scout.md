Scouts specialize in going where others have not yet gone. They move ahead of the group, locate safe routes, identify potential hazards, and gather information before others follow. Scouts add **Athletics, Medicine, Perception, and Stealth** to their Career Skills. If this is the character’s starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain one free rank in each without spending starting experience.

A Scout may join the expedition because they want to be among the first to set foot on an unknown world. Their ability to operate independently makes them invaluable when the expedition needs someone to investigate unfamiliar territory, find a safe route, or determine what lies ahead.
