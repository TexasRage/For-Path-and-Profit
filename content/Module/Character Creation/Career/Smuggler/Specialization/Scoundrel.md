Scoundrels are quick-witted, adaptable, and always looking for an angle. Part con artist, part gunslinger, and part cantina brawler, they rely on charm, deception, luck, and a willingness to bend or ignore the rules whenever doing so benefits them. Some are grifters and middlemen, while others are thrill-seekers or opportunists who drift from one job to the next. Scoundrels rarely stay in one place for long, preferring the freedom to chase credits, excitement, and whatever opportunity comes their way.

A Scoundrel's additional Career skills are **Charm, Cool, Deception, and Ranged (Light)**. If Scoundrel is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills allow the Scoundrel to serve as a capable negotiator and underworld operator while still holding their own in a fight, making them a versatile member of almost any group.
