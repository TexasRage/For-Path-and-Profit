Slicers specialize in computers, security systems, communications networks, and digital information. They can bypass security, manipulate data, access restricted systems, and exploit weaknesses that less technically minded characters would never notice. A skilled Slicer can turn information itself into a weapon.

A Slicer's additional Career skills are **Computers, Deception, Perception, and Skulduggery**. If Slicer is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills make Slicers particularly effective at defeating electronic security, manipulating information, and gaining access to systems that were never intended to be opened.
