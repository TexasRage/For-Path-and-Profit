Gadgeteers specialize in high-tech equipment and weapons, combining technical expertise with combat ability to turn customized gear into a personal arsenal. A Gadgeteer adds **Brawl, Coercion, Mechanics, and Ranged (Light)** to their Career Skills. If Gadgeteer is their starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain 1 free rank in each. Their ability to modify and exploit equipment allows them to adapt to changing circumstances and overcome challenges through ingenuity.
