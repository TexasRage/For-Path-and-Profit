Skip Tracers specialize in finding people who do not want to be found. They rely on investigation, negotiation, and underworld contacts to locate quarry and recover what their clients are owed, preferably without resorting to violence. A Skip Tracer adds **Cool, Knowledge (Underworld), Negotiation, and Skulduggery** to their Career Skills. If Skip Tracer is their starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain 1 free rank in each. Through clues, contacts, and social pressure, Skip Tracers can track down even elusive targets, making them equally useful for locating people, information, or resources.
