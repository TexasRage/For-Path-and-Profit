Martial Artists turn their own bodies into weapons, relying on physical conditioning, precision, and disciplined technique rather than conventional arms. A Martial Artist adds **Athletics, Brawl, Coordination, and Discipline** to their Career Skills. If Martial Artist is their starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain 1 free rank in each. Their training emphasizes precision and control, allowing them to fight effectively without weapons while enduring the demands of close-quarters combat.
