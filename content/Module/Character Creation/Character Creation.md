Before creating a character, players should consider what kind of person they want to portray. The Star Wars galaxy is filled with countless possibilities: a daring pilot chasing the next great discovery, a hardened survivor determined to tame an unknown world, a skilled mechanic keeping vital equipment running, or a diplomat trying to unite people divided by circumstance.

The GM should provide players with an overview of the campaign and its expected challenges. A campaign focused on exploration and survival may benefit from characters with strong technical, survival, or scientific abilities. A campaign centered on conflict between factions may reward characters skilled in combat, leadership, or negotiation. Understanding the challenges ahead helps players create characters that fit naturally into the story.

Players are encouraged to discuss their character concepts with one another and consider how their characters fit together as a team. A successful expedition requires many different talents: someone who can navigate the wilderness, someone who can repair equipment, someone who can defend the group, and someone who can interact with the wider galaxy. While some overlap is expected, each character should bring their own unique perspective and abilities to the group.

However, there is no wrong choice when creating a character. Every character concept has a place in the Star Wars universe, and the GM should work with players to incorporate their ideas into the campaign. Some of the most memorable stories come from unlikely groups of individuals who must learn to rely on one another while facing challenges beyond their expectations.
