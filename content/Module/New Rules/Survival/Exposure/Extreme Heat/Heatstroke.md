_Heatstroke_ is a severe condition caused by prolonged exposure to extreme heat, particularly when a character cannot maintain adequate hydration.

A character suffering from _Heatstroke_ upgrades the difficulty of all Intellect, Cunning, and Presence checks once.

## Recovery

The next time the character participates in a [[Daily Needs]] check outside of [[Extreme Heat]], they recover from Heatstroke.
