Survival in the frontier depends on maintaining an adequate supply of food and water. Rather than tracking food and water separately, the game abstracts these necessities into _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_.

Each character must have access to _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_ to satisfy their [[Daily Needs]]. Characters who fail to do so enter _[[Deprivation]]_, which imposes increasingly severe consequences if it continues.

## Daily Needs

Once per day, each character makes a [[Daily Needs]] check to determine how effectively they ration their _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_.

The result of the check determines whether the character removes a unit of _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_, conserves their supplies, or gains a benefit from effective rationing.

See [[Daily Needs]] for the complete procedure.

### Deprivation

A character who goes a [[Planetary Day]] without consuming _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_ suffers _[[Deprivation]]_. Deprivation causes _[[Exhaustion]]_ and limits the character's ability to recover Strain.

See _[[Deprivation]]_ for the complete rules.

## Storage

Characters can carry _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_ in their inventory. A _[[Ration Box]]_ provides dedicated storage for these supplies and can safely hold up to 6 units.

See [[Ration Box]] for storage rules.
