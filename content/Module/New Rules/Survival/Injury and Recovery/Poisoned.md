Poison is an [[Injury and Recovery|Injury]] caused by exposure to a harmful substance. The effects of Poison depend on the substance involved.

A character suffering from Poison suffers \[setback] on all checks.

### Recovery

A character recovers from Poison using the normal [[Injury and Recovery|Injury recovery]] rules.
