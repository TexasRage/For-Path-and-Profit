A Quarter-Day represents approximately six hours of the 24-hour [[Planetary Day]] and is the primary unit of structured time during exploration. Rather than tracking individual hours, the [[Expedition]] uses Quarter-Days to determine how much time characters have available for travel, exploration, rest, and other activities.

Each [[Planetary Day]] consists of four Quarter-Days:

- **Morning** — approximately 6 AM–12 PM
- **Afternoon** — approximately 12 PM–6 PM
- **Evening** — approximately 6 PM–12 AM
- **Night** — approximately 12 AM–6 AM
