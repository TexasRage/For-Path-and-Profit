A Planetary Day on [[Mycorris]] lasts 24 hours and is divided into four [[Quarter-Day|Quarter-Days]]. Most structured exploration and survival gameplay takes place within these periods, allowing the [[Expedition]] to track the passage of time without requiring characters to account for individual hours.

### Quarter-Day

A [[Quarter-Day]] represents roughly six hours of activity and is the primary unit of time used during exploration. Each [[Quarter-Day]] provides an opportunity for characters to perform [[Expedition Action|Expedition Actions]], travel, gather resources, rest, and respond to changing environmental conditions.

The four [[Quarter-Day|Quarter-Days]] are Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Night. The current [[Quarter-Day]] determines the general time of day and can affect [[Brightness]] and other environmental conditions.

### Vision and Brightness

[[Vision]] and [[Brightness]] represent two separate aspects of visibility during exploration.

[[Vision]] describes how clearly characters can see through the environment. [[Weather]] and environmental conditions such as fog, dust, smoke, heavy rain, or snowfall can obscure vision and limit how far characters can see.

[[Brightness]] describes the amount of available light. The current [[Quarter-Day]] and [[Seasons|Season]] determine whether an area is brightly lit, dimly lit, or completely dark. Appropriate equipment can mitigate the effects of poor lighting.

Both [[Vision]] and [[Brightness]] can affect sight-based checks, [[Scan]], Stealth, and other activities where the ability to see clearly is important.
