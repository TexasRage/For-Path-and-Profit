Without proper shelter, resting on [[Mycorris]] is dangerous, though careful preparation allows the [[Expedition]] to establish a temporary campsite.

When a character performs the **Make Camp Action**, they spend a [[Quarter-Day]] locating and preparing a suitable site for the [[Expedition]] to rest. Other characters may assist by using the **[[Assist Maneuver]]**. The character makes a **Survival** or **Knowledge (Xenology)** check. The difficulty is determined by the [[Terrain|Explore difficulty]] of the terrain in the hex, as shown in the **[[Terrain]]** table.

If any character performing the **Make Camp Action** or assisting with the action has _[[Flame Paste]]_ or another means of controlled ignition, they may create a fire as part of this action. A fire established during this action may be used for **[[Cook|Cook Actions]]**.

On a success, the [[Expedition]] establishes a suitable campsite protected from the worst of the surrounding conditions. If the check generates \[success]\[success]\[success], the campsite is especially well concealed, difficult to approach, or provides a commanding view of the surrounding area. The **[[Scan|Scout]]** adds \[boost] to **[[Scan|Scan checks]]** made from this camp.

On a failure, a usable camp is still established, but the location is suboptimal. Roll on the **Making Camp Hazards** table.

If the check fails with \[failure]\[failure]\[failure] or generates \[despair], the camp automatically gains the **Bad Camp** result.

#### Making Camp Hazards

| d10 | Hazard                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| :-: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  | **Bad Camp**: The camp is poorly situated and extremely uncomfortable. Any character taking the **[[Rest\|Rest Action]]** must make a **Resilience check** with difficulty equal to the **[[Terrain\|Explore difficulty]]** of the current hex. On a successful check, the character gains the benefits of a [[Long Rest]]. On a failed check, the character’s rest is disrupted and they only gain the benefits of a [[Short Rest]], while also suffering the consequences of insufficient rest. |
|  2  | **Fire:** A blaze breaks out in the camp. Each [[Expedition]] member suffers 10 Wounds from the flames, which may be reduced by a successful **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Coordination check**. Each net success on the check reduces the Wounds suffered by 1. If a character fails this check, they also lose a random piece of equipment, which is destroyed in the fire.                                                                                                              |
|  3  | **Tainted Consumables**: Some of the [[Expedition\|Expedition’s]] supplies have become spoiled or contaminated. Each member of the [[Expedition]] must remove a _[[Consumable]]_ from their inventory, if available, or suffer the _[[Poisoned\|Poisoned Injury]]_.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|  4  | **Lost Supplies**: Poor packing, scavengers, weather, or simple carelessness causes some of the [[Expedition\|Expedition’s]] supplies to go missing. Each member of the [[Expedition]] must remove a _[[Consumable]]_ from their inventory, if available.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|  5  | **Exposed:** The [[Expedition\|Expedition’s]] camp is left vulnerable to the elements. Each [[Expedition]] member must succeed on an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Resilience check** or suffer an [[Injury and Recovery\|Injury]] based on the current season: _[[Heatstroke]]_ during [[The Siphon]], _[[Frostbite]]_ during [[The Anchor]], or _[[Poisoned]]_ during [[The Bloom]].                                                                                                     |
|  6  | **Predator Lair**: The [[Expedition]] unknowingly establishes camp within a creature’s territory or lair, and it returns while the camp is being set up. If any character used the **[[Scan\|Scan Action]]**, the [[Expedition]] may make an **opposed Vigilance vs. Stealth check** to avoid being _[[Surprised]]_. The GM selects a suitable creature for the encounter. Do not roll for a random encounter during this [[Quarter-Day]], as this replaces it.                                   |
