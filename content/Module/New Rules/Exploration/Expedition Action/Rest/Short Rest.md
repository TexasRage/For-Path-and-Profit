A **Short Rest** replaces the **Post-Encounter Strain Recovery** rules described in the standard Star Wars RPG rules. It gives a character time to recover Strain and collect their thoughts without requiring a full period of sleep.

You can take one [[Short Rest]] during each [[Quarter-Day]]. You can take a [[Short Rest]] in addition to any other [[Expedition Action]] you undertake during that [[Quarter-Day]].

When a character gains the benefits of a **Short Rest**, they make a **Simple Cool or Discipline check**.

The character recovers 1 Strain for each \[success] generated. Each \[advantage]\[advantage] may be spent to recover 1 additional Strain.

Alternatively, \[advantage]\[advantage] may be spent to remove \[setback] from the character's next check or add \[boost] to their next check.
