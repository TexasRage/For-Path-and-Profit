[[Mycorris]] is home to a vast array of creatures, from docile herbivores to dangerous predators. The [[Expedition]] may perform the **Hunt Action** to gather _[[Protein|Proteins]]_.

Characters may perform the **Hunt Action** independently, or multiple characters may work together by having additional characters use the **[[Assist Maneuver]]**.

When a character performs the **Hunt Action**, they spend a [[Quarter-Day]] searching for prey.

### Tracking Phase

The character makes a **Survival check** to locate prey. The difficulty is determined by the terrain of the hex being hunted, as shown in the **[[Terrain]]** table.

On a successful check, roll on the **Prey** table to determine the creature that has been tracked. If the check generates \[success]\[success]\[success], roll twice and choose either result. The GM may require a reroll if the selected prey is unsuitable for the terrain.

### Hunting Phase

The character must either set an _[[Electro-Snare]]_ or similar trap, or attack the prey with a suitable ranged weapon.

Setting a trap requires a **Survival check**. Alternatively, the character may make a combat check using an appropriate ranged weapon. The difficulty is determined by the prey, as shown in the **Prey** table.

On a successful check, the character harvests the listed amount of _[[Protein|Proteins]]_.

If the **Tracking Phase** check fails, no prey is found.

If the **Hunting Phase** check fails, the prey escapes and no _[[Protein|Proteins]]_ are harvested.

If either check fails with \[failure]\[failure]\[failure] or generates \[despair], the character suffers a **Hunting Hazard**.

#### Hunting Hazard

| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| 6   | **Prey**: The hunter becomes the hunted. While hunting, the character draws the attention of a suitable creature and is immediately attacked.                                                                                                              |

### Hunting the Hunter

The [[Expedition]] may encounter creatures not listed on the **Prey** table. These creatures may still be hunted for _[[Protein|Proteins]]_. The amount of _[[Protein|Proteins]]_ harvested is determined by the creature's Silhouette:

- **Silhouette 0:** 1 _[[Protein|Protein]]_
- **Silhouette 1:** 3 _[[Protein|Proteins]]_
- **Silhouette 2:** 4 _[[Protein|Proteins]]_
- **Silhouette 3:** 5 _[[Protein|Proteins]]_

Due to the unpredictable nature of meat from unlisted creatures, a character who consumes these _[[Protein|Proteins]]_ raw increases the difficulty of the Resilience check by 1.

If the _[[Protein|Proteins]]_ are first processed into _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_ through [[Cook|cooking]], this increased risk is avoided.
