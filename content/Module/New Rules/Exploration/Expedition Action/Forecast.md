A character may perform the **Forecast Action** to predict the weather for the following [[Planetary Day]].

The character makes a **Hard (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Survival, Knowledge (Education), or Perception check**.

On a success, the GM resolves the following day's [[Shifts|Weather Shift]] in advance and informs the character of the resulting [[Weather Condition|Weather Condition]].

If the check also generates \[triumph], the character may request that the GM reroll the **Weather Shift** and choose either result.

Once the **Weather Shift** has been resolved, the [[Expedition]] may spend 1 [[Destiny Point]] to reroll it. The **Weather Shift** may only be rerolled once.
