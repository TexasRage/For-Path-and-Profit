Mycorris experiences three distinct seasons: [[The Bloom]], [[The Siphon]], and [[The Anchor]]. Each represents a different phase of the [[Mycorr Tree]] cycle, bringing major changes to the planet's ecology, climate, and available resources.

- [[The Bloom]] — A season of rapid growth and heavy rainfall. The [[Mycorr Tree]] surges with life, driving lush vegetation and abundant wildlife across [[Mycorris]]. Abundant moisture supports the planet's ecosystems, making this the most productive season for [[Forage]], [[Hunt]], and [[Fish]]. Powerful storms and [[Weather Condition|Ion Storms]] can occur during this season.

- [[The Siphon]] — A season of depletion. The [[Mycorr Tree]] aggressively draws moisture and nutrients from the soil and atmosphere, creating hot, dry conditions. Water becomes scarce, vegetation withers, and the landscape becomes increasingly difficult to survive in.

- [[The Anchor]] — A season of recovery and restraint. Atmospheric moisture replenishes as temperatures fall toward extreme cold, slowing the [[Mycorr Tree|Mycorr Tree's]] siphoning activity. Frost spreads across the landscape, allowing the ecosystem to stabilize before the cycle begins again.
