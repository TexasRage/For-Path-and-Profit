A season of depletion and oppressive heat. The [[Mycorr Tree]] draws moisture and nutrients aggressively from the soil and atmosphere, leaving the air hot, dry, and heavy with dust. Rivers and wetlands recede, vegetation wilts, and the landscape becomes increasingly barren as the season progresses. Wildlife retreats toward remaining sources of water, making it more difficult to find and harvest food.

Characters add a \[setback] to [[Forage]], [[Hunt]], and [[Fish]] checks during the Siphon. Characters exposed to [[Extreme Heat]] must consume additional [[Consumable|Consumables]] to remain hydrated. If the effects of Extreme Heat are not counteracted, prolonged exposure can cause [[Heatstroke]].
