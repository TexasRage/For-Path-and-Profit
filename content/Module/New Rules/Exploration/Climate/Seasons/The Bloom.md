The Bloom is a season of renewal and rapid growth. The [[Mycorr Tree]] surges with life, drawing moisture from the atmosphere and driving lush vegetation across [[Mycorris]]. Heavy rainfall replenishes rivers and wetlands, while plants and wildlife become increasingly active and abundant. The landscape is at its most fertile, with life flourishing across the planet.

Characters add a \[boost] to **[[Forage]], [[Hunt]], and [[Fish]] checks** during the Bloom, making it the most productive season for gathering food and other natural resources.

The increased activity of the [[Mycorr Tree]] also produces unusual atmospheric disturbances. [[Weather Condition|Ion Storms]] are a defining feature of the Bloom, occurring when atmospheric energy builds to dangerous levels before discharging across the planet. These storms can appear with little warning and pose a significant hazard to anyone caught outdoors.
