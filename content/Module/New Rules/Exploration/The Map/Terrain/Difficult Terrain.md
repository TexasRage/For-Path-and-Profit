Difficult Terrain represents terrain that can be traversed but significantly hinders movement.

When moving through Difficult Terrain during structured gameplay, a character must spend 2 **[[Move Maneuver|Move Maneuvers]]** to move 1 range band instead of the normal 1 **[[Move Maneuver]]**.

During exploration, if the [[Explore]] Action begins in Difficult Terrain, the [[Expedition]]'s movement is reduced by 1 hex.

Certain [[Vehicles]], [[Mounts]], or other methods of travel may ignore or otherwise interact with Difficult Terrain as specified by their rules.
