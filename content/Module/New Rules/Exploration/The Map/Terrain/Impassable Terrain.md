Impassable Terrain cannot normally be traversed by a character or Expedition.

During structured gameplay, characters cannot move through Impassable Terrain unless a rule or ability allows them to do so.

During exploration, an [[Expedition]] cannot enter a hex containing Impassable Terrain unless its method of travel allows it to overcome the terrain.

Certain [[Vehicles]], [[Mounts]], or other methods of travel may ignore or otherwise interact with Impassable Terrain as specified by their rules.
