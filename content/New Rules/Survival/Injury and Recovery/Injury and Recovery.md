Injuries represent physical conditions that require medical attention beyond the normal recovery of [[Wounds]]. Unlike [[Critical Injury|Critical Injuries]], which are immediate consequences of severe attacks and accidents, Injuries represent conditions that persist until properly treated.

### Injuries

A character may gain an **Injury** as a result of environmental hazards, exploration hazards, or other effects. Each Injury describes its own effects and the requirements necessary to recover from it.

- _[[Heatstroke]]_
- _[[Frostbite]]_
- _[[Poisoned]]_
- _[[Hobbled]]_

### Recovery

A character may recover from an Injury in the following ways:

- **Long Rest:** After completing a [[Long Rest]], a character recovers from 1 Injury.
- **Maximum Wounds:** If a character would recover Wounds while already at their maximum [[Wounds|Wound Threshold]], they instead recover from 1 Injury.
- **Medpac:** A character may expend the supplies in a _[[Medpac]]_ to treat an Injury. Doing so requires 1 hour and a [[Short Rest]].

Some Injuries have additional requirements or restrictions on recovery. These are described in the individual Injury's rules.
