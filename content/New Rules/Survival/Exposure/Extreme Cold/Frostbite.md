Frostbite is an Injury caused by prolonged exposure to [[Extreme Cold]]. The affected character's extremities become numb and damaged, impairing their physical capabilities.

The character upgrades the difficulty of all **Brawn**, **Agility**, and **Willpower** checks once.

### Recovery

The next time the character completes a [[Long Rest]] outside of [[Extreme Cold]], they recover from Frostbite.
