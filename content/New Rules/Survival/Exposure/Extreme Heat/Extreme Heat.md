Extreme Heat places additional strain on characters and increases the amount of _[[Consumable|Consumables]]_ they require. Characters exposed to Extreme Heat suffer \[setback] on all checks. Characters wearing Medium or Heavy Armor suffer an additional \[setback].

While exposed to Extreme Heat, a character's **[[Daily Needs|Daily Needs check]]** is more demanding. On any \[dark] result, they removes a _[[Consumable]]_; on a \[double dark] result, they remove an additional  _[[Consumable]]_ (for a total of 2).

If a character fails to fulfill their [[Daily Needs]] by the end of a [[Planetary Day]] while exposed to Extreme Heat, they must immediately make an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Resilience check**. The difficulty increases by 1 for each consecutive [[Quarter-Day]] the character has spent exposed to Extreme Heat. On a failure, the character suffers 1 rank of _[[Exhaustion]]_.

A \[Despair] result on the **Resilience check** causes the character to suffer _[[Heatstroke]]_.
