You can take one [[Short Rest]] during each [[Quarter-Day]]. You can take a [[Short Rest]] in addition to any other [[Expedition Action]] you undertake during that [[Quarter-Day]].

To take a [[Long Rest]], you must first **[[Make Camp]]** and then take the **[[Rest Action]]**.

If you do not complete a [[Long Rest]] during a [[Planetary Day]], or if your [[Long Rest]] is disturbed, you must succeed on an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Resilience check** or suffer 1 rank of _[[Exhaustion]]_. The difficulty increases by 1 for each consecutive [[Planetary Day]] you go without completing a [[Long Rest]].
