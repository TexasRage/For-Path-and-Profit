_[[Exhaustion]]_ represents physical and mental fatigue accumulated through exertion, exposure, deprivation, and prolonged activity. _[[Exhaustion]]_ is tracked in ranks, with each rank making it increasingly difficult for a character to withstand further strain.

Each rank of _[[Exhaustion]]_ reduces the character’s [[Strain Threshold]] by 1.

_[[Exhaustion]]_ can only be recovered through [[Long Rest]]. Upon completing a [[Long Rest]], the character removes 1 rank of _[[Exhaustion]]_.
