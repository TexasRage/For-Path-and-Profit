[[Mycorris]] is an unrelenting world that wears down both mind and body. The [[Expedition]] must properly rest if they hope to survive.

When the [[Expedition]] performs the **Rest Action**, characters spend one [[Quarter-Day]] resting and recovering. If the [[Expedition]] has established a [[Make Camp|camp]], each character automatically gains the benefits of a [[Long Rest]].

Without a camp, each character must make a **Resilience check** with a difficulty equal to the [[Terrain|Explore difficulty]] of the terrain they are resting in, as shown in the **[[Terrain]]** table.

On a success, the character gains the benefits of a [[Long Rest]]. On a failure, the character gains the benefits of a [[Short Rest]].

[[Short Rest]] — Recover Strain and take a brief period to recover and collect your thoughts.

[[Long Rest]] — Recover Wounds, Strain, and other benefits from a full period of rest.

If a character is mounted, they may perform the [[Rest|Rest Action]] while remaining in the saddle, provided the mount is being led or otherwise guided. The mount will naturally follow the [[Expedition]] while the character rests, but cannot react to danger while the character is asleep.

If a character is traveling in a [[Vehicles|Vehicle]], only passengers may benefit from a [[Rest|Rest Action]] while remaining aboard. A character actively piloting or otherwise operating the vehicle cannot rest while performing their duties.
