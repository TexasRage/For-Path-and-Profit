A **Long Rest** replaces the **Full Night's Rest** described in the standard Star Wars RPG rules. It provides a full period of rest, allowing a character to recover from the physical and mental strain of exploration.

When a character gains the benefits of a **Long Rest**, they heal 1 Wound, recover Strain equal to half the character’s Strain Threshold, and fulfill their [[Daily Needs|Daily Sleep]] requirement.

If a character recovers a Wound while already at their maximum [[Wounds|Wound Threshold]], they may instead recover from one [[Injury]].
