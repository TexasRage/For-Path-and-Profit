The [[Expedition]] may **Forage** to gather natural resources and other useful _[[Material|Materials]]_. Characters may perform the **Forage Action** independently, or multiple characters may work together by having additional characters use the **[[Assist Maneuver]]**.

When a character performs the **Forage Action**, they spend a [[Quarter-Day]] searching the surrounding area for _[[Material|Materials]]_. The character makes either a **Survival** or **Knowledge (Xenology)** check. If equipped with a _[[Hand Scanner]]_, they may instead make a **Computers** or **Knowledge (Education)** check. The difficulty is determined by the terrain of the hex being foraged, as shown in the **[[Terrain]]** table.

On a successful check, the character gathers 2d10 _[[Material|Materials]]_. If the check generates \[success]\[success]\[success], they gather an additional 1d10 _[[Material|Materials]]_.

A character may instead forgo gathering _[[Material|Materials]]_ to replenish a _[[Water Canteen]]_ or _[[Medpac]]_. On a successful check, the chosen item is replenished instead of gathering _[[Material|Materials]]_.

On a failed check, no _[[Material|Materials]]_ are gathered and no equipment is replenished. If the check generates \[failure]\[failure]\[failure] or \[despair], roll on the **Foraging Hazard** table.

#### Foraging Hazard

| d6  | Hazard                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Poisonous:** The character harvests 1d2 _[[Materials]]_ but gains the _[[Poisoned\|Poisoned Injury]]_.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 2   | **Shaken Confidence:** The harsh conditions, close calls, and mounting frustration of an unsuccessful search begin to wear on the [[Expedition\|Expedition's]] morale. Flip 1 Destiny Point as doubt and anxiety spread through the group.                                                                                    |
| 3   | **Allergic Reaction:** The character develops an intense rash, swelling, or irritation from exposure to an allergen or foreign substance. The character cannot benefit from a [[Long Rest]] until the reaction subsides after 1 day or is successfully treated with an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Medicine check**. |
| 4   | **Major Injury:** The character suffers a severe fall from Short range and gains the _[[Hobbled\|Hobbled Injury]]_.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 5   | **Misplaced Equipment:** The character misplaces a random piece of equipment. The character may attempt to recover it by spending a [[Quarter-Day]] searching the area.                                                                                                                                                       |
| 6   | **Prey:** The character unknowingly enters a hunting ground and is attacked by local creatures.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
