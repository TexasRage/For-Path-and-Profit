When the [[Expedition]] performs the **Explore Action**, the party travels from hex to hex, uncovering and mapping the world of [[Mycorris]].

Under normal conditions, the [[Expedition]] may travel up to 2 hexes per [[Quarter-Day]] while traveling on foot. If the **Explore Action** begins in [[Difficult Terrain]], the Expedition's movement is reduced by 1 hex.

[[Mounts]] increase the Expedition's movement by 1 hex. [[Vehicles]] increase its movement by an amount equal to their **Speed** rating.

An [[Expedition]] may typically perform the **Explore Action** twice per [[Planetary Day]]. The Expedition may [[#Press On|Press On]] to perform the **Explore Action** a third time that day, subject to the additional risks and consequences described below.

Unless the GM determines otherwise, the **Explore Action** is performed as a group. One character serves as the **Lead**, guiding the Expedition toward its destination.

When the Expedition chooses a destination hex, the **Lead** makes a **Survival check**. If the Lead possesses _[[Surveyor's Tools]]_, they may instead make a **Knowledge (Education) check**. The difficulty is determined by the destination hex's **[[Terrain|Explore difficulty]]**.

On a success, the [[Expedition]] enters the chosen hex.

On a failure, the [[Expedition]] still enters the chosen hex, but the GM rolls on the **Exploration Hazard** table. If the Lead fails the check with \[threat] or rolls \[despair], the Expedition suffers the **Lost Hazard**.

If the [[Expedition]] is not **Lost** and has movement remaining, it may continue exploring by choosing another destination hex and repeating the process until its movement is exhausted.

### Press On

Normally, the [[Expedition]] may perform the **Explore Action** only twice per [[Planetary Day]]. The Expedition may **Press On** to perform the **Explore Action** one additional time that day, pushing beyond its normal limits.

When **Press On** is performed, each member of the [[Expedition]] suffers 2 [[Strain]]. [[Vehicles]] suffer 1 [[System Strain]]. Each member of the Expedition must then make a **Resilience check** against a difficulty equal to the **[[Terrain|Explore difficulty]]** of the destination hex.

A character who fails this check suffers 1 rank of [[Exhaustion]]. If half or more of the Expedition fails the check, the Expedition makes no progress and must immediately [[Rest]].

Special rules apply when the Expedition is using [[Mounts]] or [[Vehicles]]:

- **[[Mounts]]:** Each rider makes a **Survival check** against the destination hex's **[[Terrain|Explore difficulty]]**. On a success, the mount makes the **Resilience check** instead of its rider. On a failure, the mount suffers the _[[Lame]]_ Critical Injury.
- **[[Vehicles|Wheeled Vehicles and Landspeeders]]:** The **Pilot** makes a **Piloting check** against the destination hex's **[[Terrain|Explore difficulty]]**. On a success, the crew makes a **Mechanics or Computers check**, as appropriate for the [[Vehicles]], instead of Resilience. On a failure, the vehicle suffers a [[Maintenance Event]].
- **[[Vehicles|Airspeeders and Starships]]:** These vehicles automatically succeed on the check, but immediately suffer a [[Maintenance Event]].

### Charted Paths

Once a hex has been charted, the [[Expedition]] no longer needs to make an [[Explore|Explore check]] to travel through it. The **Lead** or **Pilot** may navigate the Expedition through a charted hex automatically.

Players may track charted hexes however they prefer, such as marking them on a physical map or recording them as explored in their notes.

[[Vehicles|Airspeeders]] and [[Vehicles|Starships]] do not automatically chart terrain, encounters, or [[Points of Interest]] when traveling over a hex. Airborne hexes can be charted by landing or performing the [[Scan]] action.
