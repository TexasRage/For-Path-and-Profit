The [[Expedition]] must remain aware of its surroundings while exploring [[Mycorris]]. When a character performs the **Scan Action**, they assume the role of **Scout**, monitoring the Expedition's surroundings while the group travels.

The [[Expedition]] may perform the **Scan Action** while [[Explore|Exploring]], allowing the Scout to monitor the Expedition's surroundings without preventing the group from performing another [[Expedition Action]].

If the **Scout** is aboard an [[Vehicles|Airspeeder]] or [[Vehicles|Starship]], the **Scan Action** may only be performed while the vehicle is stationary, as its scanners require time to acquire a lock. Scanning from an airborne vehicle only charts the terrain directly beneath the vehicle.

The **Scout** is responsible for detecting approaching threats, environmental hazards, and other dangers encountered during exploration.

At the start of each [[Quarter-Day]], the **Scout** may make either a **Vigilance** or **Perception** check. If equipped with a _[[General Purpose Scanner]]_, _[[Hand Scanner]]_, or operating a vehicle equipped with scanner systems, they may instead make a **Computers** check.

The difficulty is determined by the terrain of the hex the [[Expedition]] is currently entering (see the **[[Terrain]]** table). The difficulty may be modified by the circumstances of the encounter.

The result of the check is resolved according to the [[Random Encounters|encounter]] or hazard being detected.
