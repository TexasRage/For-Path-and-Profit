[[Mycorris]]' oceans, rivers, and lakes are abundant with aquatic life, making fishing a reliable method of acquiring _[[Protein|Proteins]]_ for the [[Expedition]].

A character performing the **Fish Action** must possess a _[[Tackle Kit]]_ and be within a hex containing a body of water. Characters may perform the **Fish Action** independently, or multiple characters may work together by having additional characters use the **[[Assist Maneuver]]**.

When a character performs the **Fish Action**, they spend a [[Quarter-Day]] beside or on a body of water attempting to catch fish. The character makes an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Survival or Coordination check**.

On a success, the character catches a fish worth 1 _[[Protein|Protein]]_. If the character succeeds with \[success]\[success]\[success], they catch a large fish worth 2 _[[Protein|Proteins]]_. If the character succeeds with \[success]\[success]\[success]\[success]\[success], they catch a huge fish worth 3 _[[Protein|Proteins]]_.

If the character fails the check, no fish are caught. If the check fails with \[failure]\[failure]\[failure] or generates \[despair], the character's _[[Tackle Kit]]_ becomes [[Equipment Damage|Damaged]] one step.
