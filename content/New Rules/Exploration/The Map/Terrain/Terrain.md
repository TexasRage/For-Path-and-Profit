Terrain describes the predominant environment within a hex. Each terrain type affects exploration by modifying the difficulty of relevant [[Expedition Action|Expedition Actions]], influencing movement and travel, and shaping the resources, hazards, and discoveries encountered there.

### Navigating Terrain

The method of travel can affect how the Expedition interacts with terrain. Some terrain may reduce movement, require a particular vehicle, or prevent certain forms of travel entirely.

[[Mounts]], [[Vehicles|Landspeeders]], and [[Vehicles|Wheeled Vehicles]] remain subject to terrain effects. [[Vehicles|Airspeeders]] and [[Vehicles|Starships]] ignore ground terrain when traveling.

### Terrain Categories

Terrain may be classified as **[[Difficult Terrain]]** or **[[Impassable Terrain]]**.

**[[Difficult Terrain]]** represents terrain that can be traversed but slows or complicates movement.

**[[Impassable Terrain]]** represents terrain that cannot normally be traversed by the Expedition's current method of travel.

See [[Difficult Terrain]] and [[Impassable Terrain]] for their respective effects.
