Weather conditions represent the immediate environmental effects produced by the [[Weather|Weather Counter]]. Each condition modifies visibility, travel, or character survival while it remains active.

#### Weather Condition

| Weather Condition            | Effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Blizzard**                 | Characters suffer [[Extreme Cold]] with its effects upgraded three times. Vision is [[Heavily Obscured]], and all terrain is treated as [[Difficult Terrain]].                                                                               |
| **Medium Snowfall**          | Characters suffer [[Extreme Cold]] with its effects upgraded twice. Vision is [[Moderately Obscured]].                                                                                                                                       |
| **Light Snowfall**           | Characters suffer [[Extreme Cold]] with its effects upgraded once. Vision is [[Lightly Obscured]].                                                                                                                                           |
| **Ion Storm**                | Vision is [[Heavily Obscured]]. Technology exposed to the storm suffers 1 step of [[Equipment Damage]]. Vehicles suffer 1 [[System Strain]] and trigger 1 [[Maintenance Event]] for each [[Quarter-Day]] they are operated during the storm. |
| **Heavy Rain and Lightning** | Vision is [[Heavily Obscured]].                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Medium Rainfall**          | Vision is [[Moderately Obscured]].                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Light Rainfall**           | Vision is [[Lightly Obscured]].                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Stifling Heat**            | Characters suffer [[Extreme Heat]].                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Sweltering Heat**          | Characters suffer [[Extreme Heat]] with its effects upgraded once.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Scorching Heat**           | Characters suffer [[Extreme Heat]] with its effects upgraded twice.                                                                                                                                                                          |

### Mud

The [[Planetary Day]] following any Weather Condition that produces precipitation, the terrain becomes muddy.

All terrain is treated as [[Difficult Terrain]]. Terrain that was already considered Difficult Terrain becomes [[Impassable Terrain]] instead.

Characters suffer \[setback] on **Athletics and Coordination checks** while traveling through mud.

Mud persists until the end of the following [[Planetary Day]].
