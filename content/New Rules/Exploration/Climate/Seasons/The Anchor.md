A season of recovery and restraint. Temperatures fall dramatically as the [[Mycorr Tree]] slows its activity and the planet begins to replenish itself. Frost spreads across the landscape, waterways grow cold and sluggish, and much of the vegetation enters a dormant state. Wildlife becomes less active and more difficult to find as the ecosystem conserves its resources through the cold season.

Characters add a \[setback]\[setback] to **[[Forage]], [[Hunt]], and [[Fish]] checks** during the Anchor, making the gathering of food and natural resources more difficult.

The cold can expose characters to [[Extreme Cold]], which can cause [[Exhaustion]] and, with prolonged exposure, [[Frostbite]]. Appropriate shelter and equipment can help protect characters from the worst effects of the season.
