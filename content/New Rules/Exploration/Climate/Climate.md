[Mycorris](Mycorris.md)' weather patterns are governed by the [[Mycorr Tree]] cycle, a planet-spanning ecological system in which the great forest network regulates atmospheric moisture, pressure, and temperature. This cycle drives the planet's seasons and creates the distinct environmental conditions found throughout the year.

### Seasons

[[Seasons]] describes the three major phases of the [[Mycorr Tree]] cycle and the broad environmental conditions associated with each. The current season determines the general climate of [[Mycorris]] and limits which [[Weather Condition]] can occur.

- [[The Bloom]] — A period of rapid growth, heavy rainfall, and abundant [[Hypersap]] production. The atmosphere is warm and humid, and dangerous ion storms may occur.
- [[The Siphon]] — A period of depletion as the [[Mycorr Tree]] draws moisture and nutrients from the environment. Temperatures rise, water becomes scarce, and dry conditions dominate the landscape.
- [[The Anchor]] — A period of recovery and restraint. Temperatures fall dramatically, frost spreads across the landscape, and the [[Mycorr Tree|Mycorr Tree's]] activity slows as the planet replenishes itself.

### Weather

[[Weather]] describes the day-to-day conditions experienced across [[Mycorris]]. Rather than being determined independently each day, weather changes gradually over time, allowing periods of stable conditions to develop while still permitting sudden shifts.

[[Shifts]] explains how the current weather changes from day to day using the [[Weather|Weather Counter]]. The counter persists over time, moving toward or away from the season's ideal conditions as the weather changes. The current season determines which results are possible.

[[Weather Condition]] describes the individual conditions produced by the [[Weather|Weather Counter]], ranging from clear and comfortable days to heavy storms, extreme heat, and extreme cold. These conditions can affect visibility, travel, exploration actions, and character survival.
