Mycorris is an untamed frontier world shaped by dense ecosystems, fractured history, and the remnants of forces that once studied or exploited it. Exploration is not simply travel between points on a map, but an ongoing process of survival, interpretation, and discovery. The environment is active, often hostile, and frequently conceals as much as it reveals. Every journey across Mycorris carries the risk of depletion, misdirection, and unintended encounters with the planet’s layered past.

[[The Map]] — Covers the planetary map, hex-based exploration, and the different [[Terrain]] encountered across Mycorris.

[[Climate]] — Covers the planet’s [[Seasons]] and [[Weather|Weather Counter]] used to determine changing conditions.

[[Planetary Day]] — Covers the four [[Quarter-Day|Quarter-Days]] used to track time during exploration, as well as [[Vision]] and [[Brightness]].

### Exploration Actions

[[Expedition Action|Expedition Actions]] are the primary activities characters perform while traveling and surviving in the frontier. Each action represents a distinct use of time during a [[Quarter-Day]].

- [[Explore]] — Travel through and chart new hexes.
- [[Scan]] — Observe the surrounding area for threats, creatures, and environmental details.
- [[Forage]] — Gather wild resources and replenish essential supplies.
- [[Hunt]] — Track and harvest wildlife for [[Protein|Proteins]].
- [[Fish]] — Catch aquatic wildlife for [[Protein|Proteins]].
- [[Cook]] — Process [[Protein|Proteins]] into [[Consumable|Consumables]].
- [[Make Camp]] — Establish temporary shelter for the expedition.
- [[Modify Equipment]] — Repair, maintain, or modify equipment.
- [[Rest]] — Recover from the physical and mental strain of exploration.
- [[Forecast]] — Predict upcoming weather conditions.
- [[Adventure]] — Investigate locations, pursue objectives, and resolve story-driven encounters.
