Power Armor incorporates an integrated life-support system and powered exoskeleton, allowing the wearer to operate in environments that would otherwise be hazardous or inaccessible. The suit's helmet incorporates a comlink along with enhanced optical and auditory sensors, while powered assistance reduces the physical burden of wearing its substantial protective systems.

Power Armor is sealed armor and can sustain the wearer in a vacuum or toxic environment for up to 1 hour. The suit's integrated sensors allow the wearer to remove 2 \[setback] from **Perception, Surveillance, Vigilance, and combat checks** caused by darkness, smoke, or similar conditions that obscure vision.

While powered, Power Armor increases the wearer's [[Brawn]] by 1 and provides +1 rank of Athletics. These increases do not increase the wearer's [[Soak]] or [[Wound Threshold]].

Power Armor requires a supply of [[Fuel Cell]] to operate. A [[Fuel Cell]] power supply provides 12 hours of operation.

If the armor loses power or otherwise ceases to function, its [[Encumbrance]] increases to 12, its [[Defense]] becomes 0, and the wearer loses the additional [[Brawn]] and Athletics rank provided by the powered systems.
