Heavy Armor represents the highest class of personal battlefield protection short of vehicle-scale systems, designed for prolonged frontline engagement in the most hostile combat environments. Constructed from reinforced layered plating and advanced support frameworks, these armor systems prioritize maximum survivability and environmental protection over mobility and concealment.

All Heavy Armor includes an integrated helmet as part of the armor profile. The helmet is considered part of the armor for the purposes of attachments and modifications, but provides no additional effects or equipment unless otherwise specified.

Heavy Armor may be Sealed Armor, providing protection against hazardous environments and allowing the wearer to operate in conditions that would otherwise require specialized environmental equipment. Whether a suit is sealed is specified in its individual profile.
