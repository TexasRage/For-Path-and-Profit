Heavy Battle Armor is designed for personnel who expect to operate in sustained and highly dangerous combat. While often associated with commandos, bounty hunters, and assassin droids, heavy battle armor is used by a wide variety of military and security forces throughout the galaxy.

Heavy Battle Armor may take the form of a fully sealed armor system or a heavy protective vest paired with a blast helmet. Regardless of configuration, its reinforced construction provides substantial protection while allowing the wearer to operate in demanding combat environments.
