Armored Clothing consists of light vests, jackets, or reinforced trousers fitted with plastoid or ceramic plating, often layered with mesh or energy-dispersal materials. The armor is designed to protect vital areas without significantly restricting movement.

When worn beneath suitable clothing, the protective elements are generally unobtrusive, though an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Perception check** may reveal that the wearer's garments have been reinforced.

The embedded plating helps absorb and redirect incoming attacks away from critical areas, providing meaningful protection despite its relatively light construction.

**Models Include:** Ayelixe Fabrico Armorweave, A/KT Tuff1 Combat Jumpsuit, Tenloss Syndicate "ShadowStalker" Assassin Gear.
