Blast Vests are designed to protect the wearer from explosive shockwaves and flying debris. Their layered construction helps distribute sudden impacts across the body, providing additional protection when caught within the area of an explosion.

When worn, a Blast Vest provides +1 [[Soak]] against attacks using the [[Blast]] quality.
