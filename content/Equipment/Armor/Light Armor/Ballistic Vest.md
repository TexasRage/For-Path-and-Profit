Ballistic Vests use layered fibers, ceramic inserts, or other impact-resistant materials to protect the wearer's vital areas from physical projectiles. They are particularly effective against conventional firearms and other projectile weapons while remaining light enough for everyday use.

When worn, a Ballistic Vest provides +1 [[Soak]] against projectile attacks.
