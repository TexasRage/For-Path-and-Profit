Energy Vests incorporate specialized conductive layers and energy-dispersal materials designed to diffuse the effects of directed-energy weapons. The additional protection is particularly useful against blasters and other energy-based attacks, while adding little bulk compared to heavier armor systems.

When worn, an Energy Vest provides +1 [[Soak]] against energy-based attacks.
