The Hands-Free Weapon System allows armor to be modified with integrated weapon mounts, allowing the wearer to deploy additional firepower while keeping their hands free for other tasks. Popular among spacers, bounty hunters, and special forces, these systems use articulated mounts, control interfaces, and stabilization systems to operate mounted weapons in combat.

A weapon must also be modified with an Armor Mount modification before it can be installed. Installing an Armor Mount requires 1 Hard Point on the weapon.

This attachment can mount any Ranged (Light) or Ranged (Heavy) weapon with an Encumbrance of 4 or less, subject to GM discretion.

**Base Modifiers**: Mounts a single weapon on the armor, allowing the wearer to aim and fire it without using their hands or arms.

**Modification Options**: None.
