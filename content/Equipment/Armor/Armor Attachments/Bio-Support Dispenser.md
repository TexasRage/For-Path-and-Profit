Sophisticated medical technology integrated into armor can enhance the effects of in-combat treatments through carefully regulated medication delivery and automated support systems. These systems monitor vital signs and administer controlled doses of stimulants, coagulants, and other medical compounds when needed, improving the effectiveness of emergency treatment.

This attachment may only be installed on a fully enclosed suit of armor.

**Base Modifiers**: When the wearer takes a [[Short Rest]], they also heal 1 [[Wound]]. In addition, the first time in an encounter that the character's current [[Wounds]] exceed half their [[Wound Threshold]], the armor automatically applies a [[Stimpack]] to the character.

**Modification Options**: 1x Heal 1 additional [[Wound]] during a [[Short Rest]].

**Models Include**: Athakam Medtech Smartmed 1000 Integrated Systems, Arakyd Biosteady Smartmonitor.
