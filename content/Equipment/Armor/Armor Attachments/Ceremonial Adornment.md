Diplomats and their retinues typically avoid armor and weapons, as visible displays of force can undermine their intentions. However, carefully crafted adornments, symbols of office, cultural markings, and decorative elements can transform protective equipment into a sign of authority, tradition, or respect.

The GM may alter or remove this benefit when the armor's appearance is particularly intimidating, aggressive, or inappropriate for the situation.
