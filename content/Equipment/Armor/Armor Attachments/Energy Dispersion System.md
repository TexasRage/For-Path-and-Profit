This minute metallic wiring is woven throughout the armor to distribute electrical shocks across the protective surface while grounding the wearer against dangerous energy surges. Originally developed for hazardous environment workers and military personnel, these systems reduce the effectiveness of electrical attacks and other energy-based threats.

**Base Modifiers:** While using the wearer's [[Soak]] to reduce damage dealt to the wearer's [[Strain Threshold]], count the [[Soak]] value as 2 higher.

**Modification Options:** None.

**Models Include:** Atlas Corp Model 88 Electric Shield, Merr-Sonn AEGIS Nanoarray.
