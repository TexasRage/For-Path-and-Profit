Armor Spikes add spikes, blades, and other protrusions to the armor's shoulders, knuckles, feet, knees, elbows, or other exposed areas. These modifications turn the armor itself into a weapon, allowing the wearer to inflict additional harm when fighting at close range.

This attachment may be applied only to armor that covers the arms or legs.

**Base Modifiers**: When the wearer suffers a hit from a Melee, Brawl, or Lightsaber check, after the attack is resolved, the character may spend \[threat]\[threat]\[threat] or \[despair] to automatically inflict one hit for 6 damage on the attacker.

**Modification Options**: 3x Increase the damage attackers suffer by +1 Mods, 1x Innate Talent ([[Intimidating]]) Mod.
