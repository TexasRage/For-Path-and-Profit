Armor Inserts, also known as trauma plates, are thin plasteel and ballistic gel plates designed to be concealed within clothing or light protective gear. While they lack the protection of dedicated armor systems, they provide additional protection against common threats without significantly affecting mobility or appearance.

This attachment can be applied to any clothing or armor with a Defense of 0 or less and a Soak of 1 or less.

**Base Modifiers**: Turns any article of clothing into [[Armored Clothing]]. Character can notice the inclusion of armor inserts in clothing by making an **Average (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]) Perception check**.

**Modification Options**: 1x Increase soak by +1 Mod, 1x Increase defense by +1 Mod, 1x Increase difficulty of check to notice inclusion to **Hard (\[difficulty]\[difficulty]\[difficulty])** Mod.

**Models Include**: Creshaldyne "Slimline" Armor Plates.
