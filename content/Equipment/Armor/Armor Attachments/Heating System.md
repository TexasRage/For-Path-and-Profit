Heating systems are installed in sealed armor and protective suits to regulate body temperature in dangerously cold environments. Using thermal regulators, heated layers, and environmental controls, these systems allow the wearer to operate effectively in frigid conditions that would otherwise threaten survival.

This attachment can be installed on any armor that covers the entire body and can be sealed.

**Base Modifiers**: Reduces the difficulty of **[[Resilience]] checks** made to resist effects of [[Extreme Cold]] by one. Removes two \[setback]\[setback] added to checks due to [[Extreme Cold]].

**Modification Options**: None.

**Models Include**: Atlas Corp DL87 "Frostback" Armor Enhancement, Min-Dal Model 00 Heating System.
