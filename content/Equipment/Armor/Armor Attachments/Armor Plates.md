Additional reinforced plating designed to absorb catastrophic impacts and protect the structural integrity of the wearer’s equipment. These sacrificial plates are intended to deform and fail under extreme stress, reducing damage transferred into the armor itself.

**Base Modifiers**: When the wearer suffers a [[Critical Injury]], after resolving the effects of the injury, they may choose whether the equipped armor or this attachment suffers the resulting equipment damage.

If this attachment suffers the damage, it immediately becomes **Majorly Damaged**. It may be repaired following the **Equipment Repair** rules. The wearer still suffers the [[Critical Injury]] normally.

**Modification Options**: None
