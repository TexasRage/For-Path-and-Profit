An Individual Field Disruptor generates a localized field of disruptive energy around the wearer, interfering with other energy fields and disrupting nearby electronic systems. The field forms a shimmering sphere approximately two meters in diameter centered on the device and wearer, but its intensity also obscures the wearer's visibility.

While active, the field interferes with other energy fields. If it overlaps another energy field, that field provides no Defense for as long as the two fields intersect.

If the wearer enters Engaged range with a droid, the droid suffers 5 Stun Damage. If the field enters Engaged range with another electronic device, the device shorts out and becomes inactive for the remainder of the encounter.

While the field is active, the wearer counts as being armed with a Melee weapon that has +2 Damage and the Stun Damage quality when making melee attacks.
