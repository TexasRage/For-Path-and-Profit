Damaged armor can be restored using the same repair procedures used for weapons. Repairs require a [[Tool Kit]] and are performed as part of the [[Modify Equipment]] Action.

A character may repair armor using Mechanics or an appropriate skill associated with the armor's technology. Armor that relies heavily on programming, circuitry, or electronic systems may also be repaired using Computers.

The repair difficulty and time are determined by the armor's current Damage level. Each difficulty die of the repair check requires approximately 2 hours of work. Attempting repairs without the proper tools increases the difficulty once, while attempting to complete repairs in less time upgrades the difficulty once.

A successful repair restores one step of Damage. If \[despair] is generated on the repair check, the armor instead suffers one additional step of Damage.

Repair costs are based on the severity of the armor's Damage. Characters repairing their own equipment reduce the repair cost by 10% per \[success] generated on the repair check, while professional repair services generally charge the full amount.
