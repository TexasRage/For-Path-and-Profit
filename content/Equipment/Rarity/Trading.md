Trading represents commercial transactions in which the [[Expedition]] purchases goods in one location and transports them to another location where those goods are more valuable.

Trading uses the normal [[Selling]] rules, but the item's value is first adjusted based on the difference in [[Rarity]] between the location where it was purchased and the location where it is sold.

## Trade Value

Before determining the sale price, compare the item's [[Rarity]] at the location where it was purchased to its [[Rarity]] at the destination.

Use the difference in [[Rarity]] to determine the item's adjusted value.

###### TABLE 6-3: TRADE VALUE

|**Rarity Difference**|**Cost Multiplier**|
|---|---|
|+0–1|×1|
|+2|×2|
|+3|×3|
|+4 or higher|×4|

The adjusted value becomes the item's new cost for the purpose of determining its sale price.

After adjusting the item's value, resolve the [[Selling]] check normally. A successful Negotiation check sells the goods for one-quarter of the adjusted value, while \[advantage] can increase the final sale price as described in [[Selling]].

These rules apply equally to legal and [[Black Market]] goods.

## Commercial Trade

Trading rules are intended for commercial transactions, such as purchasing multiple items or a cargo shipment at one location and selling them elsewhere.

They do not apply to ordinary purchases. A character purchasing an item for personal use pays its listed price, regardless of the item's Rarity at that location.

The GM may modify or ignore these rules when circumstances such as bulk purchases, marketing, local demand, transportation, legality, or political conditions significantly affect the value of the goods.

The GM has final say over the availability and value of goods. Narrative circumstances may make otherwise valuable goods difficult or impossible to sell.
