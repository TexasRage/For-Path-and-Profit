Characters can sell equipment they no longer need to recover part of its listed value. Selling an item requires 1 [[Quarter-Day]], representing the time spent finding a buyer, negotiating a price, and completing the transaction.

A character makes a **Negotiation check** using the item's [[Rarity]] to determine the difficulty. The GM may modify the check based on local demand, scarcity, legality, or other circumstances.

## Selling Results

The item's sale price is determined by the result of the **Negotiation check**:

- \[Success]: The item sells for one-quarter of its listed price.
- \[Success]\[Success]: The item sells for one-half of its listed price.
- \[Success]\[Success]\[Success]: The item sells for three-quarters of its listed price.

A failed check means the character is unable to find a buyer willing to purchase the item at an acceptable price.

## BLACK MARKET GOODS

The same selling rules apply to [[Black Market]] goods. Selling a Restricted or illegal item does not automatically change its sale price, but the GM may modify the circumstances or outcome based on local laws, demand, and the risks involved.
