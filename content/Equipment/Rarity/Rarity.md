Some items are more difficult to acquire than others depending on where a character is located within the galaxy. Availability is shaped by local trade conditions, supply chains, technological development, and other circumstances.

**Rarity** is a numerical value from 0–10 representing how difficult an item is to locate. Lower Rarity values represent commonly available goods, while higher values represent increasingly rare or difficult-to-obtain items.

Rarity is used to determine the availability and acquisition difficulty of equipment throughout the galaxy.

## Rarity Modifiers

An item's effective Rarity may be modified by the location where it is being acquired. Local trade conditions, technological development, supply chains, and other circumstances can make equipment easier or more difficult to obtain.

The GM may apply additional Rarity modifiers when appropriate.

See [[Acquiring Equipment]] for the Rarity table and rules for resolving acquisition checks.
