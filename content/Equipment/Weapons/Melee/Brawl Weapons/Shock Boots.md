Shock Boots are specialized footwear that incorporates the same technology found in Shock Gloves. Hidden accelerometers and pressure sensors detect kicking motions, triggering compact power generators that discharge a powerful electrical shock through conductive fibers woven into the sole. Externally, they are nearly indistinguishable from ordinary boots, making them a favorite among undercover operatives and those who never wish to be unarmed.

Because they are worn on the feet, Shock Boots do not require a free hand to use. Add \[setback] to any **Perception check** made to recognize Shock Boots as a weapon rather than ordinary footwear.
