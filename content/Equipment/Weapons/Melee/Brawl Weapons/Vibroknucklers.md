Vibroknucklers are worn like Brass Knuckles but incorporate miniature vibroblades along the striking surface that activate when the wearer clenches their fist. Compact yet deadly, they are capable of inflicting deep, devastating wounds while remaining easy to conceal. Numerous manufacturers produce their own variants, each balancing durability, concealability, and cutting power.

Add \[setback] to any **Perception check** made to locate Vibroknucklers concealed on a character's person. Vibroknucklers cannot inflict Strain damage.
