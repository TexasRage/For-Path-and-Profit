Vibroaxes are heavy, two-handed melee weapons fitted with ultrasonic vibration generators along a broad cutting edge. Designed for powerful combatants, they deliver devastating blows capable of tearing through armor and causing severe structural and bodily trauma. Due to their weight and balance, Vibroaxes are difficult to wield effectively without significant physical strength.
