Electronets are capture devices made from duracord mesh woven with conductive filaments that can deliver a powerful electrical discharge. Commonly used by corporate explorers, wildlife capture teams, and law enforcement personnel, they are designed to restrain living targets without causing lasting harm. The electrical charge is typically activated remotely after the net has entangled its target, preventing accidental discharge during deployment.
