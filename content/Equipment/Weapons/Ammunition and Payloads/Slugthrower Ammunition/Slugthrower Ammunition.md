Slugthrowers can be loaded with a wide variety of specialized ammunition, allowing them to be adapted for different targets and situations.

Loading a different ammunition type requires a Maneuver. Once loaded, the weapon continues to use that ammunition until the wielder reloads with another type or the ammunition is depleted by generating \[threat]\[threat] or \[despair] on a combat check.

Unless otherwise noted, specialized ammunition modifies the weapon's profile as described by the ammunition entry. Any item qualities granted by the ammunition are added to the weapon's existing qualities.

## Slugthrower Pistol Ammunition

Slugthrower Pistols are chambered for a wide variety of specialized ammunition, allowing a simple sidearm to be tailored for everything from personal defense and law enforcement to hunting dangerous game or engaging armored opponents.

While standard ball ammunition remains the most common choice, specialty rounds are widely available throughout the galaxy, each offering distinct tactical advantages.

Unless otherwise specified, loading specialized ammunition replaces only the weapon's listed Damage, Critical rating, Range, and item qualities with those shown in the ammunition profile. Because many specialty rounds replace the projectile with chemical, explosive, or other specialized payloads, they may not retain the weapon's normal armor-piercing capabilities.

## Slugthrower Rifle Ammunition

The greater power and range of Slugthrower Rifles make them ideal platforms for advanced ammunition. From guided hunting projectiles and incendiary rounds to explosive and tranquilizer payloads, specialized rifle cartridges are designed to exploit the weapon's superior accuracy and velocity.

Although these rounds are often more expensive and difficult to acquire than standard ammunition, experienced marksmen and hunters consider them indispensable when preparing for dangerous missions.

Loading a different ammunition type modifies the weapon's profile as shown in the ammunition entry. Depending on the ammunition selected, the weapon may lose its inherent armor-piercing capabilities in exchange for the listed special qualities.

## Scattergun Ammunition

Scatterguns are renowned for their versatility, capable of firing an extensive variety of specialized shells in addition to conventional buckshot. By replacing the weapon's payload with slugs, guided flechettes, chemical agents, or explosive charges, a single scattergun can fulfill roles ranging from breaching and crowd control to hunting dangerous wildlife and engaging heavily armored opponents.

Loading a different ammunition type modifies the weapon's profile as shown in the ammunition entry. Because many specialized shells fundamentally alter the weapon's payload, they may not retain the scattergun's inherent stopping power or special qualities, including its _Knockdown_ quality.
