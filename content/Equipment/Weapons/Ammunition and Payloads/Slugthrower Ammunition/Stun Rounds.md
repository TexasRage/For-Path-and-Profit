Stun Rounds are specialized non-lethal projectiles designed to incapacitate targets without causing significant physical trauma. Depending on the manufacturer, they may use conductive compounds, impact-dispersing materials, or other technologies intended to disrupt the target's nervous system.

When loaded, Stun Rounds replace the weapon's normal Damage with [[Stun Damage]] and add the [[Disorient]] quality to the weapon's profile.
