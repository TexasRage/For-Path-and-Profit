Armor Piercing Slugs are heavy, hardened projectiles designed to penetrate reinforced armor and other durable materials. Their dense construction allows them to punch through protective plating while retaining enough force to injure the target behind it, making them particularly effective against heavily armored opponents.

When loaded, Armor Piercing Slugs add [[Pierce]] to the weapon's profile, but remove the weapon's [[Sweep]] and [[Vicious]] qualities.
