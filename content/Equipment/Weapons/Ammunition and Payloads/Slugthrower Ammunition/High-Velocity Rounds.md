High-Velocity Rounds use increased propellant charges and specially designed projectiles to achieve greater muzzle velocity. The additional velocity produces greater stopping power and allows the projectile to transfer more energy to the target, but places additional stress on the weapon with every shot.

When **\[threat]\[threat]** would cause the weapon to run [[Ammunition and Payloads|Out of Ammunition]], the wielder may choose to either have the weapon become [[Ammunition and Payloads|Out of Ammunition]] or suffer 1 step of [[Weapon Damage|Damage]]. If the wielder chooses to damage the weapon, it remains loaded and may continue to be used for the remainder of the encounter.v

When **\[threat]\[threat]\[threat]** or **\[despair]** would cause the weapon to run [[Ammunition and Payloads|Out of Ammunition]], the weapon becomes [[Ammunition and Payloads|Out of Ammunition]] and suffers 1 step of [[Weapon Damage|Damage]]. This [[Weapon Damage|Damage]] cannot be avoided.
