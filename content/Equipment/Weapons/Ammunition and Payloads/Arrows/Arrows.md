Compound Bows can use a variety of specialized arrows, allowing them to be adapted for hunting, survival, and combat.

Unless otherwise specified, loading specialized arrows replaces the bow's listed Damage, Critical rating, Range, and item qualities with those shown in the arrow's profile. Any item qualities granted by the ammunition are added to the weapon's existing qualities unless the arrow specifically states otherwise.
