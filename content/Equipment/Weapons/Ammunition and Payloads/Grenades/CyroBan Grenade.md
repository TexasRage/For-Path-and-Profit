CryoBan grenades release a supercooled chemical agent derived from compounds used in the carbon-freezing process, rapidly absorbing heat from the surrounding area. Originally developed as fire-suppression devices for shipboard emergencies, they have since found use among mercenaries and bounty hunters as nontraditional weapons capable of inflicting frostbite, nerve damage, and cold shock.

A character using a CryoBan grenade adds a \[boost] to checks made to extinguish fires.
