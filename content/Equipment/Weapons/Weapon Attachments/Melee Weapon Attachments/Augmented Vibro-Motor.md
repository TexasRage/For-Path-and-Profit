The cutting edge of a vibro weapon's blade is made many times more dangerous than that of a standard blade by the power of its rapid vibro-motor. By increasing the frequency and intensity of the blade's vibrations, the weapon converts even the wielder's slightest movement into a more powerful and potentially lethal strike.

**Base Modifiers**: Increases the weapon's base damage by 1. Grants the weapon the [[Cumbersome]] 3 quality or increases its existing [[Cumbersome]] rating by 1.

**Modification Options**: 1x Damage +1 Mod, 1x Item Quality ([[Vicious]] +1) Mod, 1 Decrease the weapon's [[Cumbersome]] quality by 1 Mod.
