Marksman Barrels are precision-engineered replacement barrels designed for soldiers, scouts, and designated marksmen who demand exceptional long-range accuracy. Featuring refined Galven circuitry, reinforced focusing assemblies, and improved thermal regulation, these barrels produce more stable, consistent blaster bolts that retain their cohesion over extended distances, making them ideal for precision engagements.

This attachment can only be applied to blaster rifles.

**Base Modifiers**: Increases weapon's range by one range band. The weapon gains the [[Cumbersome]] 2 quality.

**Modification Options**: 2x Weapon Quality ([[Accurate]] +1) Mods, 1x Innate Talent ([[Sniper Shot]]) Mod.

**Models Include**: Merr-Sonn KX756 Marksman Barrel, SoroSuub WQ80 "Long-shot" Barrel, Arakyd 3300 Hawkeye Sharpshooter's Barrel.
