An optimized energy cell uses improved storage materials and more efficient power regulation to increase the number of shots a weapon can fire before requiring a recharge or replacement. Favored by soldiers, explorers, and bounty hunters alike, these upgraded cells help ensure a weapon remains operational when every shot counts.

This attachment can be applied to any energy weapon.

**Base Modifiers**: When the GM spends a \[Despair] result to cause the weapon to run out of ammunition, the GM must also spend \[threat]\[threat] to have that effect apply to this weapon.

**Modification Options**: None.

**Models Include**: Czerka Arms Model 4 Expanded Gas Reservoir, BlasTech X-3 Extended Energy Cell.
