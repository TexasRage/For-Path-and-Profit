Beam Splitters modify a weapon's Galven pattern by installing a honeycomb array of precision emitters within the barrel. Rather than projecting a single focused bolt, the weapon disperses its energy into multiple tightly grouped plasma streams, creating a wider impact pattern that is especially effective at close range. While this greatly improves hit probability against nearby targets, the dispersed energy rapidly loses cohesion over distance.

Beam Splitters can be applied to any blaster or other suitable energy weapon that does not possess the [[Blast]] quality.

**Base Modifiers**: Grants the [[Sweep]] item quality. Reduces the weapon's range by 1 to a minimum of engaged.

**Modification Options**: 2x Item Quality ([[Disorient]] +1) Mods.

**Models Include**: ArMek RE-312 Beam Splitter, Safidine "Sweeper" Emitter Modification Kit, Concordian Crescent Technologies ZE-1111 Spread Barrel.
