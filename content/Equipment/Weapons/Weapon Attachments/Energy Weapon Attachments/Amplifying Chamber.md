Blasters generate their bolts by energizing a measured quantity of volatile gas into superheated plasma before projecting it through the weapon's focusing systems. An Amplifying Chamber increases the efficiency of this process by further compressing and energizing the plasma, producing more powerful and destructive bolts at the cost of increased stress on the weapon's internal components.

This attachment can only be applied to blasters.

**Base Modifiers**: Adds the [[Blast]] 4 item quality. Adds \[setback] to all **Mechanics checks** to maintain or repair this weapon.

**Modification Options**: 1x Damage +1 Mod, 5x Item Quality ([[Blast]] +1) Mods.

**Models Include**: Rothana RX-99 AmpChamber, BlasTech A-13 Acceleration Chamber.
