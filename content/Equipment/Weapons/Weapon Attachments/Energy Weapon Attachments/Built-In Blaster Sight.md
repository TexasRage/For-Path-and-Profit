A popular modification among military marksmen and tactical operators, a built-in blaster sight projects a faint, low-power targeting beam whenever the trigger is partially depressed. This harmless beam "paints" the intended point of impact, allowing the wielder to confirm their aim before firing without relying solely on traditional sights.

This attachment can be applied to energy ranged weapons.

**Base Modifiers**: Remove \[setback] added by the [[Called Shot]] use of the [[Aim]] maneuver from Ranged (Light) and Ranged (Heavy) checks.

**Modification Options**: None.

**Models Include**: Neuro-Savv "Deadeye" Sight, Tana Ire y-30Z Targeting Beam.
