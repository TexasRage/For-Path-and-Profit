Some users form a deep connection with their weapons, taking additional steps to ensure that only they can operate them. Gene-Lock systems use biometric scanners and personalized security protocols to prevent unauthorized users from accessing a weapon's advanced functions.

This attachment can be applied to any handheld weapon. This modification is most effective on ranged weapons, though melee weapons with technological components may also benefit. An unauthorized user may still use the weapon as a basic melee weapon, but any integrated technology, such as a vibro-motor, targeting systems, or other electronic components, will not function.

**Base Modifiers**: The weapon functions only in the hands of its authorized owner.

**Modification Options**: 1x Self Destruct Mod (The weapon detonates if an unauthorized user tries to fire it, inflicting one Critical Injury on the user. The weapon is destroyed if it self-destructs.)
