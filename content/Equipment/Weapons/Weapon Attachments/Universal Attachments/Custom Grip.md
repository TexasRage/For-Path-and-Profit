A Custom Grip is a personalized modification designed to fit a weapon to its wielder's hand. Through careful adjustment of the grip angle, texture, and balance, a skilled tinkerer can improve the weapon's handling and comfort, allowing the wielder to operate it more effectively.

This attachment can be applied to any weapon with a handle.

**Base Modifiers**: Remove from all combat checks the weapon's owner makes using this weapon. Anyone other than the owner instead adds to all combat checks made using it.

**Modification Options**: 1x Item Quality ([[Accurate]] +1) Mod
