A Hunting Barrel is an extended replacement barrel designed to maximize a rifle's range and accuracy. Constructed with precision components, enhanced stabilizers, and improved targeting systems, the barrel improves shot consistency and allows the weapon to engage targets effectively at greater distances. Popular among hunters, scouts, and marksmen, this modification transforms a standard rifle into a dedicated long-range weapon capable of delivering precise shots across open terrain. The increased performance comes at the expense of portability, as the elongated barrel makes the weapon more difficult to maneuver in close quarters.

This attachment can only be applied to a Blaster Rifle or Slugthrower Rifle.

**Base Modifiers**: Increases weapon's range by one range band. Adds [[Cumbersome]] (+2) quality to the weapon, and increases [[Encumbrance]] by +1.

**Modification Options**: 2x Item Quality ([[Accurate]] +1) Mods, 1x Innate Talent ([[Lethal Blows]]) Mod.

**Models Include:** Czerka Model 2 "Huntsman" Precision Barrel, SoroSuub WQ60 "Sure-shot" Barrel, Arakyd 3100 "Deadeye" Barrel.
