Most micro-rockets are fired directly at their target, relying on the firer's aim and the rocket's acceleration to ensure a direct hit. A guidance system uses advanced sensors, targeting processors, and maneuvering thrusters to correct the rocket's flight path after launch, allowing it to pursue targets with greater accuracy.

This attachment can be installed on any [[Micro-Rockets|Micro-Rocket]].

**Base Modifiers**: Grants the weapon the [[Guided]] (+2) quality.

**Modification Options**: 3x Item Quality ([[Guided]] +1) Mods, 2x Item Quality ([[Accurate]] +1) Mods.

**Models Include**: Pharr Co. 'Vornskr" Guidance Package, Merr-Sonn "Outreach" Target Sensor.
