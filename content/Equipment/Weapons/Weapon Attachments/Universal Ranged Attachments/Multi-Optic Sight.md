A Multi-Optic Sight is a sophisticated sensor suite that combines magnification, thermal imaging, low-light enhancement, and advanced targeting optics into a single compact package. Designed for military marksmen, scouts, and elite operators, these systems allow a wielder to rapidly adapt to changing battlefield conditions without changing equipment.

Multi-Optic Sight can be applied to any ranged weapon.

**Base Modifiers**: Removes up to \[setback]\[setback] on any checks to use this weapon due to smoke, darkness, or other environmental conditions that obstruct vision.

**Modification Options**: 2x Skill (Perception) Mods.

**Models Include**: Galactic Arms Multi-Spectral Optical Array, Merr-Sonn 480.z1 "Beholder" Multi-Optic Sight, Atlas Corp MULTI Omni-Sight, Galactic Arms Model 44 Combat Optical Enhancer, SoroSuub XAD Omnisight.
