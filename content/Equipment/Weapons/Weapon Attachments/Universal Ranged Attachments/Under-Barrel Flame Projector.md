Uncommon but highly effective, Under-Barrel Flame Projectors provide infantry with additional close-range firepower. Mounted beneath rifle-sized weapons, these compact systems project streams of ignited fuel or chemical compounds, allowing operators to clear confined spaces, suppress enemies, and engage targets that are difficult to reach with conventional fire.

Under-Barrel Flame Projectors can only be applied to rifle-sized weapons.

**Base Modifiers**: Enables weapon to fire using the following profile at the user's choice: (Ranged \[Heavy]; Damage 10; Critical 2; Range \[Short]; [[Burn]] 5, [[Sweep]]). Adds [[Cumbersome]] (+1) to weapon.

**Modification Options**: None.

**Models Include**: Czerka "Firestorm" Under-Barrel Flame Projector, Merr-Sonn XR14 Sunsear Flame Projector, SoroSuub "Inferno" Auxiliary Flamer.
