Shortened Barrels are compact replacement barrels designed to reduce a weapon’s overall profile and make it easier to conceal. Favored by spies, plainclothes security officers, undercover operatives, and others who need to carry weapons discreetly, these modifications sacrifice some effective range in exchange for improved portability and concealment.

Shortened Barrels  can only be applied to Ranged (Light) pistols.

**Base Modifiers**: Reduces the difficulty of checks made to conceal this weapon by one. Reduces range by one range band, to a minimum of short. If already short, add \[setback] to attack checks.

**Modification Options**: 1x Innate Talent ([[Quick Draw]]) Mod.

**Models Include**: Arakyd SB4 "Stubber," SoroSuub SBSS Reduced Length Barrel.
