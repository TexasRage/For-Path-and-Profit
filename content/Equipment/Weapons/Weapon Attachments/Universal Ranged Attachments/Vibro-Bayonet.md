Although most engagements are resolved through exchanges of blaster fire, close-range firefights can quickly become brutal melee encounters. Vibro-Bayonets combine a reinforced combat blade with a compact vibro-motor, allowing rifle operators to defend themselves without abandoning their primary weapon.

Vibro-Bayonets can only be applied to Ranged (Heavy) weapons that could logically mount a bayonet.

**Base Modifiers**: Enables the weapon to be used in melee combat with the following profile at the user's choice: (Melee; Damage +1; Critical 2; Range \[Engaged]; [[Inaccurate]] 1, [[Pierce]] 2, [[Vicious]] 1).

**Modification Options**: 2 Increase the Melee weapon's damage by 1 Mods.

**Models Include**: Czerka Arms "Last Resort" Vibro-Bayonet, Merr-Sonn Model 3 Combat Attachment.
