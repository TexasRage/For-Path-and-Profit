Used with heavy squad support weapons and portable emplacement systems, tripods provide a stable firing platform for weapons that are otherwise difficult to control. These systems use a variety of technologies, from self-extending hydraulic supports to compact repulsorlift stabilizers, to secure heavy weapons in place and improve firing stability.

This attachment can only be applied to Light Repeating Blasters, Heavy Repeating Blasters, and portable Gunnery weapons that use the Ranged (Heavy) or Gunnery skill. Setting up or folding a tripod requires 2 preparation Maneuvers.

**Base Modifiers**: Decrease weapon's [[Cumbersome]] rating by 3 when set up. May not move the weapon (except to pivot) once tripod is set up.

**Modification Options**: 2x Item Quality ([[Cumbersome]] -1) Mods.

**Models Include**: BlasTech 3R Heavy Weapon Tripod Mount.
