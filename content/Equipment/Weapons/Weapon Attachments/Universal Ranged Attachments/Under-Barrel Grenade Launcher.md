Under-Barrel Grenade Launchers provide individual soldiers and small squads with additional firepower without requiring them to carry a separate support weapon. Mounted beneath rifle-sized weapons, these compact launchers allow operators to deploy explosive munitions against infantry, light vehicles, and fortified positions while retaining the versatility of their primary weapon.

Under-Barrel Grenade Launchers can only be applied to rifle-sized Ranged (Heavy) weapons.

**Base Modifiers**: Enables weapon to fire [[Grenades]]. This uses the [[Grenades|Grenade's]] profile, but it uses the Ranged (Heavy) skill to fire and has a range of medium. Adds [[Cumbersome]] (+1) to weapon and increases [[Encumbrance]] by 2. [[Grenades]] are [[Limited Ammo]] 1 weapons, and thus the launcher is [[Limited Ammo]] 1.

**Modification Options**: 5x Item Quality ([[Limited Ammo]] +1) Mods.

**Models Include**: Czerka "Thumper" Under-Barrel Grenade Launcher, SoroSuub XA203 Auxiliary Grenade Launcher, Zone Control "Viper" Under-Barrel Grenade Launcher.
