There are times when an operator needs to scan their surroundings for threats while keeping their weapon at the ready. Integrated Scanners combine compact sensor packages with a weapon-mounted display, allowing the wielder to detect lifeforms, movement, energy signatures, and other hazards without lowering their weapon.

This attachment can be applied to any Ranged (Heavy) weapon.

**Base Modifiers**: The weapon incorporates a [[General Purpose Scanner]] that the wielder can use while the weapon is drawn. The wielder adds an automatic \[advantage]\[advantage] to Initiative checks while the weapon is drawn.

**Modification Options**: 2x Remove a \[setback] from Initiative checks Mods.

**Models Include**: Carbanti United Electronics Mk. II Omni-Scope, CryonCorp "Heads-Up" Scanner Suite.
