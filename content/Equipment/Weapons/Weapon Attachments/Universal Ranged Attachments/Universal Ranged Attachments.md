Universal Ranged Attachments are designed for ranged weapon systems regardless of their underlying firing mechanism. This category includes blasters, energy weapons, slugthrowers, projectile launchers, and similar weapons that discharge or project a ranged attack.

These attachments interact with core weapon functions such as firing systems, targeting systems, ammunition or energy delivery, and recoil management.

Universal Ranged Attachments may be installed on any qualifying ranged weapon, provided the weapon meets all specific requirements listed in the individual attachment entry.
