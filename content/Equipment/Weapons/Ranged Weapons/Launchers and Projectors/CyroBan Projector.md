CryoBan projectors discharge specialized cryogenic compounds that rapidly absorb heat, creating areas of intense cold. Originally developed for firefighting, rescue operations, and hazardous environment response, these devices are capable of rapidly suppressing flames and stabilizing dangerous conditions. However, modified models with removed safety restrictors and increased output capacity have found use as improvised weapons and defensive tools, freezing targets and making them vulnerable to further attacks.

A character equipped with a CryoBan projector adds \[boost] to checks made to extinguish fires. When making an assisted check, add an additional \[boost] for each assisting character equipped with a CryoBan projector.

When used as a weapon, if the target is wearing armor that removes one or more \[setback] imposed by extreme cold, this weapon’s Pierce quality counts as 0.

**Models Include**: SEI CryoBan Industrial Projector, numerous modified variants.
