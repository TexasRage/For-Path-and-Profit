Originally developed for use with heavy aquatic armor, the Mini-Torpedo Launcher is a portable support weapon designed for underwater combat. It features an integrated two-torpedo magazine and can fire a variety of specialized [[Mini-Torpedoes]] to meet different tactical needs.

While unguided, Mini-Torpedoes are highly accurate underwater, particularly in the hands of a skilled operator. The launcher is intended for submerged use; all combat checks made to fire it outside of water have their difficulty upgraded twice.

The profile listed represents standard anti-personnel Mini-Torpedoes. Specialized Mini-Torpedoes may be loaded into the launcher, replacing the weapon's **Damage**, **Critical rating**, **Range**, and **qualities**.

Regardless of the ammunition used, the launcher retains the **Cumbersome** quality.

See [[Mini-Torpedoes]] for available ammunition types.
