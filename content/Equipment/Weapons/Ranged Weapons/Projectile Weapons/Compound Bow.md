Traditional projectile-firing bows are a rarity throughout the galaxy. Although considered archaic compared to modern firearms, modern compound bows use advanced materials and compound mechanisms to provide substantial draw strength while remaining compact, portable, and reliable.

Compound bows are favored by hunters, survivalists, and specialists who value their simplicity and near-silent operation. Their ability to use a wide variety of specialized arrows also allows them to fill roles where conventional firearms are impractical.

Standard bow ammunition consists of lightweight shafts fitted with specialized arrowheads and stabilizing fletching. Basic broadhead arrows remain inexpensive and widely available, costing **1 credit each**.

See [[Arrows]] for rules governing bow ammunition and available specialized arrows.

**Models Include:** Corellian Compound Bow and Field Sports FS19 Compound Bow.
