A balance between concealability and stopping power, Light Blaster Pistols are favored by those who frequently need a firearm but prefer not to draw attention to it. Smugglers, spies, and rogues who operate in plain sight often rely on these weapons, as do smaller-framed species for whom heavier sidearms are less practical.

While not as easily hidden as Holdout Pistols, light blasters offer improved range and stopping power, making them a reliable compromise for both self-defense and covert operations.

**Models Include**: Merr-Sonn Model 44, BlasTech DL-18, DDC Defender Sporter.
