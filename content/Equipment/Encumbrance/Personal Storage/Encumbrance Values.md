All equipment has an [[Encumbrance]] value representing its weight, bulk, and general difficulty of transport. [[Encumbrance]] is an abstract measure rather than a precise representation of an item's mass.

Most commonly carried items have an [[Encumbrance]] value between 0 and 5, ranging from small personal effects to heavy weapons and equipment.

[[Armor]] uses [[Encumbrance]] differently depending on whether it is worn or carried. [[Armor|Armor's Encumbrance]] is reduced while worn but applies in full while being carried.

## Encumbrance 0

Items with 0 [[Encumbrance]] are sufficiently small or lightweight that they do not meaningfully contribute to a character's carrying burden.

The GM may assign an [[Encumbrance]] value to an item when its quantity, bulk, or method of transport makes its normal value inappropriate.
