Vehicles can transport large quantities of equipment, supplies, and other cargo. Each vehicle has an Encumbrance Capacity listed in its profile, representing the amount of cargo its dedicated storage spaces can accommodate.

Cargo stored within a vehicle's dedicated storage does not count toward the [[Encumbrance]] carried by individual characters.

## Cargo Capacity

A vehicle's Encumbrance Capacity can be used to transport equipment, supplies, trade goods, and other cargo with an [[Encumbrance]] value.

The GM may determine that unusually large, awkward, or specialized cargo requires additional space or cannot be stored in the vehicle's normal cargo capacity.

Cargo may be organized within crates, containers, or other suitable storage. See [[Storage]] and [[Packing]] for rules governing how cargo is organized and stored.

## Overloading

A vehicle may exceed its normal Encumbrance Capacity by tightly packing, stacking, and securing cargo into otherwise unused space.

See [[Overloaded]] for the effects of carrying cargo beyond the vehicle's normal capacity.

## Carried Vehicles

Vehicles may themselves be transported as cargo aboard another vehicle, vessel, or other suitable means of transportation.

See [[Carried Vehicles]] for the [[Encumbrance]] required to transport a vehicle and the conditions that apply while it is being carried.
