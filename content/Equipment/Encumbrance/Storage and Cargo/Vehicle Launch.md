Launching a stored or docked vehicle requires time to power up its systems and safely deploy.

A vehicle requires **1 round per Silhouette** to launch.

If the vehicle is stored under **Cramped** conditions, double the required launch time.

A vehicle deployed from a [[Vehicle Docks|Hangar Bay]] reduces its launch time by **2 rounds**, to a minimum of 1 round.

## Keep it Running

A vehicle may be left powered on or connected to an external power source, such as a starport supply. If so, reduce its required launch time by **2 rounds**, to a minimum of 1 round.

If this reduces the launch time below 1 round, launching the vehicle instead requires only a **Maneuver**.

Vehicles of Silhouette 6 or larger are considered powered up by default, as their systems are rarely fully shut down. These vehicles do not benefit from this reduction.

If a Silhouette 6 or larger vehicle is completely powered down, performing a cold start requires **1 hour per Silhouette**.

## "Get Us Out of Here!"

A character may attempt to expedite launch procedures with an **Easy Piloting check**.

For vehicles of Silhouette 5 or larger, a character on the bridge may instead use **Leadership**.

Each uncancelled \[success] reduces the remaining launch time by 1 round. This check may be attempted once per round until the vehicle launches.

Another character at an appropriate station may assist using Piloting or Mechanics.

If the vehicle is stored under **Cramped** conditions, upgrade the difficulty of this check once.
