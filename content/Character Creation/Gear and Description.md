At this stage, most of the mechanical details of the character have been determined. Now each player has the opportunity to define the details that make their character unique, including their equipment, appearance, and personality.

These details help establish who the character is, where they came from, and why they were chosen to join the Republic Expedition to Mycorris.

## STARTING GEAR

Player Characters begin the expedition with a budget of 500 credits to purchase starting equipment. This represents the personal gear, tools, weapons, and supplies they brought with them before departing for Mycorris.

Players may spend these credits to purchase items from Chapter V. Characters may not purchase Restricted (R) items unless specifically approved by the GM.

Characters keep any unspent credits when they begin the game. After purchasing starting equipment, roll 1d100. The character gains this many additional credits as pocket money. This additional money represents personal savings and may not be spent during character creation.

Characters begin the game only with items purchased using starting credits or reasonable

## PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

The galaxy is home to countless species, each with their own unique appearances and cultures. When determining their character's appearance, players should begin by reviewing the information provided for their chosen species.

Players should consider how their character's appearance reflects their history, experiences, and reason for joining the expedition.

Some details to consider include:

- Height, Weight, and Build: A character's physical build is generally determined by their species, but individuals can vary greatly. Consider whether their appearance reflects their characteristics, profession, lifestyle, or past experiences.
- Hair and Eye Color: Many species have a wide variety of natural or artificial appearances. Consider how these features may distinguish the character from others of their species.
- Skin, Scale, or Fur Color: Species often have common coloration, but individuals may vary. Unique coloration, markings, or modifications can help define a character.
- Scars, Tattoos, and Identifying Marks: Scars, tattoos, cybernetics, clothing, and other personal details can reveal a character's history. Consider how these features were acquired and what stories they may tell.

## PERSONALITY

A character's personality defines how they interact with the galaxy and the people around them. Players should consider their character's traits, habits, beliefs, fears, and personal quirks.

A character's Motivation and Obligation are useful tools for developing personality. Motivation represents what drives a character forward, while Obligation represents the problems, debts, and responsibilities they must deal with.

Consider how these forces shape your character. A character may be optimistic despite overwhelming debt, a hardened survivor searching for redemption, or an explorer willing to risk everything for discovery.

Players are encouraged to allow their characters to grow throughout the campaign. The challenges of exploring Mycorris will test their beliefs, create new relationships, and may change who they become.
