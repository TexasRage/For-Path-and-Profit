A character with a Relationship Motivation is driven by their connection to a specific person, creature, group, or place. The relationship may be loving, respectful, competitive, antagonistic, or one-sided, but the character is willing to set aside their own needs to protect or support its subject.

### Specific Relationships

| d100   | Primary Relation                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1-10   | Place of Origin: The character is deeply proud of where they grew up, whether it was a planet, town, space station, or ship. They seek to improve and protect their home and its people.                     |
| 11-20  | Pet: The character shares a close bond with a pet or non-combatant animal companion.                                                                                                                         |
| 21-30  | Childhood Friend: The character maintains a close friendship with someone from their childhood. Despite the distance between them, they want the best for their companion.                                   |
| 31-40  | Comrades: The character is deeply loyal to those they serve alongside. These may be current companions, former military comrades, business associates, or others who shared important experiences with them. |
| 41-50  | Sibling: The character maintains a close relationship with one or more siblings. Their siblings may live far away and are unlikely to be combatants.                                                         |
| 51-60  | Mentor: The character is particularly close to a mentor, teacher, professor, or other figure who provided guidance and knowledge during their formative years.                                               |
| 61-70  | Parents: The character maintains close ties with one or both parents and seeks their approval. This relationship may be supportive or complicated.                                                           |
| 71-80  | Family or Clan: The character has strong ties to an extended family, clan, or tribe. They value their family's support and seek to maintain their place within it.                                           |
| 81-90  | Droid Companion: The character shares a close bond with a particular droid. The droid may be a family servant, astromech, protocol droid, or trusted companion.                                              |
| 91-100 | Former Nemesis: The character has formed a close bond with a former rival or enemy. Although their differences have been resolved, their relationship may remain competitive.                                |
