Modders specialize in improving existing technology. Rather than simply repairing equipment, they look for ways to make it faster, stronger, more efficient, or better suited to a particular purpose. Their work often involves unconventional modifications, custom components, and solutions that manufacturers never intended.

A Modder's additional Career skills are **Computers, Mechanics, Perception, and Piloting (Planetary)**. If Modder is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. Modders excel at customizing equipment and vehicles, making them particularly valuable to groups that rely on specialized or heavily modified gear.
