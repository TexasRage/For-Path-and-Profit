Droid Techs specialize in the construction, maintenance, repair, and programming of droids. They understand that droids are more than collections of components and circuitry, and many develop strong relationships with the machines they work on. A Droid Tech may maintain a fleet of labor droids, repair a trusted companion, or design specialized systems for unusual tasks.

A Droid Tech's additional Career skills are **Computers, Discipline, Knowledge (Education), and Mechanics**. If Droid Tech is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills allow Droid Techs to repair and program droids, understand their construction, and manage the behavioral problems that can arise when working with sophisticated artificial beings.
