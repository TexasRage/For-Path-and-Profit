Demolitionists are experts in explosives, incendiaries, and sabotage. They understand the principles of combustion, explosions, and structural failure, using that knowledge to create traps, destroy targets, and solve problems through inventive applications of controlled destruction. Although their work is dangerous, experienced Demolitionists know how to remain calm under pressure and avoid being caught in their own handiwork.

A Demolitionist's additional Career skills are **Computers, Cool, Mechanics, and Skulduggery**. If Demolitionist is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills make the Demolitionist more technically capable than many Hired Guns, allowing them to specialize in explosives and sabotage while also handling traps, security systems, machinery, and other challenges that require a more subtle approach.
