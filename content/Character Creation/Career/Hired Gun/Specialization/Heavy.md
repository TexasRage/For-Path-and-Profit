Heavies believe that the simplest solution is often the biggest weapon. Physically imposing and accustomed to carrying heavy firepower, they serve as walking arsenals on the battlefield, relying on overwhelming firepower and durability rather than stealth or subtlety. A Heavy is often direct and straightforward, with the strength and resilience to carry weapons and armor that would be impractical for others. In a group, a Heavy fills the role of a mobile weapons platform, capable of dealing tremendous damage while absorbing punishment.

A Heavy's additional Career skills are **Gunnery, Perception, Ranged (Heavy), and Resilience**. If Heavy is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills make the Heavy particularly effective with heavy weapons, allowing them to operate large weapon systems, spot threats, withstand the physical demands of their equipment, and bring overwhelming firepower to the fight.
