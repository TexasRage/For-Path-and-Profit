Marauders prefer to settle problems up close, relying on melee weapons, raw strength, and sheer toughness rather than keeping their distance. They are formidable combatants who can both deal and withstand tremendous punishment, making them ideal for breaking through enemy lines, intimidating opponents, or serving as the group's close-quarters specialist. Some come from cultures that favor melee combat, low-technology worlds where advanced weapons were unavailable, or traditions where fighting at close range is considered more honorable.

A Marauder's additional Career skills are **Coercion, Melee, Resilience, and Survival**. If Marauder is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills make Marauders particularly effective in close combat while allowing them to endure harsh conditions, intimidate others, and survive in dangerous environments.
