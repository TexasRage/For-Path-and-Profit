| d100   | Specific Conflicts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-10   | **Mayhem**: The character thrives on chaos and destruction. Conflict is exciting, and they rarely pass up an opportunity to make a situation louder, messier, or more dangerous—even when doing so puts themselves at risk.                                     |
| 11-20  | **Conquest**: The character is driven to overcome anyone or anything standing in their way. They believe strength earns respect, and when others refuse to yield, they are more than willing to use force.                                                      |
| 21-30  | **Squadmates**: The character values the bonds formed through shared danger. They may distrust those who have never faced combat alongside them, but those who have earned their loyalty can count on them without hesitation.                                  |
| 31-40  | **Blood Money**: Violence is simply the character's profession. They fight because it is what they know and what people are willing to pay them to do. To them, a firefight is no different from any other day's work.                                          |
| 41-50  | **Honor in Battle**: The character follows a personal code when it comes to combat. Whether based on tradition, training, or personal belief, they refuse to abandon their principles simply because doing so would be easier.                                  |
| 51-60  | **Best in the Field**: The character wants to be the best combatant they can possibly become. Every fight is a test, every opponent a challenge, and every victory another step toward proving they are the one who can be counted on when the shooting starts. |
| 61-70  | **Revenge**: The character fights to avenge a wrong that was done to them or someone they care about. Their desire for vengeance may be justified or misguided, but it gives their life a purpose that they are unwilling to abandon.                           |
| 71-80  | **Out of the Game**: The character does not intend to fight forever. They are earning enough credits to retire, pay off their debts, secure a future, or accomplish one final goal before leaving the life of violence behind.                                  |
| 81-90  | **Adrenaline Junky**: The character lives for the thrill of danger. They fight for the rush of facing impossible odds, surviving by instinct, and walking away when everyone else thought they were finished.                                                   |
| 91-100 | **Life Debt**: The character owes someone a debt that cannot be repaid with credits alone. Whether they were saved, protected, or given a second chance, they are willing to fight for the person who gave them that opportunity.                               |
