| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01-08  | **Bounty**: The Hired Gun failed to eliminate or capture a target, and that failure continues to haunt them. Whether the target remains a threat or the character simply refuses to leave the job unfinished, they are determined to settle the score.                                               |
| 09-16  | **Dutybound**: Someone once saved the Hired Gun's life or rescued them from a failed contract. They now feel honor-bound to repay that debt, often by risking their own life to aid another.                                                                                                         |
| 17-24  | **Responsibility**: The Hired Gun feels responsible for someone incapable of surviving without their help. Whether it's an inexperienced hunter, former partner, or old comrade, they have a habit of needing rescue at the worst possible time.                                                     |
| 25-32  | **Favor**: A mentor, sponsor, or influential contact helped launch the Hired Gun's career. Now that debt is coming due, and the favors requested are becoming increasingly difficult—or morally questionable.                                                                                        |
| 33-40  | **Family**: No matter how far they've run from their past, the Hired Gun still has family they care about. Enemies, creditors, or rivals may exploit those ties to get to them.                                                                                                                      |
| 41-48  | **Adrenaline Rush**: The Hired Gun is addicted to the thrill of combat and constantly seeks danger. If they go an entire session without participating in a fight, the GM may add \[setback] to skill checks during the next session, or until the character has the opportunity to engage in combat. |
| 49-56  | **Debt**: The Hired Gun borrowed heavily to maintain their weapons, armor, or equipment. Unable to repay in credits, they are often forced to perform dangerous jobs or favors for their creditor.                                                                                                   |
| 57-64  | **Collateral Accountability**: A past contract caused unintended damage or claimed an innocent life, leaving the Hired Gun indebted to a powerful individual. If this Obligation is triggered, the GM may add \[setback] to affected skill checks as guilt and anxiety weigh on the character.        |
| 65-72  | **A Score to Settle**: Someone wronged the Hired Gun, and they refuse to let it go. Whether the slight was real or imagined, they are always looking for the chance to even the score.                                                                                                               |
| 73-80  | **Betrayal**: The Hired Gun abandoned the ideals or people who trained them, earning the scorn of their former allies. Attempts to bring them back—or eliminate them—continue to complicate their life.                                                                                              |
| 81-88  | **Oath**: The Hired Gun has sworn to complete a dangerous mission or uphold a strict personal code. Breaking that oath would betray everything they stand for.                                                                                                                                       |
| 89-96  | **The Price of a Name**: The Hired Gun falsely claimed another's accomplishments or identity for personal gain. Now the truth is catching up, threatening their reputation, livelihood, or even their life.                                                                                          |
