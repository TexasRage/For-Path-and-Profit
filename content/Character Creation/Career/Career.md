At this stage, the player chooses their character's Career and starting Specialization. A Career represents the character's core profession, way of life, and the experiences that shaped them, while a Specialization represents a more focused area of training and expertise. A character's Career remains with them throughout their story, while they may develop additional Specializations as they gain experience.

When choosing a Career, players should consider both the role they want to fill within the Mycorris Expedition and the story they want to tell. A Career can help establish a character's history, skills, goals, and place within the expedition. Players should consider what brought their character into this line of work, what they did before joining the expedition, and what they hope to accomplish on Mycorris.

Careers also provide Background, Obligation, and Motivation options tailored to characters in that profession. After selecting a Career, players should use the options provided in that Career's section to further develop their character.

## Choosing a Specialization

A Specialization represents the character's current area of focus within their Career. It may reflect formal education, practical experience, natural talent, or a particular role the character has undertaken.

When choosing a Specialization, consider what the character typically does as part of their profession. What skills have they developed? Who taught them? What kind of work do they perform? How does their Specialization contribute to the expedition?

A character begins with one Specialization but may purchase additional Specializations as they gain experience. This allows characters to expand their expertise and develop in new directions throughout the campaign.
