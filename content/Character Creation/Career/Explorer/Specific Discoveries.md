| d100   | Specific Discoveries                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-10   | **Self**: The Explorer may pursue many goals, but their greatest journey is one of self-discovery. Each new world, challenge, and experience offers another chance to learn who they truly are and what they are capable of.                                                                        |
| 11-20  | **Civilizations**: The galaxy is home to countless cultures and societies that remain unknown to the wider galaxy. The Explorer seeks out these communities to learn their histories, understand their customs, and make first contact with those who have remained hidden from the greater galaxy. |
| 21-30  | **Planets**: The Explorer seeks worlds that have never been properly mapped or understood. They may be searching for habitable planets, valuable locations, or simply places that no one else has visited.                                                                                          |
| 31-40  | **Treasures**: Some discoveries are valuable simply because they are valuable. The Explorer seeks lost fortunes, ancient artifacts, forgotten caches, and other treasures hidden across the galaxy, whether for the wealth, the glory, or the thrill of finding them.                               |
| 41-50  | **Secrets**: The Explorer seeks information that others have deliberately hidden. Such secrets may reveal lost histories, powerful organizations, forgotten events, or truths that someone would rather remain buried.                                                                              |
| 51-60  | **Knowledge**: Lost and forgotten knowledge can be as valuable as any treasure. The Explorer searches abandoned settlements, ancient ruins, derelict ships, and forgotten archives for information that has been lost to time.                                                                      |
| 61-70  | **Resources**: The Explorer seeks valuable sources of raw materials, rare substances, or other resources that could transform a settlement, business, or entire region. Finding such a resource can bring wealth, influence, and lasting recognition.                                               |
| 71-80  | **Technology**: The Explorer searches for lost, experimental, or previously unknown technology. They may seek ancient designs, forgotten engineering techniques, or discoveries that could change what the galaxy believes is possible.                                                             |
| 81-90  | **The Lost**: The Explorer seeks those who have disappeared—lost travelers, isolated communities, missing expeditions, or individuals who vanished without explanation. Some are waiting to be found, while others may have chosen to disappear.                                                    |
| 91-100 | Anywhere but Here!: The Explorer is driven by the journey itself. They are never satisfied with what they have already seen and are always searching for the next horizon, the next unexplored world, and the next adventure.                                                                       |
