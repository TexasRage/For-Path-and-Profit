Traders travel the galaxy looking for goods, markets, and opportunities. They understand that information can be just as valuable as cargo, and that knowing who to deal with can be more important than knowing what to sell. Traders add **Charm, Deception, Negotiation, and Streetwise** to their Career Skills. If this is the character’s starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain one free rank in each without spending starting experience.

A Trader may join the expedition hoping to establish new trade opportunities, secure valuable resources, or become the first to open commercial routes to the new world. Others may simply see the expedition as the greatest business opportunity they have ever encountered, with an entire world of untapped markets waiting to be discovered.
