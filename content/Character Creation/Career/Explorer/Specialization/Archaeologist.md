Archaeologists explore the remains of ancient civilizations, searching for artifacts and knowledge that can reveal the galaxy’s forgotten history. Their work often leads them into dangerous ruins and abandoned worlds where the past has remained untouched for centuries. Archaeologists add **Discipline, Knowledge (Education), Knowledge (Lore), and Skulduggery** to their Career Skills. If this is the character’s starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain one free rank in each without spending starting experience.

An Archaeologist may join the expedition because Mycorris offers the chance to uncover something that has never been studied. They may seek evidence of ancient inhabitants, forgotten technology, or clues to mysteries that have endured for generations. For an Archaeologist, every ruin is a story waiting to be uncovered.
