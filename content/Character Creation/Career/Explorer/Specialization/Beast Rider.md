Beast Riders specialize in working with the creatures of the galaxy’s wild places. They train, ride, and care for animals while learning how to survive alongside dangerous wildlife. Beast Riders add **Athletics, Knowledge (Xenology), Survival, and Vigilance** to their Career Skills. If this is the character’s starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain one free rank in each without spending starting experience.

A Beast Rider may join the expedition because unexplored worlds are home to creatures no one has encountered before. They may hope to study or work with these animals, seek out rare species, or simply prove they can master whatever the new world throws at them. On a frontier world, a skilled Beast Rider can turn dangerous wildlife from a threat into an invaluable resource.
