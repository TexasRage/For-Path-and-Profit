Saboteurs specialize in bypassing security, infiltrating facilities, and disabling equipment. Although their talents are often associated with criminal activity, those same skills can prove invaluable to an Explorer dealing with hostile environments, abandoned facilities, or unfamiliar technology. Saboteurs add **Mechanics, Skulduggery, Stealth, and Vigilance** to their Career Skills. If this is the character’s starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain one free rank in each without spending starting experience.

A Saboteur may join the expedition because their skills are useful far from civilization, where locked doors, damaged machinery, and unfamiliar technology can become serious obstacles. Others may simply see the expedition as a chance to put their talents to work somewhere they are less likely to attract unwanted attention. On an unexplored world, knowing how to get past what stands in your way can be just as valuable as knowing what lies beyond it.
