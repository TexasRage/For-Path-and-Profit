Thieves are covert operators who specialize in getting into places they should not be, taking things that do not belong to them, and leaving without being noticed. They rely on stealth, observation, and technical skill rather than brute force, making them adept at bypassing locks and security, concealing themselves, spotting hidden threats, and catching opponents off guard. Some work alone, while others operate as part of criminal crews or take specialized jobs for powerful clients seeking solutions outside the law.

A Thief's additional Career skills are **Computers, Skulduggery, Stealth, and Vigilance**. If Thief is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills make the Thief particularly effective at infiltration, security, concealment, and reconnaissance, allowing them to handle obstacles that would otherwise require force or attract unwanted attention.
