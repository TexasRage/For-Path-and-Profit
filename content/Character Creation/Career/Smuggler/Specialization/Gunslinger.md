Gunslingers are masters of the blaster, relying on speed, precision, and composure to survive the dangers of the frontier. They are quick on the draw and rarely hesitate when violence begins, often developing a strong bond with a favored weapon and a reputation that can attract both work and trouble. Gunslingers may work as hired protection, bounty hunters, smugglers, or freelance guns for criminal organizations, using their skills and connections to find employment wherever they travel.

A Gunslinger's additional Career skills are **Coercion, Cool, Knowledge (Outer Rim), and Ranged (Light)**. If Gunslinger is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. These skills reinforce the Gunslinger's ability to survive on the frontier, remain calm under pressure, intimidate opponents, and handle the blasters that define their profession.
