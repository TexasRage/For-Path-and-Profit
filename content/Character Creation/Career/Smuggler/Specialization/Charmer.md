Charmers rely on confidence, wit, and persuasion rather than intimidation or violence. They are skilled at getting what they want simply by convincing others to give it to them, using flattery, negotiation, deception, and an effortless social presence. A Charmer can talk their way into restricted places, smooth over tense situations, negotiate favorable deals, and take charge when necessary. Although they prefer words to violence, their experience as a Smuggler still leaves them capable of handling themselves when diplomacy fails.

A Charmer's additional Career skills are **Charm, Cool, Leadership, and Negotiation**. If Charmer is this character's starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. Their combination of social expertise and Smuggler skills makes them valuable in negotiations, underworld dealings, and situations where a clever word can accomplish what a blaster cannot.
