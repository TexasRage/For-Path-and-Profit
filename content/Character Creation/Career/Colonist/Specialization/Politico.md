Politicos understand how to influence people and turn ideas into action. They may be politicians, diplomats, community organizers, administrators, or simply charismatic individuals who have learned how to navigate competing interests and persuade others to follow their lead. On the frontier, where institutions are still taking shape, a skilled Politico can have an outsized influence on the community.

A Politico adds **Charm, Coercion, Deception, and Knowledge (Core Worlds)** to their Career Skills. If Politico is their starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain one free rank in each, without spending starting experience. A Politico may join the expedition to represent a government or organization, pursue political ambitions, escape the failures of an old administration, or build a new community according to their ideals. In a settlement without established institutions, a capable Politico can quickly become a leader.
