Performers understand the power of art, entertainment, and personality. They may be musicians, actors, dancers, storytellers, or other artists who use their talents to entertain, inspire, persuade, or preserve culture. In a new settlement, a Performer can help maintain morale, strengthen community, and preserve traditions far from home.

A Performer adds **Charm, Deception, Leadership, and Streetwise** to their Career Skills. If Performer is their starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain one free rank in each, without spending starting experience. A Performer may join the expedition seeking a new audience, hoping to escape a stagnant career, or believing that art and culture are essential to building a new community. On a distant world, even a simple song or performance can provide a much-needed reminder of home.
