Marshals bring law and order to places where formal institutions are weak or nonexistent. They may be lawkeepers, investigators, security officers, or community leaders who have learned to maintain order without the resources of a major government. On the frontier, a Marshal often has to rely on judgment, discipline, and personal authority to keep the peace.

A Marshal adds **Cool, Discipline, Knowledge (Underworld), and Vigilance** to their Career Skills. If Marshal is their starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain one free rank in each, without spending starting experience. A Marshal may join the expedition because they believe the new settlement will need someone willing to enforce its laws and protect its people. Others may be seeking a fresh start away from corrupt institutions or restrictive governments. Whatever their reason, Marshals are often among the first to confront the dangers that threaten a growing community.
