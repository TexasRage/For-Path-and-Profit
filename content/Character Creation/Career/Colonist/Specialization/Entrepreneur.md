Entrepreneurs see opportunities where others see obstacles. They are traders, investors, business owners, and ambitious individuals who believe almost anything can become a profitable venture with enough planning and determination. Their talents allow them to identify opportunities, negotiate favorable arrangements, and turn limited resources into something valuable.

An Entrepreneur adds **Deception, Knowledge (Underworld), Negotiation, and Streetwise** to their Career Skills. If Entrepreneur is their starting Specialization, they may choose **two** of these skills and gain **one free rank in each**, without spending starting experience. An Entrepreneur may join the expedition seeking wealth, new markets, or the opportunity to establish a business from the ground up. Others believe prosperity and development can improve the lives of everyone around them. Whatever their motivation, Entrepreneurs are always looking for the next opportunity.
