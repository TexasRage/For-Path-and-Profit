Scholars devote themselves to understanding the galaxy. They may be historians, scientists, researchers, educators, archaeologists, or experts in subjects that have little practical value until someone encounters the right problem. Their knowledge can become invaluable when an expedition encounters unfamiliar cultures, ancient ruins, strange lifeforms, or mysteries that have no easy explanation.

A Scholar adds **Discipline, Knowledge (Xenology), Knowledge (Education), and Knowledge (Lore)** to their Career Skills. If Scholar is their starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain one free rank in each, without spending starting experience. A Scholar may join the expedition to study an unexplored world, uncover lost knowledge, educate future settlers, or make discoveries that could benefit the galaxy. To a Scholar, the unknown is not merely a danger—it is an opportunity to learn something no one has learned before.
