The Bounty Hunter's eight Career Skills are **Athletics, Brawl, Perception, Piloting (Planetary), Piloting (Space), Ranged (Heavy), Streetwise, and Vigilance**.

At character creation, the Bounty Hunter automatically gains one rank in four of these skills of their choice without spending experience.

A Bounty Hunter is a skilled tracker, investigator, and combat specialist. While many are known for pursuing fugitives and collecting rewards, others use their abilities as scouts, security specialists, and problem solvers on the frontier.

Within the Mycorris Expedition, Bounty Hunters may serve as wilderness trackers, security contractors, hunters of dangerous threats, or specialists hired to locate individuals, creatures, or valuable resources. Their experience with hostile environments, pursuit, and combat makes them valuable members of an expedition where danger can come from both the unknown wilderness and those seeking to exploit it.

## BOUNTY HUNTER BACKGROUNDS

Characters become Bounty Hunters for many reasons. Some pursue criminals out of a sense of justice, while others seek revenge, adventure, or simply a reliable way to make a living. Their work may be guided by personal principles, professional ambition, or the pursuit of a particular target.

For a character joining the expedition, Bounty Hunting may be their established profession or a new opportunity. They might have been hired to provide security, accepted a contract connected to the expedition, or joined in search of a target on Mycorris. The following backgrounds provide starting points for developing a Bounty Hunter's history and can be combined with the character's Motivation and Obligation to create a more complete concept.

### IN IT FOR THE MONEY

For some Bounty Hunters, the job is ultimately about one thing: credits. They may have entered the profession because they needed work, discovered they enjoyed it, or realized that few occupations offered the same combination of freedom and earning potential.

A Bounty Hunter driven by profit may take almost any contract if the price is right. They may seek wealth and status, invest in better equipment, or simply use their earnings to maintain the lifestyle their profession demands. Whatever their goal, credits are the measure by which they judge success.

### LAWBRINGER

Some Bounty Hunters see themselves as more than hired guns. They believe in the rule of law, the pursuit of justice, or protecting the innocent from those who prey upon them. To these hunters, a bounty is an opportunity to bring someone to justice when conventional authorities cannot or will not.

Their interpretation of justice varies. Some work alongside legitimate authorities, while others pursue fugitives, corrupt officials, or anyone they believe has escaped justice. Whether strict law enforcers, personal avengers, or idealists, these Bounty Hunters believe someone must stand against those who abuse others or break the rules.

### THRILL OF THE HUNT

For some Bounty Hunters, the bounty is secondary to the pursuit. Tracking a target, overcoming their defenses, and finally closing the distance provides a thrill that credits alone cannot match. Every quarry is a new challenge and an opportunity to test the hunter's skill and determination.

These hunters may enjoy stalking prey, chasing targets across dangerous terrain, or overcoming particularly capable or elusive quarry. Some deliberately pursue difficult targets or prolong a chase rather than take the easiest path to capture. For them, the hunt itself is the real reward.

## BOUNTY HUNTER OBLIGATIONS

In the Outer Rim, Bounty Hunters are feared, employed, and resented in equal measure. Local authorities hire them when conventional methods fail, corporations and criminal organizations use them when convenient, and individuals turn to them when they have nowhere else to go. They are professional trackers and extractors who pursue fugitives, recover assets, enforce contracts, and deliver targets to whoever can pay. Some are former soldiers or security officers, while others are former criminals or survivors who discovered that hunting pays better than being hunted. They operate without a universal chain of command or guaranteed protection, relying on reputation and skill to survive.

Every contract can leave behind enemies, debts, rivalries, or obligations to employers and clients. A successful hunter may gain valuable contacts and a strong reputation, but the same reputation can attract unwanted attention. A Bounty Hunter's Obligation represents the debts, loyalties, vendettas, and other entanglements they carry between jobs, providing the Game Master with opportunities to connect the character's past to the campaign. Players may use [[Bounty Hunter Obligations]] table instead of the [[Core Obligations]] table, choosing or rolling an Obligation that fits their character's history. They may also take additional Obligation at character creation for extra experience or credits.

## BOUNTY HUNTER SPECIALIZATIONS

Bounty Hunters may approach their profession in many different ways. Some rely on stealth and careful investigation, while others specialize in direct confrontation, specialized weapons, or the pursuit of particular types of targets. Each specialization represents a different set of skills and methods for tracking, capturing, and overcoming dangerous quarry.

The following specializations are available to Bounty Hunters. Each offers a distinct approach to the hunt, allowing characters to develop their own methods and reputation while pursuing targets across the galaxy.

## BOUNTY HUNTER MOTIVATIONS

Bounty Hunters are often guided by a personal code that determines which jobs they accept, how they pursue their targets, and what lines they refuse to cross. Their Motivation may come from a strict sense of justice, professional pride, personal honor, or rules developed through years of working in a dangerous profession. A Bounty Hunter's Motivation helps define what they stand for and how they behave when a contract conflicts with their principles.

Bounty Hunters may roll on the [[Types of Motivation]] table or choose a Motivation that fits their character. The career-specific result is Codes. If a character has Codes as their Motivation, they may then choose or roll for a specific Code on the [[Specific Codes]] table. These options represent the different principles that can guide a Bounty Hunter, from upholding the law and never abandoning a contract to pursuing the biggest score, protecting innocents, or maintaining a personal standard of honor.
