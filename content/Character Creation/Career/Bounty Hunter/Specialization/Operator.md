Operators specialize in vehicles and starships, pursuing quarry wherever they flee. They are aggressive pilots capable of handling difficult terrain, high-speed pursuits, and heavy weapons when a chase inevitably becomes a firefight. An Operator adds **Astrogation, Gunnery, Piloting (Planetary), and Piloting (Space)** to their Career Skills. If Operator is their starting Specialization, they may choose two of these skills and gain 1 free rank in each. Operators excel when a hunt requires a vehicle or starship, using their piloting skills to pursue targets, maneuver through difficult environments, and turn their vehicles into weapons.
