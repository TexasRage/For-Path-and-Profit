Several of a character's attributes are derived during character creation rather than purchased directly. These values are calculated using the character's species, characteristics, equipment, and talents, and determine how resilient they are in combat and other dangerous situations.

## WOUND THRESHOLD

A character's Wound Threshold represents how much physical damage they can withstand before becoming incapacitated. It is determined by adding their species' Wound Threshold to their Brawn rating (after spending starting experience).

Once determined during character creation, later increases to Brawn do not increase Wound Threshold. It can only be increased through talents or other game effects.

## STRAIN THRESHOLD

A character's Strain Threshold represents how much mental or physical exertion they can endure before becoming incapacitated. It is determined by adding their species' Strain Threshold to their Willpower rating (after spending starting experience).

Once determined during character creation, later increases to Willpower do not increase Strain Threshold. It can only be increased through talents or other game effects.

## DEFENSE

Defense represents how difficult a character is to hit. It is divided into Melee Defense and Ranged Defense, both of which begin at 0.

Defense is most commonly gained from armor, cover, talents, and other abilities.

## SOAK

Soak reduces incoming damage before wounds are applied. A character's base Soak is equal to their Brawn rating, and unlike Wound Threshold, it increases whenever their Brawn increases.

Additional Soak is commonly gained from armor, talents, and other abilities.
