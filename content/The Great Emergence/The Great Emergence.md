The galaxy stands in fragile recovery. The Great Hyperspace Disaster—the destruction of the Legacy Run—has left vast stretches of the Outer Rim destabilized and uncertain. Hyperspace lanes remain unreliable, scattered debris continues to threaten travel, and confidence in frontier expansion has faltered.

Chancellor Lina Soh and the Galactic Republic seek to restore faith in exploration through renewed outreach and carefully sanctioned expeditions. Even as construction of Starlight Beacon progresses, the frontier remains volatile—riddled with unexplained phenomena and regions only partially charted since the Disaster.

Amid this uncertainty, long-range probes have detected something unprecedented: a planetary body appearing intermittently along unstable hyperspace vectors. Whether this world is newly charted, displaced, or something stranger remains unknown. What is certain is that it warrants investigation.

## Using This Book

_For Path and Profit_ introduces a structured exploration and base-building experience for the Star Wars RPG/Genesys system. It combines classic hexcrawl elements with long-term settlement development, resource management, and planetary exploration.

This book provides the rules and tools needed to navigate the mysterious world of [[Mycorris]], establish a permanent presence, and shape the future of a newly discovered frontier world.

## What's Different

The following changes and expanded systems are used throughout this campaign:

- Modified [[Talents]] to better fit the setting.
- Expanded [[Equipment System]], consolidating equipment into broad archetypes while reworking Hard Points to give players greater freedom to customize their gear through attachments and modifications.
- [[Survival and Resource Management]], combining Daily Needs, consumables, homestead and vehicle maintenance, fuel cell management, and resource tracking.
- [[Hexcrawl Exploration]], introducing structured wilderness travel, navigation, environmental effects, and weather hazards.
- [[Quarter-Day Time System]], providing a consistent framework for exploration, survival, homestead activities, and other player actions.
- [[Recovery and Injury]], replacing full recovery from overnight rest with Short Rests and Long Rests, while introducing new Critical Injury mechanics and a separate Injury system.
- [[Homestead Systems]], providing additional rules for construction, development, maintenance, and long-term settlement management.

### Hexcrawling

The campaign shifts between traditional narrative play, range band encounters, and structured hexcrawl exploration depending on the situation.

Range bands remain the primary system for encounters and immediate action scenes, while hexcrawl rules govern overland travel, navigation, environmental hazards, and discovery. The game can transition freely between these modes as the focus changes from exploration to action.

## Playing For Path and Profit

The players begin as members of a sanctioned Republic expedition sent to investigate a newly discovered world on the edge of the galaxy. Their mission is to establish a foothold, investigate the planet's mysteries, and determine its value to the Republic.

After fulfilling the expedition's initial objectives, the players are granted significant autonomy over the planet's future. From there, their choices determine whether Mycorris becomes a center of exploration, commerce, diplomacy, or survival.

### For Path

Your expedition represents the Republic's first step into a newly discovered world believed to be connected to the mysterious [[Hyperspace Emergences]].

Your primary mission is to determine whether Mycorris is linked to these phenomena, and if so, to what extent. The Republic requires answers—are these anomalies natural, artificial, or something far more dangerous?

Alongside this central objective, the expedition is required to establish a [[Homestead]] as a base of operations on the planet. This includes creating a permanent surface presence capable of supporting continued research, exploration, and limited colonization efforts.

While the Republic provides the framework of the mission, the act of exploration itself is left to the players. The unknown world beyond the settlement is theirs to chart, and what they choose to investigate—or ignore—will shape both their understanding of Mycorris and their place within it.

### And Profit

Once the Republic's objectives are achieved, the expedition enters a new phase. The players gain control over the development of Mycorris and decide what role the planet will play in the galaxy.

They may expand the settlement, exploit valuable resources, build diplomatic relationships, uncover forgotten secrets, or simply fight to survive.

The world is theirs to shape—but every choice carries consequences.
